## ambient noise

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T12:26:54.829Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 14:26:54 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Ambient noise music, also known as ambient drone or ambient industrial, is a subgenre of ambient music that incorporates elements of noise, drone, and industrial music. It often features long, sustained sounds, textures, and drones that create a sonic atmosphere without traditional melody, harmony, or rhythm. This type of music can be very immersive and often uses a variety of unconventional instruments and sound sources to create its distinctive soundscapes. The use of effects processing, field recordings, and other forms of sound manipulation is also common in ambient noise music.
