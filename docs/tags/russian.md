## russian

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T05:01:38.432Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 07:01:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Russian rock and alternative music emerged from Soviet underground culture, blending Western rock influences with Slavic melodies and poetic, often philosophical lyrics. The genre emphasizes raw emotional expression, distorted guitars, and atmospheric soundscapes that reflect post-Soviet identity. It maintains a distinctly melancholic yet rebellious character rooted in Russian literary and cultural traditions.
