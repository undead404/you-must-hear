### Description

Serbian music encompasses a diverse sonic landscape blending Balkan folk traditions with contemporary rock, electronic, and alternative influences. The tag captures everything from brass band energy and turbo-folk&#39;s synth-driven pop to experimental post-punk and indie sounds rooted in the region&#39;s complex cultural identity. This music often features driving rhythms, minor-key melodies, and a raw, passionate energy reflecting Serbia&#39;s vibrant underground and mainstream scenes.
