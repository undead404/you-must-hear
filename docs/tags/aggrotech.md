## aggrotech

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T02:45:29.106Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 04:45:29 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Aggrotech is an aggressive electronic music genre that emerged from industrial and EBM scenes in the 1990s, characterized by distorted vocals, harsh synthesizers, and pounding danceable beats. The sound combines dark cyberpunk aesthetics with themes of violence, technology, and dystopia. It&#39;s popular in underground goth and industrial club scenes across Europe and North America.
