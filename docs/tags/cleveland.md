### Description

The Cleveland tag represents the diverse musical output from Ohio&#39;s gritty industrial city, spanning punk, industrial rock, hip-hop, and alternative scenes shaped by working-class roots and Rust Belt resilience. This sound often carries raw, unpolished energy and rebellious attitude, reflecting the city&#39;s underdog spirit and blue-collar ethos. Cleveland&#39;s musical identity blends aggression with innovation, producing both hard-edged rap and experimental rock that captures urban Midwestern authenticity.
