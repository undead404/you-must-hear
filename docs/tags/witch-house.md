## witch house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-10T13:07:02.164Z">Mon Nov 10 2025 15:07:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Witch house is a genre of electronic music that emerged in the late 2000s. It is characterized by its dark, occult-inspired atmosphere, use of lo-fi production techniques, chopped and screwed vocals, and influences from genres such as hip-hop, shoegaze, and industrial music. Witch house artists often incorporate elements of witchcraft, horror, and the supernatural into their music and aesthetics, with many using symbols and imagery associated with these themes in their album artwork and music videos. Some notable witch house artists include Salem, oOoOO, and Crystal Castles.
