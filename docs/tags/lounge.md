## lounge

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-03T17:25:07.541Z">Mon Nov 03 2025 19:25:07 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Lounge music is a genre of popular music that emerged in the 1950s and 1960s. It is characterized by its relaxed and laid-back vibe, and is often associated with the sophisticated and glamorous lifestyle of the mid-century jet set. Lounge music features a mix of different styles, including jazz, Latin, and exotica, and typically incorporates lush arrangements, smooth melodies, and rich harmonies. The genre experienced a revival in the 1990s, and has since continued to evolve, incorporating elements of electronic music and other genres.
