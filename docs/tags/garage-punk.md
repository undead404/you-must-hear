## garage punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T07:05:24.848Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 09:05:24 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Garage punk is a style of punk rock that takes inspiration from garage rock, a raw and primitive style of rock and roll that emerged in the 1960s. Garage punk is characterized by a lo-fi sound, simple and often aggressive instrumentation, and raw, often shouted vocals. The music is typically played with a stripped-down approach, emphasizing energy and attitude over technical proficiency. Lyrics often deal with themes of rebellion, social commentary, and personal struggles. Garage punk emerged in the late 1970s and has since become a popular subgenre of punk rock.
