## polish black metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:43:59.168Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:43:59 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Polish Black Metal is a subgenre of extreme metal music that originated in Poland in the early 1990s. It is characterized by fast tempos, raw production, aggressive lyrics, and often incorporates elements of death metal, thrash metal, and folk music. The lyrical themes often focus on Satanism, anti-Christianity, and paganism, making it a unique and intense listening experience.
