## didgeridoo

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T21:22:53.663Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 23:22:53 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Didgeridoo music refers to the music created with the didgeridoo, a wind instrument developed by Indigenous Australians over 1,500 years ago. The didgeridoo is traditionally made from a eucalyptus tree branch that has been hollowed out by termites. Its unique sound is produced by vibrating the lips while blowing into the instrument, and the player can create a range of sounds and rhythms by using circular breathing techniques and altering the shape of their mouth. Didgeridoo music is often associated with Indigenous Australian culture and is used in ceremonial contexts as well as contemporary music genres such as world music, ambient music, and electronic music.
