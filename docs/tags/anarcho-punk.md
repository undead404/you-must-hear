## anarcho-punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:31:03.412Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:31:03 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Anarcho-punk emerged in the late 1970s as a politically radical offshoot of punk rock, characterized by raw, aggressive sound and explicitly anti-authoritarian lyrics challenging capitalism, war, and social hierarchy. The music emphasizes DIY ethics, with bands often self-releasing records and organizing benefit shows for activist causes. Beyond mere musical style, it represents a complete rejection of mainstream culture and commerce in favor of direct action and collective liberation.
