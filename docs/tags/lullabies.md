## lullabies

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:45:51.118Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:45:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Lullabies are gentle, soothing songs designed to calm infants and young children into sleep, characterized by soft melodies, repetitive patterns, and slow tempos. Found across virtually every culture worldwide, they often feature simple lyrics, humming, or wordless vocalizations that create a peaceful, secure atmosphere. While traditionally sung by parents, modern lullabies span classical arrangements, music box instrumentals, and contemporary adaptations of familiar children&#39;s songs.
