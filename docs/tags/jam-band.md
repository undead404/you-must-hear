## jam band

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:40:04.549Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:40:04 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Jam bands are rock-oriented groups known for extended improvisational performances that transform songs into lengthy explorations. The genre emerged from 1960s psychedelic rock and emphasizes live concerts over studio recordings, fostering devoted fan communities. Their sound blends rock, jazz, blues, and folk with spontaneous musical interplay.
