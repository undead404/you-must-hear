## classical composer

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:00:11.640Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:00:11 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Classical composer music refers to music composed by composers who lived from the mid-16th century to the present day and who created music within the Western art music tradition. This encompasses a wide range of styles and genres, including Baroque, Classical, Romantic, and Modernist, among others. Some of the most famous classical composers include Johann Sebastian Bach, Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Ludwig van Beethoven, Franz Schubert, Frederic Chopin, Richard Wagner, and Igor Stravinsky, to name just a few. Classical composer music has had a profound influence on Western music and continues to be performed and studied today.
