## wales

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T06:36:17.597Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 08:36:17 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Music from Wales encompasses diverse sounds rooted in the country&#39;s rich Celtic heritage, from traditional harp melodies to contemporary rock and alternative music sung in both Welsh and English. The tag reflects Wales&#39;s strong cultural identity, where ancient bardic traditions merge with modern indie sensibilities and working-class punk energy. Welsh-language lyrics and folk influences often appear alongside broader British rock styles, creating a distinctive musical voice that honors tradition while embracing innovation.
