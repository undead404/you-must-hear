## israel

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T06:54:48.914Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 08:54:48 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Israeli music blends Middle Eastern and Mediterranean influences with Western pop, rock, and folk traditions, reflecting the country&#39;s diverse cultural heritage. The genre encompasses everything from traditional Mizrahi and Sephardic sounds to modern hip-hop and indie rock sung primarily in Hebrew. This eclectic style mirrors Israel&#39;s multicultural society, combining Jewish diaspora musical traditions with contemporary global genres.
