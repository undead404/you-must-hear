## ritual industrial

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T13:54:41.144Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 15:54:41 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Ritual industrial is a genre of music that blends elements of industrial music, ambient, and ritualistic music. It is characterized by repetitive rhythms, hypnotic and trance-like melodies, and the use of sounds and textures that evoke religious or mystical themes. Ritual industrial often features samples of chants, bells, and other ritualistic sounds, and may incorporate elements of shamanic or occult practices. The genre has roots in the industrial and post-industrial scenes of the 1980s and 1990s, but has since evolved and diversified. Some prominent artists in the genre include Coil, Current 93, and Nurse With Wound.
