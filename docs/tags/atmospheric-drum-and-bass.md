## atmospheric drum and bass

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-16T20:06:02.585Z">Sun Nov 16 2025 22:06:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Atmospheric drum and bass blends rapid breakbeats and deep basslines with ethereal, ambient textures to create an immersive sonic experience. Emerging from the broader drum and bass scene, it emphasizes mood and emotion over dancefloor energy. The genre often incorporates melancholic melodies, lush pads, and introspective themes that appeal to listeners seeking both intensity and contemplation.
