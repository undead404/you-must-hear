## tunisia

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T10:49:17.909Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 12:49:17 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Music from Tunisia blends North African, Arab, and Mediterranean influences, featuring traditional instruments like the oud and darbuka alongside contemporary production. The sound ranges from contemplative Sufi-influenced jazz to folk melodies and modern electronic fusions. It reflects Tunisia&#39;s cultural crossroads, merging ancient maqam scales with global musical traditions.
