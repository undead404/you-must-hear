## us indie

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T08:58:09.127Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 11:58:09 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

US indie encompasses American independent music that exists outside mainstream commercial structures, characterized by DIY ethics and creative freedom. The sound often blends atmospheric electronica, dream pop, and downtempo elements with introspective lyrics and lush production. It represents artists who prioritize artistic vision over commercial appeal, creating intimate and experimental sonic landscapes.
