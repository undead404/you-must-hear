## southern hip hop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T13:59:18.414Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 15:59:18 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Southern hip hop is a subgenre of hip hop music that emerged in the southern United States, primarily in Atlanta, New Orleans, Houston, and Memphis in the late 1980s and early 1990s. It is characterized by slower, heavier beats, a focus on storytelling, and often gritty or humorous lyrics that reflect the experiences of Southern African American communities. Some of the most well-known southern hip hop artists include Outkast, UGK, Three 6 Mafia, T.I., Lil Wayne, and Young Jeezy. The genre has had a major impact on the broader hip hop landscape, influencing the sound and style of many contemporary rap artists.
