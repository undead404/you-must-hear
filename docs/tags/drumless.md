## drumless

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:34:35.238Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:34:35 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Drumless is a minimalist hip-hop subgenre that strips away traditional drum patterns, relying instead on atmospheric samples, melodic loops, and sparse instrumentation. Emerging from underground rap in the 2010s, it creates an eerie, meditative sound that emphasizes lyricism and mood over conventional rhythm. The style reflects a DIY ethos and experimental approach to production.
