## nigeria

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T10:59:29.243Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 12:59:29 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Nigerian music refers to the musical styles and genres that originate from Nigeria, a country in West Africa that is known for its diverse cultural heritage. Nigerian music encompasses a wide range of styles, including traditional music, highlife, juju, fuji, Afrobeat, hip-hop, and more. It is known for its rich rhythms, energetic beats, and diverse vocal styles, and has had a significant impact on popular music around the world, particularly in African and Western countries. Some of the most popular Nigerian musicians include Fela Kuti, King Sunny Ade, Wizkid, Davido, and Burna Boy, among others.
