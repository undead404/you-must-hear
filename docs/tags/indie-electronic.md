## indie electronic

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-24T15:34:38.319Z">Mon Nov 24 2025 17:34:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Indie electronic music is a genre of music that combines elements of indie rock and electronic music. It often features electronic instruments, such as synthesizers and drum machines, but is characterized by a more lo-fi or DIY sound, as opposed to the more polished production values associated with mainstream electronic dance music. The genre emerged in the 2000s and has been associated with a number of different subgenres, including chillwave, synthpop, and indietronica. Some notable indie electronic artists include The Postal Service, LCD Soundsystem, Grimes, and Caribou.
