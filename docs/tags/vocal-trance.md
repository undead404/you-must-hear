## vocal trance

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:39:46.897Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:39:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Vocal trance is an electronic dance music genre that combines uplifting, melodic trance production with ethereal singing, often featuring female vocalists. Emerging in the late 1990s, it became a staple of European club culture and large-scale dance festivals. The genre is characterized by emotional buildups, soaring vocals, and euphoric drops that create an transcendent atmosphere on the dancefloor.
