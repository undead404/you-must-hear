## parody

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-25T09:25:36.890Z">Sat Oct 25 2025 12:25:36 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Parody music recreates recognizable songs or musical styles with altered lyrics or exaggerated performances, typically for comedic effect or social commentary. These works rely on listeners&#39; familiarity with the original material, transforming serious songs into humorous critiques or absurd reinterpretations that highlight the conventions of specific genres. The approach ranges from direct lyrical rewrites of pop hits to full stylistic send-ups that blend multiple genres into unexpected combinations.
