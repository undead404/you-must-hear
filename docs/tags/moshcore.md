## moshcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T04:55:51.831Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 06:55:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Moshcore is an aggressive subgenre of metalcore and hardcore punk designed specifically to incite intense moshing and physical crowd participation at live shows. The music features crushingly heavy breakdowns, rhythmic chugging guitars, and confrontational vocals that prioritize raw energy over technical complexity. Emerging from early 2000s hardcore scenes, it emphasizes visceral impact and crowd interaction above all else.
