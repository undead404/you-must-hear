## ohio

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-23T00:11:36.718Z">Thu Oct 23 2025 03:11:36 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

&quot;Ohio&quot; represents the diverse independent music scene emerging from Ohio, characterized by lo-fi indie rock, experimental pop, and genre-blending alternative sounds. The style reflects the state&#39;s working-class ethos through DIY production ethics and unconventional songwriting approaches. Artists from this tradition often embrace raw authenticity over polish, creating music that challenges mainstream conventions while maintaining melodic accessibility.
