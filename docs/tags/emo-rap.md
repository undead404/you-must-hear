## emo rap

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-17T17:10:54.390Z">Mon Nov 17 2025 19:10:54 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Emo rap, also known as emo hip hop or SoundCloud rap, is a subgenre of hip hop music that combines elements of emo and punk rock with trap music. It originated on SoundCloud, a popular music sharing platform, in the mid-2010s and became popular in the late 2010s. Emo rap typically features melancholic and introspective lyrics about themes such as depression, anxiety, and drug abuse, often with references to emo and punk rock culture. The music often features distorted guitar samples, heavy basslines, and electronic beats. Some notable artists in the emo rap genre include Lil Peep, XXXTentacion, and Juice WRLD.
