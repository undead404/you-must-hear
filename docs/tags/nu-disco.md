## nu-disco

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-22T16:31:22.657Z">Sat Nov 22 2025 18:31:22 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Nu-disco is a genre of dance music that emerged in the early 2000s and is influenced by disco, funk, and electronic music. It combines the groovy rhythms and melodies of classic disco with modern production techniques and elements of house and electro. Nu-disco is characterized by its use of funky basslines, catchy melodies, and often includes live instrumentation such as guitar, bass, and drums. It is often associated with the disco revival movement and has gained popularity in both underground and mainstream dance music scenes.
