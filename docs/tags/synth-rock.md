## synth rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T10:43:03.667Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 12:43:03 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Synth rock blends the energy and structure of rock music with prominent synthesizers and electronic textures, creating a sound that bridges organic and digital worlds. Emerging from new wave and post-punk movements, it emphasizes atmospheric layers and melodic hooks while maintaining guitar-driven intensity. The style ranges from darkly theatrical to brightly anthemic, united by its fusion of electronic production with rock&#39;s emotional directness.
