## blue-eyed soul

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-29T20:05:56.474Z">Wed Oct 29 2025 22:05:56 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Blue-eyed soul is R&amp;B and soul music performed by white artists, emerging in the 1960s when non-Black musicians adopted the emotional vocal style and instrumentation of African American soul. The genre blends gospel-influenced singing, rhythm and blues grooves, and pop sensibilities. While sometimes controversial due to questions of cultural appropriation, it has produced commercially successful and critically acclaimed music across multiple decades.
