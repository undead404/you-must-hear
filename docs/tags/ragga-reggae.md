## ragga-reggae

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T11:38:56.576Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 13:38:56 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Ragga-reggae blends traditional reggae&#39;s rhythmic foundation with the digital, dancehall-influenced sound of ragga, featuring rapid-fire DJ-style vocals over electronic beats and synthesized rhythms. Emerging from Jamaica in the late 1980s, it brought a grittier, more urban edge to reggae while maintaining its conscious lyrics and cultural roots. The style emphasizes energetic delivery, heavy bass lines, and production that bridges classic reggae&#39;s warmth with modern dancehall&#39;s intensity.
