## roots rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T01:02:46.017Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 03:02:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Roots rock is a back-to-basics style that emerged in the late 1960s as artists stripped away psychedelic excess to reconnect with rock&#39;s foundations in folk, country, and blues. The genre emphasizes organic instrumentation, straightforward songwriting, and authentic American musical traditions. It values raw emotion and musical honesty over studio polish and commercial trends.
