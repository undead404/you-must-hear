## slow jams

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T14:27:24.834Z">Sat Nov 08 2025 16:27:24 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Slow jams are sensual R&amp;B songs with relaxed tempos, smooth melodies, and romantic or intimate lyrics. Emerging from 1990s and 2000s urban music culture, they emphasize vocal harmonies and seductive grooves designed for late-night listening. The genre creates a sultry atmosphere perfect for romance and emotional connection.
