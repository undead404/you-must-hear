## vibraphone

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T14:03:48.688Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 16:03:48 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

The vibraphone is a jazz mallet instrument with metal bars and motor-driven resonators that create a distinctive shimmering, bell-like tone with natural vibrato. Its warm, mellow sound became central to cool jazz and Latin jazz from the 1950s onward, bringing an elegant, sophisticated atmosphere to small group settings. The instrument bridges percussive rhythm and melodic expression, capable of both gentle accompaniment and virtuosic improvised solos.
