## coldwave

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:43:42.329Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:43:42 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Coldwave is a dark, minimalist genre that emerged from post-punk in the late 1970s, characterized by icy synthesizers, driving basslines, and detached vocals. The music creates an atmosphere of urban alienation and emotional distance, often reflecting themes of isolation and dystopia. Its stark, melancholic sound has experienced revivals across different eras and regions, particularly in Eastern Europe.
