## british folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-21T02:20:35.331Z">Tue Oct 21 2025 05:20:35 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

British folk encompasses traditional and contemporary music rooted in the storytelling and acoustic traditions of the British Isles, characterized by fingerpicked guitar, modal melodies, and poetic lyricism. The sound blends ancient ballads and rural working-class heritage with 1960s-70s folk revival innovation, often featuring introspective vocals and Celtic influences. It evokes pastoral landscapes and social history through intimate, unadorned arrangements.
