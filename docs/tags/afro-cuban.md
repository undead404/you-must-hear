## afro-cuban

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-03T17:25:40.825Z">Mon Nov 03 2025 19:25:40 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Afro-Cuban blends African rhythmic traditions with Cuban musical elements, creating an infectious, percussion-driven sound that emphasizes complex polyrhythms and call-and-response vocals. The style emerged from Cuba&#39;s cultural fusion, featuring instruments like congas, bongos, and claves alongside brass sections and piano. It&#39;s characterized by syncopated rhythms, energetic dance tempos, and a vibrant interplay between African-derived percussion and Latin jazz harmonies.
