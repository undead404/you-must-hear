## techno

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T23:38:30.401Z">Sun Nov 09 2025 01:38:30 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Techno is a genre of electronic dance music (EDM) that originated in Detroit, Michigan in the United States during the mid-to-late 1980s. It is characterized by a repetitive 4&#x2F;4 beat, synthesized melodies, and a focus on creating an immersive, hypnotic atmosphere for dancing. Techno often features complex, layered rhythms and the use of digital and analog synthesizers, drum machines, and other electronic instruments. It has since become a global phenomenon, with techno scenes and sub-genres emerging in various countries around the world.
