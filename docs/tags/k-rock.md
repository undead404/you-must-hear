## k-rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:28:59.520Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:28:59 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

K-rock is a genre blending Korean pop sensibilities with rock instrumentation, ranging from indie and alternative sounds to heavier metal influences. Emerging from South Korea&#39;s vibrant music scene, it often features polished production alongside emotional lyrics sung in Korean. The genre spans guitar-driven bands, experimental groups, and idol acts incorporating rock elements into their performances.
