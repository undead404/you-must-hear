## pop rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-30T08:53:50.940Z">Thu Oct 30 2025 10:53:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Pop rock blends the catchy melodies and accessible structures of pop music with the guitar-driven energy of rock. Emerging in the 1960s, it emphasizes strong hooks and relatable lyrics while maintaining mainstream appeal. The genre balances rock&#39;s edge with pop&#39;s radio-friendly polish.
