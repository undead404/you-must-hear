## roots reggae

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T00:22:46.350Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 02:22:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Roots reggae emerged in 1970s Jamaica as a spiritually-focused style emphasizing Rastafarian themes, social justice, and African identity. The sound features heavy basslines, steady one-drop rhythms, and often incorporates dub production techniques with echo and reverb effects. Its conscious lyrics and meditative groove made it both a form of cultural resistance and a vehicle for spiritual expression.
