## visual kei

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T08:29:13.379Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 10:29:13 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Visual kei is a Japanese rock movement originating in the 1980s that emphasizes dramatic visual presentation alongside diverse musical styles ranging from punk to metal to pop. Bands are known for elaborate costumes, striking makeup, and androgynous aesthetics that challenge gender norms. The genre prioritizes theatrical performance and visual artistry as equally important to the music itself.
