## viola da gamba

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T07:53:00.699Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 10:53:00 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

The viola da gamba is a bowed string instrument with frets that flourished during the Renaissance and Baroque periods, producing a warm, resonant tone between a cello and violin. This tag encompasses both historical performances of early music and contemporary experimental works that feature the instrument&#39;s distinctive voice. The sound evokes intimate chamber settings and courtly elegance while also appearing in modern compositions that explore its unique timbral qualities.
