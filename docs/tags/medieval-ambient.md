## medieval ambient

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T23:34:44.992Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 01:34:44 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Medieval ambient music is a subgenre of ambient music that incorporates elements of medieval music, such as Gregorian chants, lutes, harps, and other instruments commonly used in medieval Europe. It aims to create a soundscape that evokes the feeling of being in a medieval setting, often incorporating field recordings of nature or historical locations. The music may be slow and contemplative or more upbeat and celebratory, but it typically retains a sense of timelessness and spirituality associated with the medieval era.
