## percussion

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T13:37:05.597Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 15:37:05 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Percussion encompasses music where drums, shakers, bells, and other struck or shaken instruments take center stage, creating rhythmic foundations that span from primal tribal beats to electronic soundscapes. This tag crosses all cultural boundaries, appearing in Afro-Cuban jazz, experimental electronic music, theatrical performances, and pop productions where rhythm drives the emotional impact. The focus is on texture, groove, and the physical sensation of beat-driven sound rather than melody or harmony.
