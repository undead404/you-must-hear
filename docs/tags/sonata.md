### Description

A sonata is a multi-movement instrumental composition that emerged during the Baroque era and reached its pinnacle in the Classical and Romantic periods. Typically written for solo piano or a solo instrument with piano accompaniment, it features contrasting movements that showcase technical virtuosity and emotional depth. The form emphasizes structured musical dialogue and development, making it a cornerstone of Western classical music performance and composition.
