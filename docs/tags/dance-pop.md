## dance-pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T11:18:07.888Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 13:18:07 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dance-pop is a subgenre of pop music that emerged in the early 1980s and became popular in the late 1990s. It combines elements of dance, electronic, and pop music, and typically features a strong beat, catchy hooks, and easy-to-follow lyrics. Dance-pop often includes elements of disco, house, and techno music, and is intended to be played in nightclubs or at dance parties. It is known for its upbeat and energetic sound, and has been a popular genre in the mainstream music industry for several decades. Some of the most successful dance-pop artists include Madonna, Britney Spears, Lady Gaga, and Justin Timberlake.
