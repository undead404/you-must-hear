## neofolk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T00:52:03.981Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 02:52:03 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Neofolk blends traditional folk instrumentation with post-industrial and experimental sounds, often exploring European cultural themes and mythology. The genre emerged in the 1980s from post-punk circles, characterized by acoustic guitars, martial rhythms, and atmospheric arrangements. Its aesthetic ranges from minimalist and introspective to ritualistic and darkly romantic.
