## microtonal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T06:41:45.068Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 08:41:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Microtonal music uses intervals smaller than the standard half-steps found on a piano, dividing octaves into more than twelve notes to explore unusual scales and tunings. This approach creates otherworldly, sometimes dissonant sounds that can range from subtly exotic to dramatically alien, challenging Western ears accustomed to conventional pitch relationships. Found across experimental classical, avant-garde metal, and electronic music, it represents both ancient non-Western traditions and modern sonic exploration.
