## blackened death metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T01:41:57.444Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 03:41:57 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Blackened death metal fuses the raw, atmospheric darkness of black metal with death metal&#39;s brutal intensity and technical precision. The genre emerged in the 1990s, characterized by tremolo-picked guitars, blast beats, and vocals alternating between harsh shrieks and guttural growls. It often explores themes of anti-religion, occultism, and nihilism with an aggressive yet atmospheric sound.
