### Description

Kabarett music, also known as cabaret music, is a type of music that is associated with the cabaret culture that originated in France in the late 19th century. It is a form of entertainment that combines music, comedy, satire, and political commentary. Kabarett music often features vocalists singing in a theatrical style accompanied by piano or small ensembles. The lyrics of kabarett songs can be humorous, cynical, or provocative, and often reflect the social and political issues of the time. The genre has had a lasting influence on music and theater, and has been adapted and evolved in various countries and languages.
