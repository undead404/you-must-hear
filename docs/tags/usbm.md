## usbm

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:44:50.860Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:44:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

USBM stands for United States Black Metal, a subgenre of black metal that originated in the United States in the early 1990s. USBM bands are typically characterized by their harsh and aggressive sound, which often includes fast tempos, tremolo picking, blast beats, and dissonant chord progressions. USBM bands often incorporate elements of other genres such as death metal, thrash metal, and punk rock into their music. Some notable USBM bands include Wolves in the Throne Room, Xasthur, Leviathan, and Agalloch.
