### Description

This tag encompasses music featuring female vocalists from across Asia, spanning diverse languages including Korean, Japanese, Mandarin, and Turkish. The sound ranges from K-pop and J-pop to Mandopop and experimental electronica, unified by strong vocal performances and polished production. It represents the global reach of Asian pop music and its influence on contemporary mainstream culture.
