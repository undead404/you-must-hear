## psychedelic metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T11:48:30.220Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 13:48:30 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Psychedelic metal fuses the heaviness and aggression of metal with hypnotic, mind-altering elements like swirling guitar effects, droning repetition, and atmospheric textures. The sound evokes altered states of consciousness through extended jams, unconventional song structures, and a hazy, often doom-laden sonic palette. It bridges the raw power of metal with the experimental, trance-inducing qualities of psychedelic rock.
