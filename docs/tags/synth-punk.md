## synth punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T00:51:40.162Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 02:51:40 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Synth punk fuses punk rock&#39;s aggressive energy and DIY ethos with electronic synthesizers and drum machines, creating an abrasive, high-energy sound. Emerging from late 1970s post-punk experimentation, it strips away traditional guitars in favor of harsh, distorted electronics and confrontational vocals. The genre embraces chaos and experimentation while maintaining punk&#39;s rebellious, anti-establishment spirit.
