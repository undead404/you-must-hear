## boa vista

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T05:54:11.287Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 07:54:11 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Boa Vista is a Brazilian music genre that emerged from the country&#39;s new wave and rock scene of the 1980s. It blends tropical rhythms with post-punk energy, featuring jangly guitars, reggae influences, and Portuguese lyrics. The style captures urban Brazilian youth culture with an upbeat, danceable sound.
