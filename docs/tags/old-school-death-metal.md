## old school death metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T10:19:57.073Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 12:19:57 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Old school death metal emerged in the late 1980s as an extreme subgenre characterized by heavy, downtuned guitars, guttural vocals, and blast beat drumming. The music explores dark themes of mortality and horror through aggressive, technical compositions. It laid the foundation for all subsequent death metal styles.
