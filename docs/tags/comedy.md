### Description

Comedy music is a genre of music that is intended to be humorous or satirical in nature. It often incorporates elements of other musical genres, such as rock, pop, country, or hip hop, and features lyrics that are clever, witty, or absurd. Comedy music can take many forms, ranging from novelty songs and parodies to musical skits and comedy albums. Some notable examples of comedy musicians include &quot;Weird Al&quot; Yankovic, Spike Jones, Tom Lehrer, and Flight of the Conchords.
