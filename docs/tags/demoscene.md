## demoscene

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T01:09:46.318Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 03:09:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Demoscene music refers to the music created specifically for the demoscene, which is a subculture of computer programmers, artists, and musicians who create self-contained audiovisual demonstrations called &quot;demos&quot; that run in real-time on a computer. Demoscene music is typically electronic and composed for specific hardware platforms, such as the Commodore 64 or Amiga, using specialized software and programming techniques. It is often highly technical and innovative, with an emphasis on creating complex and unique sounds using limited resources. Demoscene music has a dedicated following within the demoscene community and has also gained recognition outside of it for its creativity and technical skill.
