## classical piano

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T11:19:34.672Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 13:19:34 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Classical piano encompasses solo keyboard works from the Baroque era through modern compositions, featuring pieces that range from technically demanding études to emotionally expressive sonatas. Rooted in European concert traditions, the genre emphasizes precise technique, dynamic control, and interpretive artistry to convey composers&#39; intricate musical ideas. Performances showcase the piano&#39;s full expressive range through works that have become foundational to Western art music.
