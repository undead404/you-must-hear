## doomgaze

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T14:27:31.366Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 16:27:31 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Doomgaze merges the crushing heaviness and glacial pace of doom metal with shoegaze&#39;s ethereal textures and walls of distorted sound. The result is an immersive, often overwhelming sonic experience that balances brutal low-end weight with dreamy, reverb-drenched atmospheres. This fusion creates meditative yet oppressive soundscapes that blur the line between beauty and bleakness.
