## soul jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T19:43:45.955Z">Sat Nov 08 2025 21:43:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Soul jazz blends the improvisational spirit of jazz with the groove and emotional intensity of soul and R&amp;B, emphasizing infectious rhythms and bluesy melodies over complex harmonic exploration. Emerging in the 1960s, it brought jazz closer to popular music through danceable beats, funky organ lines, and accessible hooks while maintaining artistic sophistication. The style bridges sacred and secular African American musical traditions, creating music that moves both body and mind with equal power.
