## crank wave

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T02:26:45.938Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 04:26:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Crank wave is a raw, aggressive style of post-punk that emerged in the 2020s, blending abrasive noise rock with sardonic social commentary. The sound features angular guitars, shouted vocals, and deliberately discordant production that channels urban anxiety and working-class frustration. It&#39;s characterized by its confrontational energy and darkly humorous critique of modern life.
