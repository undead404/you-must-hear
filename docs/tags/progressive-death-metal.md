## progressive death metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T02:41:47.368Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 04:41:47 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Progressive death metal is a subgenre of death metal that incorporates progressive elements, such as complex song structures, odd time signatures, and experimental sounds, into the traditional aggressive and heavy sound of death metal. The genre emerged in the late 1980s and early 1990s, with bands like Atheist, Cynic, and Death incorporating elements of jazz, fusion, and other genres into their music. Progressive death metal often features technically demanding instrumental performances, intricate guitar work, and a range of vocal styles, from growling to clean singing.
