## crossover thrash

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T02:08:58.140Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 04:08:58 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Crossover thrash emerged in the 1980s by fusing hardcore punk&#39;s raw aggression with thrash metal&#39;s technical precision and speed. The genre features breakneck tempos, shouted vocals, and brief, intense songs that appeal to both punk and metal audiences. Its rebellious spirit and high-energy sound made it a staple of underground music scenes.
