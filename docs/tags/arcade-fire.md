## arcade fire

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-09T05:40:04.158Z">Tue Dec 09 2025 07:40:04 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

This tag captures indie rock with grand, emotional orchestration featuring rich layers of strings, brass, and communal vocals. The sound evokes anthemic, theatrical arrangements that build from intimate moments to sweeping crescendos, often exploring themes of suburban life, spirituality, and collective experience. It represents a distinctly literary approach to rock music that values earnestness and orchestral ambition over minimalism.
