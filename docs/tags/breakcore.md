## breakcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T02:26:58.920Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 04:26:58 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Breakcore is an extreme electronic music genre characterized by aggressive, heavily chopped breakbeats, distorted bass, and chaotic sampling at very high tempos (160-300 BPM). It emerged from jungle and hardcore techno scenes in the 1990s, embracing maximalist production and deliberately abrasive aesthetics. The genre thrives in underground internet communities where producers experiment with glitchy, frenetic soundscapes that blend intensity with technical complexity.
