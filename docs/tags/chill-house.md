## chill house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T19:35:14.479Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 21:35:14 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Chill house is a relaxed electronic dance music style that combines smooth house beats with mellow melodies and atmospheric sounds. It emerged from beach club culture and creates an easygoing vibe perfect for lounging or background listening. The genre blends pop sensibilities with understated production.
