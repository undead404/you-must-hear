## fado

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-21T01:09:09.422Z">Tue Oct 21 2025 04:09:09 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Fado is a melancholic Portuguese music genre characterized by mournful vocals accompanied by acoustic guitar, expressing themes of longing, fate, and nostalgia. Originating in Lisbon&#39;s working-class neighborhoods in the early 19th century, it embodies the Portuguese concept of &quot;saudade&quot;—a deep emotional yearning. The genre features passionate, often improvised singing that conveys profound emotion through dramatic vocal ornaments.
