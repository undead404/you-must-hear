## riddim

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T06:46:14.031Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 09:46:14 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Riddim is a minimalist, groove-focused subgenre of dubstep characterized by repetitive, syncopated bassline patterns and sparse, head-nodding rhythms. The sound strips away melodic complexity in favor of raw, metallic wobbles and space between drops, creating an almost hypnotic, aggressive energy. Originating in the UK bass scene, it emphasizes heavy sound design and dancefloor impact over traditional song structure.
