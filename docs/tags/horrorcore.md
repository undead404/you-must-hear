## horrorcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T10:18:31.423Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 12:18:31 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Horrorcore is a subgenre of hip hop music that blends horror-themed lyrics with hardcore and gangsta rap. The lyrics often involve graphic depictions of violence, gore, and horror themes, such as serial killers, supernatural beings, and horror movies. The music is characterized by dark, ominous beats and samples, and often incorporates elements of rock and heavy metal music. Horrorcore emerged in the mid-1990s and has remained a niche subgenre within the hip hop community.
