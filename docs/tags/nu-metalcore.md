## nu metalcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T05:54:10.763Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 07:54:10 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Nu metalcore blends the groove-oriented, hip-hop-influenced heaviness of early 2000s nu-metal with modern metalcore&#39;s technical precision and breakdowns. This hybrid sound emerged in the late 2010s as a new generation of bands revived nu-metal&#39;s accessibility and angst while maintaining metalcore&#39;s aggressive intensity. The style emphasizes crushing low-tuned riffs, syncopated rhythms, and a balance between melodic hooks and brutal heaviness that appeals to fans of both eras.
