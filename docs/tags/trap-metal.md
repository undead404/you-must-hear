## trap metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T10:20:47.214Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 12:20:47 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Trap metal fuses aggressive hip-hop trap beats with distorted guitars and screaming vocals from heavy metal. Emerging from SoundCloud&#39;s underground in the mid-2010s, it channels raw anger and rebellion through harsh production and intense energy. The genre blends 808 bass, punk attitude, and extreme sonic chaos.
