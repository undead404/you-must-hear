## crust

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T05:07:33.501Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 07:07:33 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Crust is an aggressive fusion of anarcho-punk and extreme metal that emerged in mid-1980s Britain, characterized by distorted bass, d-beat drumming, and harsh vocals. The genre embodies anti-authoritarian politics and DIY ethics, often addressing war, poverty, and environmental collapse. Its raw, apocalyptic sound bridges punk&#39;s urgency with metal&#39;s heaviness.
