### Description

The &quot;washington&quot; tag encompasses music created by artists from Washington state, spanning the Pacific Northwest&#39;s diverse musical landscape from jazz and grunge to indie rock. This regional identifier captures the area&#39;s influence on American alternative music, reflecting both Seattle&#39;s urban energy and the broader state&#39;s introspective, rain-soaked atmosphere. The tag connects artists across decades who share geographic roots rather than a singular sound.
