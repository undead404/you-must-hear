### Description

Belarusian music refers to the music of Belarus, a country in Eastern Europe. Belarusian folk music has a rich history, and includes a variety of traditional instruments such as the bagpipe, dulcimer, and hurdy-gurdy. Classical music has also been an important part of Belarusian culture, with composers such as Yefim Volkovitch and Jazep Kozlowski contributing to the country&#39;s musical heritage. In addition, modern Belarusian music includes a range of genres such as rock, pop, and hip hop.
