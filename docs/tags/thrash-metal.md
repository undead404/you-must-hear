## thrash metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-03T06:30:31.657Z">Mon Nov 03 2025 08:30:31 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Thrash metal is a subgenre of heavy metal music that emerged in the early 1980s. It is characterized by fast, aggressive rhythms, heavy, distorted guitars, and often politically or socially conscious lyrics. The genre is usually associated with bands such as Metallica, Slayer, Megadeth, and Anthrax, who are known for their fast tempos, complex song structures, and intense live performances. Thrash metal is often cited as a major influence on the development of other heavy metal subgenres, such as death metal and black metal.
