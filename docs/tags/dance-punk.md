## dance-punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-31T01:18:14.469Z">Fri Oct 31 2025 03:18:14 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dance-punk fuses the raw energy of punk rock with electronic dance beats and funk-inspired basslines, creating an urgent yet danceable sound. Emerging in the late 1970s and resurging in the early 2000s, it thrived in underground club scenes where indie rock met disco. The genre emphasizes rhythmic intensity and angular guitars over traditional punk aggression.
