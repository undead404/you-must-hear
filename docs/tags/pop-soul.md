## pop soul

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T01:45:32.404Z">Fri Nov 14 2025 03:45:32 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Pop soul blends the catchy melodies and polished production of pop music with the emotional vocal delivery and rhythmic grooves of soul and R&amp;B. Emerging prominently in the 2000s, it emphasizes powerful vocals, danceable beats, and radio-friendly hooks. The genre appeals to mainstream audiences while maintaining the expressive depth of traditional soul music.
