### Description

&quot;Aussie&quot; captures the distinctive sound and spirit of Australian popular music, characterized by laid-back grooves, sun-soaked melodies, and a blend of rock, folk, and electronic influences that reflect the country&#39;s coastal culture. The tag encompasses everything from indie rock and roots music to psychedelic pop, unified by an easygoing yet authentic approach to songwriting. This sound often features organic instrumentation mixed with modern production, embodying Australia&#39;s creative independence and outdoor lifestyle.
