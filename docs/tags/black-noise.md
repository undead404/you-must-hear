## black noise

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T07:07:02.291Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 09:07:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Black noise is a subgenre of experimental music that emerged in the early 2000s. It is characterized by an abrasive and dissonant sound that often features harsh, high-pitched tones and distorted textures. Black noise artists often use feedback, distortion, and other unconventional sound manipulation techniques to create their music. Some of the key influences on black noise include power electronics, noise rock, and black metal. The genre has been associated with themes of nihilism, decay, and social and political critique. Some notable black noise artists include Prurient, Wolf Eyes, and Yellow Swans.
