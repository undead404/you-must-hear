## russian metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T15:45:13.754Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 17:45:13 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Russian Metal is a subgenre of heavy metal that originated in Russia in the late 1980s. It combines powerful guitar riffs, pounding drums, and strong vocals to create an intense and energetic sound. The lyrics often focus on Russian history, culture, and mythology, and the music often features traditional Russian instruments such as balalaikas and accordions.
