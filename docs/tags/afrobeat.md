## afrobeat

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-07T18:58:41.294Z">Fri Nov 07 2025 20:58:41 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Afrobeat is a dynamic genre that emerged from West Africa in the 1960s, fusing traditional African rhythms with jazz, funk, and highlife. The music features complex polyrhythmic percussion, extended instrumental sections, and bold horn arrangements that create hypnotic, danceable grooves. Often politically charged, afrobeat reflects social consciousness while celebrating African identity and culture.
