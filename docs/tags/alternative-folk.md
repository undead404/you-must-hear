## alternative folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T00:31:08.357Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 02:31:08 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Alternative folk blends traditional folk instrumentation with experimental, indie rock sensibilities and unconventional song structures. Emerging from the indie music scene of the 1990s and 2000s, it favors introspective, often melancholic lyrics delivered with raw, intimate vocals. The genre embraces lo-fi production and artistic freedom over commercial appeal.
