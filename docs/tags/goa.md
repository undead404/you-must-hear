## goa

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T22:59:04.174Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 00:59:04 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Goa is a psychedelic trance subgenre that emerged from the beach parties of Goa, India in the late 1980s. It features hypnotic, layered synthesizers, driving basslines, and tempos around 140 BPM, creating an immersive, spiritual dance experience. The sound blends Eastern mysticism with electronic production, emphasizing trippy textures and euphoric buildups.
