## world guitar

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T11:06:46.631Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 13:06:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

World guitar music is a genre of music that combines different styles of guitar playing from around the world. It incorporates elements of traditional music from various cultures such as flamenco, African, Middle Eastern, Celtic, Indian, and Latin American music, among others. The music is often characterized by intricate fingerpicking, complex rhythms, and unique scales and modes that are specific to each region&#39;s music. The guitar is the primary instrument used in world guitar music, but other instruments such as percussion, flute, and violin may also be used to complement the guitar melodies and rhythms.
