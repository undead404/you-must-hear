## symphonic rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T16:07:12.808Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 18:07:12 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Symphonic rock blends rock instrumentation with orchestral elements like strings, brass, and classical arrangements. Emerging in the late 1960s and 1970s, it creates grand, cinematic soundscapes that bridge popular and classical music traditions. The genre emphasizes elaborate compositions, lush textures, and often explores conceptual or theatrical themes.
