## orchestral

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T00:36:53.290Z">Sat Nov 08 2025 02:36:53 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Orchestral music features large ensembles of strings, brass, woodwinds, and percussion playing composed arrangements. Rooted in classical tradition, it spans from symphonies to modern cinematic scores and pop collaborations. The genre emphasizes dynamic range, layered textures, and emotional grandeur.
