## idol

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:22:08.833Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:22:08 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Idol is a Japanese pop music genre centered around highly produced groups and soloists who are marketed as aspirational figures through carefully crafted public personas. The sound typically features upbeat J-pop melodies, synchronized choreography, and an emphasis on accessibility and audience connection rather than purely musical virtuosity. Idol culture encompasses fan interaction events, merchandising, and a business model that blends music with entertainment spectacle and parasocial relationships.
