## harmonica

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T06:38:28.523Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 08:38:28 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

The harmonica tag identifies music prominently featuring the small, handheld reed instrument known for its expressive, wailing tone. Deeply rooted in blues, folk, and roots music traditions, the harmonica delivers both mournful bends and spirited riffs that convey raw emotion. Its portable nature and distinctive voice make it a signature sound in American vernacular music.
