## uplifting trance

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-20T09:42:22.741Z">Mon Oct 20 2025 12:42:22 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Uplifting trance is an energetic electronic dance music style characterized by soaring melodies, euphoric breakdowns, and driving beats typically around 138 BPM. It emerged in the late 1990s as a more emotional and anthem-driven evolution of trance, designed to create peak moments of collective joy on dance floors. The sound features layered synthesizers, dramatic build-ups, and an optimistic atmosphere that aims to transport listeners to an elevated emotional state.
