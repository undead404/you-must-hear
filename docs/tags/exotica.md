## exotica

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-21T02:20:29.220Z">Tue Oct 21 2025 05:20:29 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Exotica is a mid-century genre that conjures imaginary tropical paradises through lush orchestration, jungle sounds, and Latin rhythms. Emerging in 1950s America, it blended Polynesian themes with lounge jazz and experimental studio effects to create escapist musical fantasies. The style emphasizes mood and atmosphere over authenticity, evoking tiki bars and faraway shores.
