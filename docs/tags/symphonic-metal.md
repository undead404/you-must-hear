## symphonic metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T01:05:04.682Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 03:05:04 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Symphonic metal is a subgenre of heavy metal music that incorporates symphonic and orchestral elements, such as the use of strings, horns, and choirs. This style of metal typically features the use of classical instruments alongside electric guitars, bass, and drums. Symphonic metal bands often feature a powerful female lead vocalist, who can provide a contrast to the heavy guitar riffs and aggressive drumming. The lyrics of symphonic metal often focus on epic themes, such as fantasy, mythology, and historical events. Some well-known symphonic metal bands include Nightwish, Within Temptation, and Epica.
