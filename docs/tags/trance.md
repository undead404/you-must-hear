## trance

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T11:23:23.240Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 13:23:23 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Trance music is a genre of electronic dance music that originated in Germany in the early 1990s. It is characterized by its melodic, repetitive, and high-energy sound, often featuring a tempo ranging from 130 to 160 beats per minute. Trance music often features layered melodies, intricate harmonies, and builds up to climactic moments known as &quot;drops.&quot; It is also known for its use of synthesizers, drum machines, and other electronic instruments to create a futuristic and hypnotic sound. Trance music has evolved over the years, with subgenres such as progressive trance, uplifting trance, and psytrance.
