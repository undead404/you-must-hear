## mathgrind

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T13:20:35.918Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 15:20:35 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Mathgrind, also known as Techgrind, is a subgenre of grindcore that incorporates complex time signatures and mathematical rhythms. It is characterized by dissonant and chaotic guitar riffs, aggressive vocals, and intense drumming. The lyrics often touch on political and social issues, and the overall sound is abrasive and confrontational.
