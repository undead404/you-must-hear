## blackened deathcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T12:00:32.278Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 14:00:32 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Blackened deathcore fuses the atmospheric darkness and tremolo-picked riffs of black metal with deathcore&#39;s brutal breakdowns and guttural vocals. The style emerged in the 2010s as bands sought to add sinister, occult-tinged ambience to deathcore&#39;s punishing heaviness. It emphasizes blast beats, dissonant melodies, and an overall bleak, apocalyptic atmosphere.
