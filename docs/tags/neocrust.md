## neocrust

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T09:23:54.774Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 11:23:54 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Neocrust is a subgenre of Crust Punk that infuses more metal elements, most often Black Metal, into the genre, making it more melodic in a way similar to screamo. It is known for its melodic, dark, and atmospheric style, often incorporating influences from Sludge Metal, Post-Metal, Black Metal, and Screamo. The sound was first popularized by the band His Hero is Gone, and the genre often crosses into territories of black metal, sludge metal, and screamo.
