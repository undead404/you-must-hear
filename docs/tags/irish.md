## irish

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T17:42:08.871Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 19:42:08 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Irish music refers to music that originated in Ireland or is strongly associated with the country&#39;s culture and history. It encompasses a wide range of styles and genres, including traditional folk music, contemporary folk, rock, pop, and classical music. Some of the most recognizable Irish musical instruments include the fiddle, tin whistle, uilleann pipes, bodhrán, and the Irish harp. Traditional Irish music is known for its intricate melodies and use of rhythmic devices such as &quot;diddling&quot; and &quot;lilting.&quot; Popular Irish musicians include Enya, U2, Sinead O&#39;Connor, The Dubliners, The Chieftains, and Van Morrison.
