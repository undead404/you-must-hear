## chicago house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T21:25:14.149Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 23:25:14 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Chicago House Music originated in the mid-1980s in Chicago, combining elements of disco, European synth music, 1970s soul, and homemade beats created by DJs on drum machines and synthesizers. It is characterized by a steady 4&#x2F;4 time signature played around 120 beats per minute (BPM), giving it a danceable rhythm. The genre is known for its soulful vocals, emotive chord progressions, and the use of early synthesizers, which contribute to its complex and emotional feel.
