## north carolina

### Description

North Carolina&#39;s music scene reflects the state&#39;s geographic and cultural diversity, blending Appalachian folk traditions with punk, indie rock, and experimental sounds. The tag encompasses everything from mountain string bands to college town noise rock, unified by a DIY ethos and regional independence. This eclectic mix captures both rural storytelling traditions and urban creative communities across the Piedmont and coastal plains.
