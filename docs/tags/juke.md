## juke

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T00:41:08.981Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 03:41:08 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Juke is a frenetic, sample-heavy electronic music style born from Chicago&#39;s ghetto house scene, characterized by hyper-accelerated tempos around 160 BPM and chopped, stuttering vocal samples. The sound features relentless kick drum patterns, warped R&amp;B snippets, and an intensely physical energy designed for competitive footwork dancing. Its raw, lo-fi aesthetic and urgent rhythm create an aggressively playful sonic experience that bridges street culture and experimental club music.
