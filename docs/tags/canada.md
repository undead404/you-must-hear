### Description

&quot;Canada&quot; represents the diverse musical output from Canadian artists spanning indie rock, folk, hip-hop, and experimental electronic styles. The genre reflects Canada&#39;s multicultural identity and bilingual heritage, blending introspective songwriting with innovative production techniques. It encompasses everything from melancholic singer-songwriters to avant-garde electronic producers united by their Canadian origin.
