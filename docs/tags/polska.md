### Description

Polska refers to music originating from or strongly associated with Poland, encompassing diverse genres from hip-hop and rock to pop and folk traditions. The tag captures Polish-language lyrics and cultural sensibilities that reflect the nation&#39;s contemporary urban experience and artistic heritage. Music under this tag often blends modern production with distinctly Polish melodic and lyrical approaches.
