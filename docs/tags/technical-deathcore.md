## technical deathcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T04:28:23.532Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 06:28:23 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Technical deathcore combines the brutal breakdowns and aggressive vocals of deathcore with the complex guitar work and precision of technical death metal. The genre emerged in the late 2000s as musicians pushed deathcore&#39;s boundaries through intricate riffs, unconventional time signatures, and virtuosic playing. It appeals to extreme metal fans who appreciate both punishing heaviness and musical sophistication.
