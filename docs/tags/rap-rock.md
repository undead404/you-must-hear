## rap rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T08:12:34.765Z">Sat Nov 08 2025 10:12:34 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rap rock is a music genre that combines elements of hip-hop and rock music. The genre emerged in the late 1980s and early 1990s and is characterized by the use of rap-style vocals, often delivered in an aggressive or confrontational manner, over rock-based instrumentation. Rap rock can vary widely in terms of style, incorporating elements of punk rock, heavy metal, funk, and other genres. Some of the most popular rap rock artists include Rage Against the Machine, Limp Bizkit, and Linkin Park.
