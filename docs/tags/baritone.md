## baritone

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T01:41:24.955Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 03:41:24 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Baritone music refers to a specific vocal range for male singers, characterized by a deep and rich sound that falls between the bass and tenor voice types. The term “baritone” originates from the Greek word “barytonos,” meaning &quot;heavy sounding&quot;. Composers typically write music for this voice in the range from the second F below middle C to the F above middle C in choral music, and from the second G below middle C to the G above middle C in operatic music.
