## electronic lounge jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:25:52.121Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:25:52 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Electronic lounge jazz blends the sophistication of jazz with downtempo electronic beats and ambient textures, creating a relaxed yet urbane atmosphere. Emerging from 1990s chillout culture, it features smooth instrumentation, subtle rhythms, and often incorporates samples or live jazz elements over electronic production. The genre is designed for sophisticated listening environments like cocktail bars and lounges.
