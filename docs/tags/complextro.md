## complextro

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:58:35.066Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:58:35 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Complextro is a high-energy electronic dance music style characterized by intricate, rapidly-changing synth patterns and glitchy sound design that creates a dense, chaotic sonic texture. Emerging from electro house in the early 2010s, it emphasizes technical production complexity with heavy sidechaining, wobbling basslines, and abrupt rhythmic shifts. The style appeals to listeners who appreciate detailed, maximalist electronic music that demands active attention rather than simple dancefloor functionality.
