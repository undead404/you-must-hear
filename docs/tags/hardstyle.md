## hardstyle

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T06:26:45.343Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 08:26:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hardstyle is an intense electronic dance music genre originating from the Netherlands in the late 1990s, characterized by distorted kick drums, energetic tempos around 150 BPM, and euphoric melodies. The sound combines elements of hardcore techno and trance, creating an aggressive yet uplifting atmosphere popular at massive European dance festivals. Its distinctive &quot;reverse bass&quot; and emotional breakdowns have made it a staple of rave culture, inspiring a devoted global following.
