## french black metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T02:03:24.529Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 04:03:24 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

French black metal is a subgenre of black metal music that originated in France during the 1990s. It is characterized by its raw, aggressive sound, often with a focus on atmospheric and melodic elements. French black metal bands are known for incorporating various influences, such as folk, medieval, and classical music, into their sound, as well as incorporating French-language lyrics and themes related to French history and mythology. Notable French black metal bands include Peste Noire, Alcest, Blut Aus Nord, Les Legions Noires, and Deathspell Omega.
