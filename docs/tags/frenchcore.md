## frenchcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-22T18:13:17.674Z">Sat Nov 22 2025 20:13:17 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Frenchcore is an aggressive subgenre of hardcore techno characterized by distorted kickdrums, speeds typically between 180-230 BPM, and dark, industrial atmospheres. Originating in France during the 1990s, it emphasizes raw energy and relentless intensity while incorporating elements of gabber and speedcore. The sound is defined by its punishing bass, frantic percussion, and an uncompromising approach to dancefloor devastation.
