## hymns

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T22:02:28.484Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 00:02:28 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hymns are religious songs or poems typically sung in a church or other Christian worship setting. They are characterized by their emphasis on religious devotion and praise to God. Hymns often have a simple and repetitive melody with lyrics that are easy to sing and remember, making them accessible to a wide range of people. Many hymns have been written over the centuries, and they continue to be an important part of Christian worship and musical traditions. Some well-known hymns include &quot;Amazing Grace,&quot; &quot;How Great Thou Art,&quot; and &quot;Holy, Holy, Holy.&quot;
