## dark ambient

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-16T10:26:33.582Z">Sun Nov 16 2025 12:26:33 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dark ambient music is a subgenre of ambient music that features a darker, more foreboding atmosphere. It typically involves the use of drone-like sounds, often created with synthesizers and other electronic instruments, and incorporates elements of industrial, noise, and experimental music. The overall effect is often unsettling, eerie, and haunting, and the music is often used as a soundtrack for horror films, video games, and other works of dark fiction. Some notable dark ambient artists include Lustmord, Brian Williams, and Robert Rich.
