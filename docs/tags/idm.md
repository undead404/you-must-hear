## idm

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T06:25:56.981Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 08:25:56 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

IDM stands for Intelligent Dance Music, and it is a subgenre of electronic music that emerged in the 1990s. IDM is characterized by its experimental and avant-garde nature, often incorporating complex rhythms and time signatures, unusual sound design, and a wide range of musical influences. IDM is generally associated with artists on the Warp Records label, such as Aphex Twin, Autechre, and Boards of Canada, although there are many other artists who have contributed to the genre over the years. The term &quot;intelligent&quot; was originally used to distinguish IDM from other dance music genres that were perceived as more simplistic and formulaic.
