## uk garage

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T04:01:32.463Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 06:01:32 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

UK Garage is a fast-paced electronic dance music genre that emerged from British club culture in the 1990s, blending house music&#39;s four-on-the-floor beats with syncopated rhythms and chopped vocal samples. The sound features shuffling percussion, deep basslines, and a tempo around 130 BPM. It influenced dubstep and grime while experiencing modern revivals through pop crossovers.
