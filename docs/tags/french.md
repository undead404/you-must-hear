### Description

French music refers to the music created or produced in France or by French-speaking artists. It encompasses a wide range of styles, including chanson, pop, rock, electronic, hip-hop, classical, and traditional folk music. French music has a long and rich history, dating back centuries, and has produced many notable artists and influential musical movements. Some of the most famous French musicians include Édith Piaf, Serge Gainsbourg, Charles Aznavour, Daft Punk, and Phoenix, among many others.
