## twee pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T18:42:38.185Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 20:42:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Twee pop is a subgenre of indie pop music that emerged in the 1980s and early 1990s. It is known for its whimsical and innocent sound, often characterized by jangly guitars, playful melodies, and often saccharine-sweet lyrics. The term &quot;twee&quot; is a British slang word that refers to something excessively quaint or cutesy. Twee pop bands often incorporate elements of retro and vintage sounds, and the genre is associated with a DIY (do-it-yourself) and independent ethos. Some well-known twee pop bands include Belle and Sebastian, The Magnetic Fields, and The Pastels.
