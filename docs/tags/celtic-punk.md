## celtic punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T07:31:23.565Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 09:31:23 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Celtic punk fuses traditional Irish and Scottish folk music with punk rock&#39;s raw energy and rebellious spirit. The genre combines bagpipes, fiddles, and tin whistles with electric guitars and aggressive drumming, creating anthemic songs about working-class struggles, heritage, and revelry.
