## organ

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T03:45:46.073Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 05:45:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

The organ tag encompasses music featuring pipe organs, electronic organs, or Hammond organs as the primary instrument. Its rich, sustained tones appear across sacred classical compositions in cathedrals, jazz and soul grooves, and contemporary drone or ambient works. The instrument&#39;s massive sonic range creates everything from intimate meditation to overwhelming sonic intensity.
