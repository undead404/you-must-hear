## sadcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-24T08:19:38.025Z">Mon Nov 24 2025 10:19:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Sadcore is a subgenre of alternative rock that emerged in the 1990s. It is characterized by slow and melancholic songs, often featuring soft vocals, acoustic guitars, and minimalistic arrangements. The lyrics often deal with themes of heartbreak, loneliness, and depression. The genre is often associated with artists such as Elliott Smith, Red House Painters, and Nick Drake. Sadcore music is known for its introspective and emotional qualities, and has influenced a number of other genres, including indie folk and emo.
