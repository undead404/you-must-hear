## crunk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T22:09:03.686Z">Mon Nov 24 2025 00:09:03 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Crunk is a high-energy subgenre of Southern hip-hop that emerged in the early 2000s, characterized by aggressive, repetitive chants over heavy bass and synthesizers. The music is designed to energize crowds at parties and clubs, often featuring call-and-response vocals and explicit lyrics. Originating primarily in Memphis and Atlanta, crunk became synonymous with a rowdy, celebratory lifestyle centered around nightlife culture.
