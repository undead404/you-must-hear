### Description

&quot;Mother&quot; is a modern pop culture term describing artists who embody confidence, fierce independence, and empowering femininity through their music and persona. The sound spans mainstream pop, hip-hop, and dance genres, characterized by bold production, unapologetic lyrics, and commanding vocal delivery. It celebrates performers who inspire devotion through their charisma, style, and ability to serve iconic, trend-setting moments.
