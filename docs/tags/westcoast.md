## westcoast

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T07:55:04.844Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 10:55:04 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

West Coast represents a laid-back, sun-soaked sound rooted in California&#39;s musical legacy, blending smooth yacht rock, soft rock, and mellow pop from the 1970s and 80s. The style emphasizes polished production, warm harmonies, and gentle grooves that evoke coastal highways and easy living. It captures a distinctly American optimism with its breezy melodies and sophisticated yet accessible songwriting.
