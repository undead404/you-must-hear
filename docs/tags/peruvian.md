## peruvian

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:43:27.215Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:43:27 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Peruvian music is a rich blend of Andean, Spanish, and African roots. It is characterized by the use of traditional instruments like the charango, and styles vary across regions, with the highlands known for huayno, the north for marinera, and the coast for waltz, festejo, and tondero. The music often reflects Peru’s diverse cultural heritage, with melodies echoing Andean influences, rhythms showcasing African elements, and harmonies revealing European touches.
