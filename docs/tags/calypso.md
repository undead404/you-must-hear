## calypso

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-29T07:48:39.520Z">Wed Oct 29 2025 09:48:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Calypso is a rhythmic Caribbean music style originating in Trinidad and Tobago, characterized by syncopated beats, call-and-response vocals, and witty, often satirical lyrics commenting on social and political issues. The sound features steel drums, percussion, and melodic guitar patterns that create an infectious, danceable groove rooted in African and Caribbean traditions. Its playful yet pointed storytelling tradition has influenced numerous tropical and world music styles while maintaining its distinctive island flavor.
