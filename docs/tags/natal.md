## natal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T19:56:43.618Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 21:56:43 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Natal refers to music associated with Christmas and the nativity season, derived from the Portuguese and Spanish word for &quot;Christmas.&quot; This tag encompasses festive holiday recordings across multiple languages and cultural traditions, from classical arrangements to contemporary Christian worship and Latin American Christmas carols. The music typically celebrates themes of birth, celebration, and spiritual reflection during the Christmas period.
