## world fusion

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:33:06.122Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:33:06 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

World fusion blends traditional music from diverse cultures with contemporary styles like jazz, rock, and electronic music. It emerged as artists sought to transcend geographical boundaries, combining instruments, rhythms, and melodies from multiple traditions into innovative hybrid sounds. The genre celebrates cross-cultural collaboration while honoring each tradition&#39;s authenticity.
