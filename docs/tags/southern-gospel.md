## southern gospel

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T07:13:01.511Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 09:13:01 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Southern Gospel music is a genre of Christian music that originated in the southeastern United States. Its lyrics express personal or communal faith regarding biblical teachings and Christian life, often providing an alternative to mainstream secular music. Known for its emotional lyrics, tight harmonies, and spiritual themes, it has evolved into a popular form of music across the United States and overseas.
