## french house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T20:50:22.978Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 23:50:22 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

French house is a sophisticated strand of electronic dance music that emerged from Paris in the 1990s, characterized by filtered disco samples, punchy four-on-the-floor beats, and glamorous production. The sound blends nostalgic funk and soul influences with futuristic synthesizers, creating an irresistibly groovy yet polished aesthetic. It represents a distinctly European take on house music that emphasizes melody, style, and dancefloor euphoria.
