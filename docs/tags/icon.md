### Description

&quot;Icon&quot; celebrates artists who have achieved legendary status and cultural impact that transcends their music, representing figures who&#39;ve shaped pop culture, fashion, and social movements. This tag encompasses both established superstars and emerging performers positioned as the next generation of influential voices. The sound typically leans toward bold, confident pop with theatrical elements and unapologetic self-expression.
