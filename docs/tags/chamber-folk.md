## chamber folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-17T05:50:55.451Z">Mon Nov 17 2025 07:50:55 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Chamber folk is a genre of music that combines elements of folk music with classical music, often utilizing chamber music instrumentation such as strings, woodwinds, and piano. The style is characterized by intricate arrangements, lush harmonies, and a focus on acoustic instruments. Chamber folk artists may also incorporate elements of other genres such as indie rock, world music, and jazz into their compositions. Some notable chamber folk artists include Andrew Bird, Fleet Foxes, and Sufjan Stevens.
