## cuban

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T03:54:53.886Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 05:54:53 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Cuban music blends African rhythms with Spanish melodies, creating infectious dance styles like son, salsa, and rumba. Born from the island&#39;s rich cultural mix, it features syncopated percussion, brass sections, and call-and-response vocals. The genre emphasizes complex polyrhythms and improvisation rooted in both European and Afro-Caribbean traditions.
