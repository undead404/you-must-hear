## melodic metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T01:32:59.332Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 03:32:59 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Melodic metal combines the power and intensity of heavy metal with strong vocal melodies and harmonic guitar work. Emerging in the 1990s from European power metal scenes, it emphasizes accessibility without sacrificing technical skill or aggressive energy. The genre balances soaring choruses with intricate instrumentation and fantasy-inspired themes.
