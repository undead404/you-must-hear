## melodic hardcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-13T06:26:20.148Z">Thu Nov 13 2025 08:26:20 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Melodic hardcore is a subgenre of punk rock and hardcore punk that incorporates melodic elements into its aggressive sound. It often features complex song structures, fast and intense rhythms, and emotional lyrics. Melodic hardcore emerged in the late 1980s and early 1990s, with bands such as Bad Religion, NOFX, and Propagandhi helping to define the sound. The genre has continued to evolve and gain popularity over the years, with newer bands like Defeater and Touche Amore carrying on the tradition.
