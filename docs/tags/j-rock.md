## j-rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-12T22:11:36.637Z">Thu Nov 13 2025 00:11:36 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

J-rock is Japanese rock music that blends Western rock influences with distinctively Japanese pop sensibilities and production styles. Emerging in the 1960s and evolving through decades, it ranges from psychedelic experimentalism to polished pop-rock. The genre often features melodic vocals, intricate arrangements, and a theatrical visual aesthetic.
