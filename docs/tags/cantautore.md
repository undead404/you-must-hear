## cantautore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T07:15:11.922Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 09:15:11 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Cantautore describes the Italian singer-songwriter tradition where poetic lyrics and social commentary merge with melodic, often folk-influenced music. Emerging prominently in the 1960s-70s, it emphasizes literary craftsmanship and personal storytelling that reflects Italian culture, politics, and everyday life. The style values authentic expression and intellectual depth over commercial polish, creating intimate connections between artist and audience.
