## us power metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-09T05:20:04.383Z">Tue Dec 09 2025 07:20:04 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

US power metal is a subgenre of heavy metal that emerged in the United States in the 1980s. It is characterized by its use of fast, melodic guitar riffs, soaring vocals, and often fantasy-themed lyrics. Some of the most influential US power metal bands include Manowar, Iced Earth, Jag Panzer, and Queensrÿche. The genre has continued to evolve and grow in popularity over the years, with newer bands like Symphony X, Kamelot, and Powerwolf gaining a strong following among fans of heavy metal.
