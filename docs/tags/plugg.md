## plugg

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T04:09:45.898Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 06:09:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

&quot;Plugg&quot; is a subgenre of trap music that originated in the southern United States, particularly in Atlanta. The term &quot;plugg&quot; refers to a trap house or drug dealer&#39;s house, and the music typically revolves around themes of drug dealing, street life, and materialism. The subgenre is characterized by its heavy use of 808 drum machines, hard-hitting basslines, and intricate melodies. Zaytoven is often credited as one of the pioneers of the plugg sound, which has since been popularized by artists such as Young Thug, Future, and Gunna.
