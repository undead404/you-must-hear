## hard house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T07:08:55.152Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 09:08:55 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hard house is an aggressive, high-energy evolution of house music characterized by pounding kick drums, distorted basslines, and tempos typically ranging from 145-155 BPM. Emerging from the UK club scene in the late 1990s, it blends the repetitive groove of house with harder techno influences and euphoric synth stabs. The sound emphasizes raw power and relentless momentum, designed to energize peak-time dancefloors with its uncompromising intensity.
