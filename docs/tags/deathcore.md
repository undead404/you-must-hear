## deathcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-31T01:44:14.019Z">Fri Oct 31 2025 03:44:14 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Deathcore is a subgenre of heavy metal music that blends elements of death metal and metalcore. It typically features aggressive and brutal vocals, downtuned guitars, heavy drumming, and often employs breakdowns and chugging riffs. The lyrics often focus on themes of violence, anger, and nihilism. Some notable bands in the deathcore genre include Suicide Silence, Carnifex, Whitechapel, and Job for a Cowboy. The genre has gained a strong following within the metal community, and has evolved and diversified over time to incorporate elements of other genres such as black metal, slam, and progressive metal.
