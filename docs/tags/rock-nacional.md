## rock nacional

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:37:02.960Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:37:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rock nacional is a Latin American rock movement that emerged in the 1980s, primarily in Argentina and Brazil, blending Anglo-American rock influences with local Spanish and Portuguese lyrics addressing social and political themes. The genre encompasses diverse styles from punk and alternative rock to pop-rock, unified by its commitment to singing in native languages rather than English. It became a powerful voice for youth culture and regional identity, often reflecting working-class struggles and resistance against authoritarian regimes.
