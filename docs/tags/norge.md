### Description

&quot;Norge&quot; represents the contemporary pop music scene from Norway, characterized by accessible melodies, polished electronic production, and lyrics often switching between Norwegian and English. The sound blends international pop trends with Nordic sensibilities, featuring everything from tropical house influences to upbeat dance-pop and singer-songwriter intimacy. This tag captures Norway&#39;s modern commercial music landscape, showcasing artists who&#39;ve gained popularity both domestically and internationally through radio-friendly hooks and festival-ready anthems.
