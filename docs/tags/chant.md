## chant

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:51:01.472Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:51:01 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Chant music is a form of rhythmic speaking or singing of words or sounds, often primarily on one or two main pitches called reciting tones. It can range from simple melodies involving a limited set of notes to highly complex musical structures, often including a great deal of repetition of musical subphrases. The repetitive nature of chant music creates a meditative and contemplative quality, blurring the lines between speech and music.
