## electronicore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T00:20:44.205Z">Fri Nov 14 2025 02:20:44 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Electronicore fuses metalcore&#39;s aggressive breakdowns and screamed vocals with electronic dance music elements like synthesizers, programmed beats, and dubstep drops. Emerging in the mid-2000s, the genre bridges heavy music and club culture, creating an energetic sound designed for both mosh pits and dance floors. The style is characterized by its dramatic contrast between brutal heaviness and melodic electronic passages.
