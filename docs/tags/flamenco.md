## flamenco

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-16T12:49:37.497Z">Sun Nov 16 2025 14:49:37 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Flamenco is a passionate Spanish art form combining guitar, vocals, and dance that originated in Andalusia. The music features intricate fingerstyle guitar work, rhythmic hand claps, and emotionally intense singing. It blends Romani, Moorish, and Spanish influences into a dramatic, soulful sound.
