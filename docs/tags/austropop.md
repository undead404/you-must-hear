## austropop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T11:56:20.240Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 13:56:20 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Austropop is a distinctly Austrian popular music genre that emerged in the 1970s, blending rock, pop, and schlager with lyrics predominantly in German and Austrian dialect. The sound ranges from catchy radio-friendly pop-rock to experimental folk influences, unified by a strong regional identity. It celebrates Austrian culture while often incorporating international pop trends.
