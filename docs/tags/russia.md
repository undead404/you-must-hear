## russia

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T12:06:01.150Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 14:06:01 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

The &quot;russia&quot; tag encompasses music created by Russian artists or incorporating Slavic cultural elements, spanning from dark electronic and extreme metal to melancholic hip-hop and indie pop. This diverse collection often features Cyrillic lyrics, atmospheric production, and themes reflecting post-Soviet experiences and contemporary Russian urban life. The sound ranges from brooding and intense to introspective, united by distinctly Eastern European sensibilities.
