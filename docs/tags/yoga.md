## yoga

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T07:19:08.290Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 09:19:08 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Yoga music encompasses calming, meditative soundscapes designed to facilitate mindfulness, breathing exercises, and physical practice. Drawing from Indian classical traditions, ambient textures, and world music influences, it typically features slow tempos, droning instruments like sitars and tanpuras, and gentle atmospheric elements. The music prioritizes creating a peaceful sonic environment that supports relaxation, concentration, and spiritual reflection.
