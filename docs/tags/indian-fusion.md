## indian fusion

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:49:06.908Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:49:06 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Indian fusion music is a unique genre that blends traditional Indian music with contemporary styles. It often features a mix of classical Indian instruments and Western instruments, creating an eclectic sound. This genre emphasizes improvisation and experimentation, making it a vibrant and evolving form of music.
