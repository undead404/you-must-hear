## nsbm

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-09T05:07:51.648Z">Tue Dec 09 2025 07:07:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

NSBM stands for National Socialist Black Metal, which is a subgenre of black metal that incorporates fascist or far-right political ideologies into its music and imagery. This type of music is controversial and has been criticized for promoting extremist and hateful ideologies. Many in the metal community reject NSBM and its associated beliefs.
