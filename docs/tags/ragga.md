## ragga

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-23T06:39:21.982Z">Thu Oct 23 2025 09:39:21 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Ragga, short for raggamuffin, is a subgenre of dancehall that emerged from Jamaica in the 1980s, characterized by digitally produced rhythms and rapid-fire vocal delivery over sparse, bass-heavy beats. The style emphasizes electronic production over live instrumentation, creating an aggressive, energetic sound that became foundational to modern dancehall. Ragga&#39;s influence extends globally, shaping hip-hop, reggaeton, and electronic music while maintaining its roots in Jamaican street culture and sound system traditions.
