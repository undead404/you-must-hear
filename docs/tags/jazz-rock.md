## jazz rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-18T12:01:16.882Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 14:01:16 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Jazz rock fuses the improvisational complexity and sophisticated harmonies of jazz with rock&#39;s electric instruments and driving rhythms. Emerging in the late 1960s, it bridged counterculture and sophistication, appealing to listeners seeking musical adventure. The genre features extended solos, unconventional time signatures, and virtuosic playing.
