## lo-fi indie

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T19:06:44.401Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 21:06:44 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Lo-fi indie music is a genre of indie rock music that incorporates lo-fi production techniques, characterized by its raw and unpolished sound. Lo-fi indie artists often use low-fidelity recording equipment, such as cassette tapes or vintage analog equipment, to capture their sound, creating a vintage or retro feel. The genre is known for its DIY ethos and often features introspective lyrics and simple melodies. Lo-fi indie has gained popularity in recent years, with artists like Mac DeMarco, Soccer Mommy, and Beach Fossils gaining a following for their lo-fi, DIY approach to indie rock.
