I notice the artists you listed (Taylor Swift, Justin Bieber, etc.) don&#39;t actually perform brutal death metal. Here&#39;s an accurate definition:

Brutal death metal is an extreme subgenre emphasizing technical precision, guttural vocals, and relentless blast beats. It emerged in the early 1990s, pushing death metal to more aggressive extremes with themes of gore and violence. The sound prioritizes intensity and speed over melody.
