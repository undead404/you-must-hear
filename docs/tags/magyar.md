## magyar

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T08:12:38.204Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 10:12:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Magyar music refers to the traditional music of Hungary, which has its roots in the music of the Magyar people, an ethnic group native to Hungary. Magyar music is characterized by its unique scales and rhythms, which are different from those used in Western music. The most famous type of Magyar music is probably the Csárdás, a dance form that originated in Hungary in the 19th century and is typically played by a small ensemble consisting of a violin, a cimbalom (a type of hammered dulcimer), and a double bass. Other popular types of Magyar music include Verbunkos (military recruiting music), Hajdú dances (dances of the Hungarian Hajdú people), and Romani music (music of the Hungarian Roma people).
