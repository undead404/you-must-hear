## stand-up

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:54:45.947Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:54:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Stand-up is a performance art where solo comedians deliver humorous monologues directly to a live audience, typically from a stage with only a microphone. The genre emphasizes observational humor, personal storytelling, and social commentary through spoken word rather than music. Performances range from lighthearted anecdotes to provocative cultural critique, showcasing each comedian&#39;s unique voice and perspective.
