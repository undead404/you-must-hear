## progressive metalcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-18T15:39:12.890Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 17:39:12 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Progressive metalcore blends the aggressive breakdowns and harsh vocals of metalcore with the complex time signatures and technical instrumentation of progressive metal. Emerging in the 2000s from the metalcore scene, it appeals to listeners seeking both emotional intensity and musical sophistication. The genre features intricate guitar work, dynamic shifts between clean and screamed vocals, and atmospheric elements that push beyond traditional metalcore&#39;s boundaries.
