## cantautori

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T00:34:07.221Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 03:34:07 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Cantautori refers to the Italian singer-songwriter tradition where artists compose and perform deeply personal, poetic songs that blend literary sophistication with melodic accessibility. Emerging prominently in the 1960s and 70s, this movement emphasizes storytelling, social commentary, and emotional introspection through a distinctly Italian cultural lens. The music ranges from folk-influenced ballads to jazz-tinged compositions, unified by an emphasis on lyrical craftsmanship and authentic artistic expression.
