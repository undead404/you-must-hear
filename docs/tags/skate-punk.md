## skate punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T08:15:08.598Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 10:15:08 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Skate punk is a fast, aggressive style of punk rock that emerged from skateboarding culture in the 1980s. The music features rapid tempos, distorted guitars, and shouted vocals that match the energy and rebellion of skate culture. Its DIY ethos and raw sound appeal to youth seeking authentic, high-energy music.
