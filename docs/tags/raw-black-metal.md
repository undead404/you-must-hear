## raw black metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T06:54:59.889Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 08:54:59 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Raw black metal is an extreme subgenre characterized by deliberately lo-fi production, distorted guitars, shrieking vocals, and relentless blast beats. Emerging from the underground Scandinavian scene in the early 1990s, it emphasizes primitive aggression and anti-commercial aesthetics. The genre often explores dark themes while rejecting polished studio techniques in favor of harsh, unfiltered sonic intensity.
