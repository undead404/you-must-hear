## album rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-01T02:39:51.005Z">Sat Nov 01 2025 04:39:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Album rock emerged in the 1970s as FM radio stations began playing deeper album cuts rather than just hit singles, emphasizing artistic depth over commercial appeal. The genre spans rock, blues, and soul with longer, more experimental tracks that showcase musicianship and production quality. It represents a shift toward treating rock albums as cohesive artistic statements rather than collections of potential hits.
