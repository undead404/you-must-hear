## lds

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:47:30.699Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:47:30 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

LDS refers to music created by or for members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, encompassing both sacred hymns and contemporary secular works. The tag spans diverse genres from classical choir arrangements to modern pop and instrumental pieces, unified by Mormon cultural values rather than a specific sound. This music often emphasizes uplifting themes, family values, and spirituality while maintaining mainstream musical accessibility.
