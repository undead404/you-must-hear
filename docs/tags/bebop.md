## bebop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:33:39.516Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:33:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Bebop is a complex, fast-paced jazz style that emerged in 1940s America, characterized by rapid tempos, intricate melodies, and improvisation. It represented a shift from danceable swing to music for serious listening, emphasizing virtuosity and harmonic innovation. The genre became a cornerstone of modern jazz.
