## soundscape

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-21T01:04:17.802Z">Tue Oct 21 2025 04:04:17 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Soundscape is an atmospheric genre that creates immersive sonic environments through layered textures, ambient sounds, and minimalist composition. It emphasizes mood and space over traditional song structures, often incorporating field recordings, drones, and cinematic elements. The genre serves both meditative and narrative purposes, finding applications in film scores, relaxation settings, and experimental music contexts.
