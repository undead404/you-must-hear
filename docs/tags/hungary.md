## hungary

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T11:15:50.145Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 13:15:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hungarian music refers to the music of Hungary and the ethnic Hungarian people, who have a rich and diverse musical heritage. Traditional Hungarian music includes folk music, classical music, and Romani music, among others. Folk music has played an important role in Hungarian music culture, with various regional styles and instruments such as the cimbalom (dulcimer), violin, and kontra (fiddle). Classical music also has a strong tradition in Hungary, with composers such as Béla Bartók and Franz Liszt being some of the most well-known. Hungary has also made significant contributions to jazz music with musicians like Gábor Szabó and Béla Szakcsi Lakatos.
