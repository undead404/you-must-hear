## rock chileno

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:34:12.158Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:34:12 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rock chileno is Chilean rock music that emerged in the 1980s, blending Anglo-American rock influences with Latin American rhythms and socially conscious Spanish lyrics. The genre ranges from alternative and indie rock to pop-rock and funk fusion, often reflecting Chile&#39;s political history and cultural identity. Its sound is characterized by melodic guitar work, introspective vocals, and a distinctly South American sensibility that sets it apart from other Latin rock movements.
