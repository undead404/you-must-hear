## martial neofolk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:50:56.278Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:50:56 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Martial neofolk, also known as martial industrial, is a subgenre of industrial music that blends elements of folk, dark ambient atmospheres, and neoclassical orchestrations. It incorporates audio from military marches, historical speeches, and political lyrics, often creating an ominous and dramatic atmosphere. This genre emerged in the late 20th century and is characterized by its interest in a particular worldview or philosophy rather than pure experimentalism.
