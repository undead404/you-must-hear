## cyberpunk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:32:11.860Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:32:11 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Cyberpunk music blends aggressive electronic sounds with industrial, punk, and synthwave elements to evoke dystopian high-tech futures. The genre emerged from 1980s underground scenes, merging rebellious energy with digital aesthetics and themes of technological alienation. Its dark, futuristic soundscapes feature distorted synths, mechanical rhythms, and often confrontational vocals.
