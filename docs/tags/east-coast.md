## east coast

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-07T18:56:44.600Z">Fri Nov 07 2025 20:56:44 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

East Coast hip-hop emerged from New York City in the 1970s, characterized by complex lyricism, jazz-influenced samples, and hard-hitting boom-bap drum patterns. The style emphasizes intricate wordplay, street narratives, and aggressive delivery over gritty production. It represents urban authenticity and lyrical skill as core values of hip-hop culture.
