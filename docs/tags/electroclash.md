## electroclash

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-17T11:47:17.838Z">Mon Nov 17 2025 13:47:17 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Electroclash is a music genre that emerged in the late 1990s and early 2000s, combining elements of electronic dance music, new wave, punk rock, and synth-pop. It is characterized by its use of retro-style synthesizers and drum machines, distorted vocals, and lyrics that are often ironic or satirical. The genre is known for its DIY aesthetic and often embraces androgyny and kitsch, while also taking inspiration from the fashion and club culture of the 1980s. Some of the prominent electroclash artists include Fischerspooner, Ladytron, and Peaches.
