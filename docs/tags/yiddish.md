## yiddish

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T08:46:00.676Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 10:46:00 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Yiddish music is a genre of Jewish music that uses the Yiddish language. Yiddish music has its roots in the traditional Jewish music of Eastern Europe, with influences from both Hebrew and local folk music. It was originally performed at Jewish religious events and ceremonies, but over time it developed into a secular form of music as well. Yiddish music has a wide variety of styles, including klezmer, chassidic, folk, theater, and art songs. The themes of Yiddish music range from joyous celebration to lamentation, and the music often reflects the experiences and struggles of Jewish people throughout history.
