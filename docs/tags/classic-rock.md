## classic rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-03T06:34:15.290Z">Mon Nov 03 2025 08:34:15 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Classic rock is a guitar-driven genre that emerged in the 1960s-70s, blending blues, folk, and rock and roll into anthemic songs with memorable riffs and vocal melodies. It defined a generation&#39;s countercultural movement while establishing rock music as a dominant artistic force. The genre emphasizes raw energy, instrumental virtuosity, and rebellious themes that continue to influence modern rock.
