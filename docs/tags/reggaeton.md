## reggaeton

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T06:41:21.992Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 08:41:21 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Reggaeton is a popular music genre that originated in Puerto Rico in the late 1990s. It is a fusion of reggae, dancehall, hip hop, and Latin American music, and typically features a fast tempo, a prominent beat, and the use of electronic instrumentation. Reggaeton often features Spanish-language rapping or singing, and its lyrics often focus on themes like love, sex, partying, and social justice issues. In recent years, reggaeton has become increasingly popular around the world, with many Latin American and Spanish-language artists achieving international success in the genre.
