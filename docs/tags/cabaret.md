## cabaret

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-24T04:56:38.060Z">Mon Nov 24 2025 06:56:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Cabaret is a theatrical music genre combining dramatic vocals, piano-driven melodies, and elements of punk, vaudeville, and dark comedy. It emerged from European performance art traditions and underground club scenes, emphasizing emotional storytelling and avant-garde aesthetics. The sound is intentionally provocative, blending vintage instrumentation with contemporary rebellious energy.
