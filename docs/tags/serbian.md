### Description

Serbian music refers to the music of Serbia and the Serbian diaspora, which includes various styles and genres such as folk, pop, rock, jazz, classical, and others. Traditional Serbian music is characterized by unique rhythms, melodies, and instruments such as the gusle (a one-stringed instrument), the kaval (a type of flute), and the frula (a type of pipe). Folk music is an integral part of Serbian culture, and is often performed at weddings, festivals, and other social events. In recent years, Serbian pop and rock music have gained popularity, with many artists achieving success in the Balkan region and beyond.
