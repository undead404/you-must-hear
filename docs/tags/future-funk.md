## future funk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-18T20:01:32.086Z">Sat Oct 18 2025 23:01:32 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Future funk is an electronic music genre that samples 1970s-80s disco, funk, and city pop, particularly from Japanese sources, reimagining them with modern production techniques. Born from vaporwave in the early 2010s, it emphasizes upbeat grooves, chopped vocals, and nostalgic aesthetics often paired with anime visuals. The genre creates danceable tracks that blend retro sounds with contemporary electronic music sensibilities.
