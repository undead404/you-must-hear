## indietronica

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T21:05:59.005Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 23:05:59 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Indietronica (also known as indie electronic or electro-indie) is a genre of music that blends elements of indie rock and electronic music. It typically features melodic vocals, electronic beats, and synthesizers, and is often influenced by genres such as new wave, post-punk, and shoegaze. The term &quot;indietronica&quot; emerged in the early 2000s as a way to describe a wave of indie artists who were incorporating electronic elements into their music. Some notable indietronica artists include The Postal Service, Passion Pit, LCD Soundsystem, and M83.
