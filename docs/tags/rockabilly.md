## rockabilly

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-03T23:57:50.363Z">Tue Nov 04 2025 01:57:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rockabilly is an energetic fusion of rock and roll with country and hillbilly music that emerged in the American South during the 1950s. The genre features driving rhythms, slap-bass techniques, and distinctive vocal styles that blend country twang with rebellious attitude. Its raw, stripped-down sound became a cornerstone of early rock music and youth culture.
