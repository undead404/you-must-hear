## romania

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-24T00:34:38.223Z">Mon Nov 24 2025 02:34:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Romania has a rich and diverse musical heritage that draws from various cultural influences, including Byzantine, Ottoman, Hungarian, and Roma traditions. Some of the most popular forms of traditional Romanian music include doina (a slow, melancholic ballad), hora (a fast, upbeat dance), and colinda (a Christmas carol). The country is also known for its classical music, with renowned composers such as George Enescu and Dinu Lipatti. In addition, Romania has contributed to the world of pop music with famous artists such as Inna and Alexandra Stan.
