## melbourne

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:42:04.683Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:42:04 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Melbourne, often referred to as the live music capital of the world, hosts over 62,000 live concerts annually, offering more music venues per capita than Austin, Texas. The city’s music scene, which has been influenced by its diverse cultural heritage since the mid-1800s, spans a wide range of genres, fostering many internationally renowned artists and musicians. Melbourne’s progressive music policies and innovative branding, including the establishment of Melbourne Music Week, have helped it become a music tourism destination and a hub for music, literature, visual arts, film, and community radio.
