## rac

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T12:29:44.542Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 14:29:44 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

RAC (an acronym for &quot;Remix Artist Collective&quot;) is an electronic music style that blends indie pop sensibilities with dance-oriented production, characterized by shimmering synths, melodic hooks, and uplifting atmospheres. The genre emerged from remix culture, transforming organic instrumentation into polished, radio-friendly electronic pop. It balances emotional songwriting with accessible, feel-good production that appeals to both mainstream and alternative audiences.
