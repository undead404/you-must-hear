## goregrind

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:45:58.862Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:45:58 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Goregrind is an extreme metal subgenre that emerged in the late 1980s, combining grindcore&#39;s aggressive speed and brevity with death metal&#39;s heavy riffs. The music features pitch-shifted vocals, medical imagery, and transgressive lyrics focused on gore and pathology. It appeals to fans seeking confrontational, visceral sonic intensity within underground extreme music communities.
