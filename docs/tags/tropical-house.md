## tropical house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T04:30:33.767Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 06:30:33 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Tropical house is a subgenre of electronic dance music (EDM) that originated in the early 2010s. It is characterized by its laid-back, island-influenced sound, featuring mellow and uplifting melodies, gentle rhythms, and a strong emphasis on the use of percussion and tropical instruments such as steel drums, marimbas, and congas. The genre gained popularity with the rise of artists such as Kygo, Thomas Jack, and Matoma, and has since become a popular subgenre in EDM, with many artists incorporating elements of tropical house into their music.
