## suomisaundi

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T16:59:25.619Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 18:59:25 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Suomisaundi is a genre of electronic dance music that originated in Finland in the mid-1990s. The term &quot;suomisaundi&quot; literally means &quot;Finnish sound&quot; in Finnish, and the genre is often characterized by its unique blend of psychedelic trance, techno, and traditional Finnish folk music elements. Suomisaundi is known for its playful and experimental approach to music production, incorporating unconventional sounds and samples, and for its emphasis on live performance and improvisation. The genre has a small but dedicated following around the world and has influenced the development of other psytrance sub-genres.
