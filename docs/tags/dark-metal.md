## dark metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T14:50:42.703Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 16:50:42 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dark metal blends extreme metal&#39;s aggressive intensity with gothic and atmospheric elements, creating a brooding soundscape that emphasizes melancholy, darkness, and existential themes. Emerging in the 1990s from black and doom metal traditions, it incorporates symphonic arrangements, slow tempos, and haunting vocals. The genre appeals to listeners drawn to music exploring despair, occultism, and the shadowy aspects of human experience.
