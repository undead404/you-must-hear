## sacred

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:39:06.095Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:39:06 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Sacred music encompasses religious and spiritual compositions created for worship, meditation, or ceremonial purposes across various faith traditions. The sound ranges from ancient choral chants and classical masses to contemporary gospel and devotional works, unified by themes of reverence and transcendence. These pieces often feature vocals, organs, or traditional instruments, designed to elevate the listener&#39;s spiritual experience or accompany religious rituals.
