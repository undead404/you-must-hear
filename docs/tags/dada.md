## dada

### Description

Dada music is a subgenre of experimental music that emerged in the early 20th century in conjunction with the Dada art movement. The Dadaists were a group of artists and writers who sought to challenge traditional art forms and societal norms through their work, and their music was similarly characterized by its unconventional approach and rejection of traditional compositional techniques. Dada music often incorporated found sounds, noise, and other non-musical elements, and it was often performed in non-traditional settings such as cafes or art galleries. Some of the most famous Dada musicians include Kurt Schwitters, Marcel Duchamp, and Hans Arp.
