### Description

The &quot;x-mas&quot; tag encompasses holiday music celebrating Christmas, spanning traditional carols, secular winter songs, and contemporary pop renditions of seasonal classics. These recordings evoke festive cheer through bells, orchestral arrangements, and warm vocal performances that have become cultural touchstones during December. The style bridges generations and genres, from vintage crooner standards to modern pop interpretations of beloved holiday themes.
