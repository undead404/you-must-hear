## musique concrete

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T00:33:52.469Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 02:33:52 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Musique concrète is an experimental approach pioneered in 1940s France that treats recorded sounds—from everyday noises to manipulated instruments—as raw material for composition. Using tape editing, speed manipulation, and layering techniques, practitioners craft abstract soundscapes that challenge traditional notions of music and melody. The tag encompasses both historical avant-garde works and contemporary artists who embrace collage-based, non-musical sound sources in their creative process.
