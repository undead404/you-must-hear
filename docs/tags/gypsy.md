## gypsy

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:35:03.655Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:35:03 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Gypsy music blends Romani musical traditions with jazz, folk, and Eastern European influences, featuring passionate violin, accordion, and acoustic guitar. The genre emphasizes virtuosic improvisation, minor-key melodies, and driving rhythms that evoke both celebration and melancholy. Its sound ranges from Django Reinhardt&#39;s swing jazz to modern indie interpretations with punk energy.
