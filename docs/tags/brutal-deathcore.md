### Description

I appreciate your interest, but I need to clarify that the artists you listed (Justin Bieber, Black Veil Brides, Paul Wall, A$AP Rocky, Insane Clown Posse) don&#39;t represent brutal deathcore. Here&#39;s an accurate definition:

Brutal deathcore combines the technical intensity of death metal with hardcore punk&#39;s aggressive breakdowns, featuring guttural vocals, blast beats, and crushingly heavy guitar riffs. The genre emerged in the early 2000s from the extreme metal underground, emphasizing maximum sonic brutality and physical intensity. It cultivates a devoted fanbase within extreme music communities who appreciate its uncompromising heaviness and cathartic aggression.
