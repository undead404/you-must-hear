## broadway

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T01:01:46.835Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 03:01:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Broadway refers to theatrical music originating from New York&#39;s iconic theater district, featuring show tunes designed to advance storylines and express character emotions. The genre blends various musical styles from jazz to hip-hop, unified by dramatic vocals and orchestral arrangements. These songs are crafted for live performance, prioritizing emotional impact and narrative clarity.
