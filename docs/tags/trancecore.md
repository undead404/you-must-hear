## trancecore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T13:55:24.394Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 15:55:24 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Trancecore fuses the aggressive breakdowns and screamed vocals of metalcore with euphoric trance synths and electronic dance beats. Emerging in the late 2000s, it became popular among youth seeking high-energy music that worked both in mosh pits and clubs. The genre is characterized by dramatic contrasts between heavy guitar riffs and soaring melodic buildups.
