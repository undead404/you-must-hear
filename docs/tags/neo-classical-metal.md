## neo-classical metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T06:43:31.237Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 08:43:31 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Neo-classical metal fuses the technical precision and compositional complexity of Baroque and Classical music with the power and distortion of heavy metal. Characterized by lightning-fast arpeggios, harmonic minor scales, and virtuosic guitar solos that mirror violin techniques, it emerged in the 1980s as guitarists pushed technical boundaries. The style emphasizes shred technique and orchestral arrangements, creating an epic, dramatic sound that bridges classical concert halls and metal stages.
