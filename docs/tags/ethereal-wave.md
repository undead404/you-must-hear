## ethereal wave

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-18T15:38:28.156Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 17:38:28 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Ethereal wave is a atmospheric subgenre of gothic and dream pop that emerged in the 1980s, characterized by reverb-drenched guitars, synthesizers, and often indecipherable or layered vocals. The music creates dreamlike, otherworldly soundscapes that evoke feelings of melancholy and transcendence. It developed within underground alternative scenes, blending post-punk darkness with shoegaze&#39;s lush textures.
