## breakbeat hardcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T13:43:45.961Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 15:43:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Breakbeat hardcore emerged in early 1990s UK rave culture, combining frenetic chopped breakbeats with rumbling bass, piano stabs, and samples from hip-hop, reggae, and hardcore techno. The sound captures the euphoric chaos of illegal warehouse parties, characterized by tempos around 150-170 BPM and a raw, energetic intensity. It laid the groundwork for jungle and drum and bass while maintaining its own playful, rebellious spirit.
