## uk hardcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T19:28:37.002Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 21:28:37 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

UK Hardcore is a genre that evolved from the United Kingdom’s rave scene, characterized by a 4&#x2F;4 kick drum and breakbeat-fueled sound. It emerged around the start of the 1990s and has grown in strength during the 21st century. The genre encompasses numerous styles and influences, often using the latest synthesizers and equipment, resulting in a fresh, modern edge.
