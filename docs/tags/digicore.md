## digicore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-24T14:51:42.627Z">Mon Nov 24 2025 16:51:42 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Digicore is an extremely online genre blending hyperpop&#39;s distorted vocals and glitchy production with emo rap, trap, and punk energy, emerging from internet communities in the late 2010s. The sound features heavily processed vocals, chaotic beats, and lyrics exploring digital-age alienation and youth angst. It represents a DIY bedroom pop movement where young artists create and distribute music entirely through online platforms.
