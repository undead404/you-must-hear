### Description

The &quot;korea&quot; tag encompasses music originating from South Korea, primarily consisting of K-pop and its related genres that blend electronic production, hip-hop influences, and polished pop melodies. These tracks feature both Korean and English lyrics, characterized by dynamic arrangements, synchronized choreography culture, and high-energy performances that emphasize visual and sonic spectacle. The sound reflects Korea&#39;s modern entertainment industry, merging traditional Asian musical elements with contemporary Western pop structures.
