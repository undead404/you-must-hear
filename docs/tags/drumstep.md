## drumstep

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-20T04:00:15.181Z">Mon Oct 20 2025 07:00:15 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Drumstep blends the half-time bass weight of dubstep with drum and bass&#39;s rapid-fire percussion, typically running at 160-180 BPM with drums that feel twice as fast as the low-end groove. This hybrid emerged from UK bass music culture as producers sought to bridge the dancefloor energy of DnB with dubstep&#39;s heavy, wobbling basslines. The result is an aggressive yet dynamic sound that maintains dubstep&#39;s powerful drops while pushing the tempo into high-energy territory.
