## harmolodic

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T20:32:56.609Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 22:32:56 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Harmolodic refers to a revolutionary approach to jazz improvisation developed in the 1970s that eliminates fixed harmonic hierarchies, allowing all instruments to function as equal melodic voices. The music features free-flowing interplay where traditional roles of rhythm, harmony, and melody dissolve into collective spontaneous composition. This creates a sound characterized by angular phrases, shifting tonalities, and democratic instrumental conversation that challenges conventional jazz structure.
