## ukulele

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T07:12:21.340Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 09:12:21 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

The ukulele tag encompasses music featuring the bright, cheerful sound of this small four-stringed instrument, originally from Hawaii but now embraced worldwide. Its distinctive plucky tone evokes tropical warmth and accessible simplicity, making it popular across folk, indie, and novelty music. The instrument&#39;s portability and approachable nature have made it a favorite for both intimate singer-songwriter pieces and playful ensemble arrangements.
