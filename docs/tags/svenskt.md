### Description

Svenskt, meaning &quot;Swedish&quot; in Swedish, encompasses music sung in the Swedish language across various genres from pop and rock to folk and hip-hop. The tag connects diverse musical styles through their shared linguistic and cultural identity, reflecting Sweden&#39;s rich musical heritage and contemporary scene. This category celebrates Swedish-language expression in music, from poetic troubadour traditions to modern urban sounds.
