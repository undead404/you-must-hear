## horror punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:27:29.855Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:27:29 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Horror punk is a subgenre of punk rock and alternative rock that incorporates horror-themed lyrics, imagery, and musical elements. The genre often draws inspiration from horror movies and literature, as well as from classic punk bands such as The Misfits and The Damned. Horror punk is characterized by its use of macabre and ghoulish themes, fast and aggressive punk rock riffs, and a distinctive vocal style that can range from deep and growling to high-pitched and shrieking. Some well-known horror punk bands include The Misfits, The Cramps, The Damned, and Blitzkid.
