## drag

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:36:44.730Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:36:44 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Drag music encompasses high-energy pop, dance, and electronic tracks that celebrate queer culture and theatrical self-expression, often featuring bold vocals and club-ready beats. The sound draws from house, disco, and Latin pop influences, creating anthemic songs that champion confidence, liberation, and gender fluidity. These tracks serve as soundtracks for drag performance art, blending campy humor with empowering messages that resonate within LGBTQ+ communities and beyond.
