## latin pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T02:36:43.582Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 04:36:43 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Latin pop music, also known as Pop Latino or Latino pop, is a popular music genre that combines elements of Latin music with pop music. It originated in the Spanish-speaking countries of Latin America, as well as among Latino communities in the United States, in the late 20th century. Latin pop is characterized by its upbeat, danceable rhythms, catchy melodies, and the use of Spanish lyrics. The genre has produced many successful artists, including Ricky Martin, Shakira, Enrique Iglesias, and Jennifer Lopez, among others.
