### Description

The &quot;montreal&quot; tag represents the diverse indie music scene emerging from Quebec&#39;s bilingual metropolis, characterized by experimental approaches that blend art-rock sensibilities with electronic textures and baroque pop arrangements. This sound often embraces a certain melancholic sophistication and creative ambition, reflecting the city&#39;s unique cultural fusion of North American and European influences. The tag captures Montreal&#39;s reputation as an incubator for artists who prioritize artistic innovation over commercial convention.
