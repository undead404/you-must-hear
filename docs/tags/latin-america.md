## latin america

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:34:27.802Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:34:27 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Latin America encompasses the diverse musical traditions spanning from Mexico to Argentina, blending Indigenous, African, and European influences into distinctive regional styles. The tag captures everything from Brazilian samba and forró to Colombian cumbia and Argentine rock, unified by Spanish and Portuguese lyrics and characteristic rhythmic patterns. This music reflects the vibrant cultural identity of the continent through passionate vocals, dance-oriented beats, and a mix of traditional and contemporary production.
