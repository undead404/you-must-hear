## world

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T11:48:40.894Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 13:48:40 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

World music encompasses traditional and contemporary styles from diverse global cultures, particularly from Africa, Asia, Latin America, and Celtic regions. The genre blends indigenous instruments, folk melodies, and rhythmic patterns with modern production techniques. It celebrates cultural heritage while often fusing different traditions to create cross-cultural musical dialogues.
