## black ambient

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T05:12:45.953Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 07:12:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Black ambient music is a subgenre of ambient music that incorporates elements of black metal and other dark genres. It typically features slow, droning sounds with minimal melodies, heavy use of distortion and reverb, and a focus on creating a dark, foreboding atmosphere. The genre often explores themes related to darkness, nature, and the supernatural, and is often associated with occult or mystical themes. Some notable black ambient artists include Lustmord, Atrium Carceri, and Svarte Greiner.
