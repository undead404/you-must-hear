## vancouver

### Description

The Vancouver tag encompasses the diverse independent music scene emerging from Canada&#39;s Pacific coast city, characterized by its blend of indie rock, folk, and punk sensibilities with a distinctly West Coast laid-back yet artistically ambitious ethos. The sound often reflects the city&#39;s natural beauty and cultural diversity, mixing introspective songwriting with experimental arrangements and a DIY spirit. This music embodies Vancouver&#39;s identity as a creative hub where maritime influences meet urban innovation.
