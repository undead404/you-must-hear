### Description

This tag encompasses music from Croatia&#39;s diverse musical landscape, blending Balkan folk traditions with modern pop, rock, and electronic influences. The sound often features melodic sensibilities characteristic of the Adriatic region, incorporating both Western European production styles and distinctly Croatian lyrical themes. From energetic rock bands to dance-pop hits, the tag represents the country&#39;s rich musical identity shaped by its unique cultural crossroads between Mediterranean, Central European, and Balkan traditions.
