### Description

Taiwanese music encompasses the diverse musical output from Taiwan, blending traditional Chinese influences with contemporary pop, rock, indie, and electronic styles. The tag reflects Taiwan&#39;s unique cultural position, where Mandopop dominance coexists with experimental indie scenes and occasional incorporation of indigenous and Hokkien elements. Productions often feature polished pop sensibilities alongside more underground sounds, creating a distinctive East Asian musical identity.
