## greekpunk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T09:48:34.608Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 11:48:34 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Greek punk music, also known as &quot;ελληνικό punk&quot; or &quot;ελληνικό πανκ&quot; in Greek, refers to punk rock music that originated in Greece in the late 1970s and early 1980s. Greek punk bands were heavily influenced by British and American punk bands, and often sang in Greek. The lyrics of Greek punk songs often addressed political and social issues, and many of the bands were associated with left-wing politics. Some notable Greek punk bands include Ex-Humans, Panx Romana, and Stress.
