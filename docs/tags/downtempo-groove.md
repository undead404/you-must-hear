## downtempo groove

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:45:24.963Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:45:24 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Downtempo groove blends laid-back electronic beats with jazz, funk, and soul influences, creating a sophisticated yet relaxed atmosphere. Born from 1990s lounge and trip-hop scenes, it emphasizes smooth rhythms and organic instrumentation over dance-floor energy. The genre appeals to listeners seeking mellow, groove-oriented music for casual settings.
