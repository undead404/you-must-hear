## rock n roll

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-31T19:14:51.014Z">Fri Oct 31 2025 21:14:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rock n roll is an energetic music genre that emerged in the 1950s, combining elements of blues, country, and rhythm and blues with electric guitars and driving beats. It became a symbol of youth rebellion and cultural change, emphasizing raw emotion and danceable rhythms. The genre&#39;s straightforward song structures and powerful sound laid the foundation for modern popular music.
