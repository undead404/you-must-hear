## experimental noise

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T10:15:21.375Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 12:15:21 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Experimental noise music is a genre of music that is often characterized by its use of unconventional and non-musical sounds, as well as its disregard for traditional song structures and harmonic progression. It is often associated with avant-garde, experimental, and underground music scenes, and can incorporate elements of electronic music, free jazz, and other genres. Noise music often uses a variety of sources for its sounds, including field recordings, manipulated found sounds, and electronic instruments. Some notable experimental noise artists include Merzbow, Wolf Eyes, and John Wiese.
