## psychobilly

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T13:15:10.163Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 15:15:10 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Psychobilly is a high-energy fusion of 1950s rockabilly and punk rock that emerged in the late 1970s, characterized by upright bass slapping, distorted guitars, and rebellious attitude. The genre embraces horror imagery, B-movie aesthetics, and working-class defiance while maintaining rockabilly&#39;s raw simplicity. Its sound is aggressive yet danceable, appealing to punk and rock fans who appreciate vintage style with a dark, edgy twist.
