## oboe

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T00:45:45.901Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 02:45:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Oboe music refers to any type of music that prominently features the oboe, a woodwind instrument that is known for its distinct timbre and expressive capabilities. The oboe is a popular instrument in many types of classical music, including orchestral, chamber, and solo repertoire. It is also used in some jazz, folk, and popular music genres. Some famous oboe works include Mozart&#39;s Oboe Concerto, Richard Strauss&#39;s Oboe Concerto, and Ralph Vaughan Williams&#39;s Oboe Concerto.
