### Description

Leeds represents the diverse musical output from the northern English city, spanning from goth rock and post-punk to indie pop and soul. The tag reflects Leeds&#39; reputation as a vital music hub with influential venues and a DIY ethos that has nurtured generations of bands. The sound varies widely but shares an independent spirit and raw energy characteristic of the city&#39;s working-class cultural identity.
