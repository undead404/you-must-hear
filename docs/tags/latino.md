## latino

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:49:12.321Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:49:12 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Latino music encompasses diverse Spanish and Portuguese-language styles from Latin America and Spain, blending traditional rhythms with contemporary pop, reggaeton, and urban sounds. The genre features infectious beats, romantic lyrics, and cultural pride rooted in regional traditions like salsa, bachata, and samba. It has become a global phenomenon, celebrating Latin identity while crossing linguistic and cultural boundaries through danceable, energetic productions.
