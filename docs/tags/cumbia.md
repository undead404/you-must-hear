## cumbia

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T01:32:30.095Z">Fri Nov 14 2025 03:32:30 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Cumbia is a rhythmic Latin American dance music genre that originated along Colombia&#39;s Caribbean coast, blending African, Indigenous, and Spanish influences. The music features a distinctive drum pattern, accordion or guitar melodies, and percussion that creates an infectious, danceable groove. It has evolved into numerous regional styles across Latin America, from traditional folk forms to modern electronic and rock fusions.
