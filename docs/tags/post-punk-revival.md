## post-punk revival

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T15:08:07.591Z">Sat Nov 08 2025 17:08:07 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Post-punk revival is a subgenre of alternative rock music that emerged in the early 2000s. It takes influence from the original post-punk movement of the late 1970s and early 1980s, but also incorporates elements of garage rock, indie rock, and punk rock. The sound typically features a stripped-down, raw, and energetic style, with angular guitar riffs, driving basslines, and often aggressive or urgent vocals. Bands associated with the post-punk revival movement include Interpol, The Strokes, Yeah Yeah Yeahs, and Franz Ferdinand, among others.
