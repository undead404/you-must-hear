## viola

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-07T12:01:12.381Z">Fri Nov 07 2025 14:01:12 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Viola music refers to music that is specifically written for or performed on the viola, which is a string instrument that is similar to the violin but larger and deeper in pitch. Viola music can be found in a wide range of genres, including classical, folk, and contemporary music. In classical music, the viola is a commonly used instrument in chamber music ensembles, orchestras, and string quartets. Some famous composers who wrote music specifically for the viola include Johann Sebastian Bach, Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, and Paul Hindemith. In addition to classical music, the viola is also used in traditional and folk music from various parts of the world.
