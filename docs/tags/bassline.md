## bassline

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T17:57:54.664Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 19:57:54 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Bassline is a music genre that originated in the early 2000s in South Yorkshire and the West Midlands, closely related to UK garage. It typically features a four-to-the-floor rhythm at around 135–142 beats per minute, with a strong emphasis on bass, similar to its precursor speed garage. The genre incorporates chopped up vocal samples and a pop music aesthetic, and has evolved over time, with some artists moving into a broader scene sometimes referred to as house &amp; bass, or bass house.
