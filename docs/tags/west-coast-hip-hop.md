## west coast hip hop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-19T20:09:20.571Z">Wed Nov 19 2025 22:09:20 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

West Coast hip hop is a subgenre of hip hop music that originated in the western United States, specifically in California. It emerged in the early 1980s and was influenced by funk, soul, and disco music. West Coast hip hop is characterized by its laid-back and funky beats, smooth melodies, and lyrics that often focus on the gang culture, social issues, and life in the inner cities. Some of the most notable artists associated with West Coast hip hop include N.W.A., Dr. Dre, Ice Cube, Snoop Dogg, Tupac Shakur, and Kendrick Lamar.
