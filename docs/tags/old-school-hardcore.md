## old school hardcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:48:32.935Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:48:32 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Old School Hardcore, often referred to as Hardcore Punk, originated from the early American punk scene and is emulated by contemporary bands. It is characterized by its aggressive and fast-paced nature, featuring short, intense songs that often express socio-political commentaries. The genre is known for its raw and abrasive sound, typically featuring distorted guitars, rapid drumming, and shouted vocals.
