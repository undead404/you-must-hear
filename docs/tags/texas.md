## texas

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-18T12:10:26.842Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 14:10:26 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Texas hip-hop blends Southern rap&#39;s chopped and screwed production style with soulful, melodic flows and heavy bass. Rooted in Houston&#39;s street culture, it emphasizes slow tempos, introspective lyrics, and syrup-influenced vocal effects. The sound ranges from gritty gangsta narratives to smooth, emotional storytelling.
