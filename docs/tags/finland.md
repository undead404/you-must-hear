### Description

The Finland tag encompasses music created by Finnish artists across diverse genres, from melancholic rock and metal to atmospheric electronic and folk-influenced sounds. Finnish music often reflects the country&#39;s cultural character through introspective melodies, dark romanticism, and a distinctive Nordic sensibility. The tag captures both mainstream Finnish popular music and underground scenes that have gained international recognition.
