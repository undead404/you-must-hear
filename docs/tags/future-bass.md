## future bass

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T03:48:36.929Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 05:48:36 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Future bass is a genre of electronic dance music that originated in the late 2000s and early 2010s. It is characterized by its heavy use of synthesizers, atmospheric sounds, and complex rhythms, as well as its incorporation of elements from genres such as hip hop, R&amp;B, and trap music. Future bass often features pitched-up vocal samples and dramatic drops, and is generally considered to be a more melodic and emotional form of bass music. Some of the key artists associated with the genre include Flume, San Holo, Illenium, and Marshmello.
