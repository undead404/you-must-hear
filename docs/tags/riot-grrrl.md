## riot grrrl

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T15:53:50.639Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 17:53:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Riot grrrl is a feminist punk rock movement that emerged in the early 1990s, combining raw, aggressive music with politically charged lyrics about sexism, abuse, and female empowerment. The sound features distorted guitars, confrontational vocals, and DIY production ethics rooted in underground punk culture. Beyond music, it fostered zines, activism, and inclusive spaces challenging mainstream gender norms.
