### Description

Italian music encompasses a diverse range of styles from classical opera and baroque composition to contemporary pop, rock, and hip-hop, all unified by the melodic richness of the Italian language. The genre reflects Italy&#39;s deep cultural heritage while embracing modern influences, blending traditional instrumentation with contemporary production. Known for passionate vocals and lyrical storytelling, Italian music spans from intimate singer-songwriter ballads to energetic folk-rock and urban sounds.
