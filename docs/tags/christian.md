### Description

Christian music is music that contains lyrics focused on faith in Jesus Christ and Christian themes. It encompasses a wide range of musical styles and genres, including contemporary Christian music, gospel music, worship music, and Christian rock. Christian music often incorporates biblical texts and messages of faith and hope, and is used as a form of worship and inspiration for Christian communities. It has a long history and has evolved over time, with roots in hymns and spirituals of the 19th and early 20th centuries, and contemporary forms influenced by pop, rock, and hip-hop music.
