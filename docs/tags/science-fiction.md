## science fiction

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T06:31:19.811Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 08:31:19 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Science fiction music evokes futuristic landscapes, space exploration, and technological wonder through synthesizers, cosmic sound effects, and otherworldly atmospheres. Drawing from electronic, progressive rock, and cinematic traditions, it creates soundscapes that mirror humanity&#39;s fascination with alien worlds and advanced civilizations. The tag encompasses both dramatic film scores and experimental tracks that transport listeners beyond earthly boundaries.
