## celtic folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-21T01:03:36.341Z">Tue Oct 21 2025 04:03:36 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Celtic folk draws from the traditional music of Ireland, Scotland, Brittany, and other Celtic regions, featuring instruments like fiddles, tin whistles, bagpipes, and bodhráns. The sound blends ancient melodies with lively dance rhythms and haunting ballads that reflect centuries of cultural storytelling. Modern interpretations range from purely acoustic traditional arrangements to energetic folk-rock fusions that bring Celtic heritage to contemporary audiences.
