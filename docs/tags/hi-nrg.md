## hi-nrg

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-19T04:32:06.937Z">Wed Nov 19 2025 06:32:06 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hi-NRG is a fast-paced, electronic dance music genre that emerged in the early 1980s, characterized by pounding basslines, synthesizers, and tempos typically above 120 BPM. It became a staple of gay club culture and freestyle dance scenes, emphasizing energetic, euphoric productions designed to keep dancers moving. The genre bridges disco&#39;s theatrical flair with the emerging synth-pop sound of the era.
