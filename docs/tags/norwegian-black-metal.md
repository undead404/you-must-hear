## norwegian black metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T13:52:48.962Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 15:52:48 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Norwegian black metal is an extreme subgenre that emerged in Norway during the early 1990s, characterized by raw production, shrieking vocals, tremolo-picked guitars, and blast beat drumming. The music often incorporates themes of darkness, paganism, anti-Christianity, and Norwegian nature and folklore. Known for its deliberately lo-fi aesthetic and atmospheric intensity, the genre became infamous for its association with church burnings and criminal acts during its formative years.
