## spiritual jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-19T22:49:59.922Z">Thu Nov 20 2025 00:49:59 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Spiritual jazz emerged in the 1960s as a transcendent fusion of modal jazz, African rhythms, and meditative exploration, seeking higher consciousness through extended improvisations and devotional intensity. The music often features hypnotic repetition, cosmic textures, and influences from Eastern philosophy and Black liberation movements. It prioritizes emotional and spiritual journey over traditional song structure, creating immersive soundscapes that feel both ancient and avant-garde.
