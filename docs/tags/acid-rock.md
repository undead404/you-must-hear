## acid rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-01T01:15:50.988Z">Sat Nov 01 2025 03:15:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Acid rock emerged in the mid-1960s as a psychedelic-influenced style characterized by distorted guitars, extended improvisations, and surreal lyrics. The genre reflected counterculture experimentation with mind-altering substances, creating intense, trippy soundscapes. Its raw energy bridged garage rock and heavy metal while embracing experimental sonic textures.
