## electro house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T11:58:29.675Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 13:58:29 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Electro house is a subgenre of electronic dance music (EDM) that emerged in the early 2000s. It blends elements of electro, house, and techno to create a high-energy, upbeat sound that is designed for dancing. The genre typically features a four-on-the-floor beat, heavy use of synthesizers, and distorted basslines. The term &quot;electro house&quot; was popularized by DJs and producers in the mid-2000s to describe a new style of dance music that was emerging in clubs and festivals around the world. Some of the most well-known electro house DJs and producers include Deadmau5, Calvin Harris, and David Guetta.
