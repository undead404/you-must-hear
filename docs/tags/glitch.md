## glitch

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T05:11:05.083Z">Fri Nov 14 2025 07:11:05 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Glitch music is a subgenre of electronic music that incorporates the sounds of errors, glitches, and digital artifacts into the compositions. It often features short, repeating sound snippets, unusual beats, and unconventional time signatures. Glitch music can be created by manipulating digital audio files or by creating custom software and hardware to produce glitchy sounds. The genre emerged in the late 1990s and has since evolved to include a wide range of styles and approaches. Some notable glitch artists include Oval, Kid606, and Fennesz.
