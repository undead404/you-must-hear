## trip

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T11:18:44.424Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 13:18:44 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

&quot;Trip&quot; encompasses music that creates immersive, mind-altering sonic journeys through hypnotic rhythms, psychedelic textures, and atmospheric soundscapes. The tag bridges electronic downtempo, krautrock experimentation, and spacey ambient music designed to transport listeners into altered states of consciousness. These tracks prioritize mood and texture over conventional song structure, evoking dreamlike or meditative experiences.
