## gamelan

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T16:43:36.860Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 18:43:36 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Gamelan is a type of traditional music from Indonesia that primarily features percussion instruments such as gongs, drums, and metallophones. It is often performed as an ensemble, with multiple musicians playing different instruments simultaneously to create a complex and layered sound. Gamelan music has a long history and cultural significance in Indonesia, and it is often associated with traditional ceremonies and cultural events. There are many different styles and regional variations of gamelan music, each with their own unique characteristics and instrumentation.
