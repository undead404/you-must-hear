## electronic rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-05T21:00:50.976Z">Wed Nov 05 2025 23:00:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Electronic rock, also known as electro-rock, is a genre of rock music that incorporates electronic music elements, such as synthesizers, drum machines, and samplers. The genre emerged in the late 1970s and early 1980s with the use of synthesizers in new wave and post-punk music. It gained popularity in the 1990s with the emergence of industrial rock and electronic dance music. Electronic rock can range from experimental and avant-garde to more mainstream and commercial, and can be influenced by a variety of other genres such as punk rock, metal, and pop.
