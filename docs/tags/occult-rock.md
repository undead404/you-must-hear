## occult rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T03:10:42.196Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 05:10:42 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Occult rock fuses heavy, vintage rock sounds with themes of witchcraft, mysticism, and esoteric spirituality, often drawing from 1960s-70s psychedelic and doom aesthetics. The music typically features dark, ritualistic atmospheres, Hammond organs, and theatrical vocals that evoke supernatural imagery and anti-establishment spiritual rebellion. It emerged as both homage to proto-metal&#39;s occult fascinations and genuine exploration of alternative spiritual paths through heavy music.
