### Description

Buffalo represents the eclectic musical output from Buffalo, New York, spanning jazz fusion, alternative rock, punk, and death metal. This geographic tag reflects the city&#39;s working-class grit and creative resilience, producing sounds that range from raw intensity to experimental artistry. The diverse roster showcases Buffalo&#39;s role as an incubator for authentic, uncompromising music across multiple genres.
