## ghazal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T02:37:09.311Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 04:37:09 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Ghazal music is a genre of poetry in South Asia, often set to music. The term &quot;ghazal&quot; refers to a form of poetry that originated in Arabic literature, but became popular in Persian, Urdu, and other languages in the region. Ghazal poetry typically consists of rhyming couplets and themes of love, loss, and longing. Ghazal music often involves the singing of poetry set to music, with instruments such as the tabla, harmonium, and sitar commonly used. It is a highly emotive and expressive genre of music, with singers often improvising and adding their own interpretations to the poetry they are performing.
