## rhythm and blues

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T05:09:43.501Z">Fri Nov 14 2025 07:09:43 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rhythm and blues is a soulful genre blending jazz, gospel, and blues influences with strong vocal performances and steady rhythmic grooves. Originating in African American communities in the 1940s, it emphasizes emotional expression and storytelling. The genre has evolved from its roots to encompass modern R&amp;B with electronic production while maintaining its focus on melody and vocal artistry.
