## greece

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:50:19.565Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:50:19 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Greek music is a rich blend of Eastern and Western influences, reflecting the country’s diverse history. It is divided into two main categories: traditional Greek music and Byzantine music, both of which have been shaped by historical events such as the Byzantine period and Ottoman rule. The music is characterized by simple, expressive melodies that are often harmonized in thirds, making it enjoyable to listen to.
