## bollywood

### Description

Bollywood is the vibrant musical style of Indian Hindi-language cinema, blending traditional Indian classical and folk music with modern pop, electronic, and Western influences. These songs are integral to film narratives, featuring elaborate choreography and emotional storytelling that ranges from romantic ballads to high-energy dance numbers. The genre is characterized by its melodramatic vocals, rich orchestration mixing sitars with synthesizers, and lyrics typically sung in Hindi or Hinglish.
