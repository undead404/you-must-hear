## west coast rap

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T09:45:40.187Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 11:45:40 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

West Coast rap, also known as West Coast hip-hop, is a subgenre of hip-hop music that originated in the West Coast region of the United States in the 1970s. West Coast rap is known for its laid-back, funky beats, and its lyrics that often touch on themes such as gang violence, street life, and urban culture. Some of the most well-known West Coast rap artists include Dr. Dre, Ice Cube, Snoop Dogg, and Tupac Shakur. The West Coast rap scene has had a significant impact on the development of hip-hop music as a whole, and it continues to influence contemporary rap music today.
