## progressive psytrance

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-01-07T14:45:50.572Z">Tue Jan 07 2025 16:45:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Progressive psytrance is a hypnotic electronic dance music style that bridges atmospheric soundscapes with driving rhythms, typically ranging from 135-145 BPM. Emerging from Goa trance in the late 1990s, it emphasizes gradual builds, layered melodies, and psychedelic textures designed for lengthy DJ sets at outdoor festivals. The sound balances accessibility with mind-expanding sonic journeys that evolve subtly over extended periods.
