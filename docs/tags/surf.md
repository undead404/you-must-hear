## surf

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T14:19:22.603Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 16:19:22 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Surf music is an energetic instrumental rock genre that emerged in early 1960s Southern California, characterized by reverb-drenched electric guitars mimicking the power and motion of ocean waves. The sound features rapid picking techniques, tremolo effects, and driving rhythms that evoke beach culture and the thrill of surfing. Though rooted in coastal lifestyle, the genre has evolved to include space-age and experimental elements while maintaining its distinctive wet, rolling guitar tone.
