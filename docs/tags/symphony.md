## symphony

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T02:57:33.055Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 04:57:33 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

A symphony is a large-scale orchestral work typically composed in multiple movements, representing the pinnacle of Western classical music composition from the 18th century onward. These pieces showcase the full range and power of the orchestra, often exploring profound emotional journeys from intimate reflection to triumphant grandeur. The form evolved from courtly entertainment into a vehicle for personal expression and philosophical depth.
