## latin rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T03:41:08.060Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 05:41:08 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Latin rock fuses rock instrumentation and energy with Latin American musical traditions like salsa, cumbia, and bolero. The genre emerged in the 1960s, blending electric guitars and drums with Latin percussion, Spanish lyrics, and Caribbean rhythms. It represents a cultural bridge between Anglo-American rock and Latino identity.
