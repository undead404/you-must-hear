## dancehall reggae

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:35:10.472Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:35:10 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dancehall reggae is an electronic-influenced offshoot of reggae that emerged from Jamaica in the late 1970s, characterized by sparse, digitized rhythms and often provocative lyrics delivered in patois. The genre emphasizes the DJ or &quot;toaster&quot; who chants over minimal, bass-heavy beats rather than traditional live instrumentation. It serves as both party music and social commentary, reflecting urban Jamaican culture through its energetic, dance-oriented sound.
