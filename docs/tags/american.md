### Description

&quot;American music&quot; is a broad term used to refer to the many different styles and genres of music that have originated in the United States or have been influenced by American culture. Some of the most well-known styles of American music include jazz, blues, country, rock and roll, hip hop, and pop music. American music has had a significant impact on popular culture around the world and continues to be a major influence on the global music industry.
