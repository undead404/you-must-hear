## samba

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T09:13:42.042Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 11:13:42 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Samba is a lively Brazilian music genre characterized by syncopated rhythms, percussion-heavy instrumentation, and infectious dance beats rooted in Afro-Brazilian traditions. Born in Rio de Janeiro&#39;s working-class communities, it blends African drumming patterns with European melodic influences. The genre ranges from traditional carnival samba to more sophisticated bossa nova-influenced styles.
