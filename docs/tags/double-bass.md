## double bass

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-07T18:30:46.059Z">Fri Nov 07 2025 20:30:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Double bass music refers to music that prominently features the double bass as a primary instrument. The double bass, also known as the string bass, upright bass, or contrabass, is a large, four-stringed instrument that is played with a bow or plucked with the fingers. It is commonly used in classical music, jazz, and various forms of folk music. In double bass music, the instrument may serve as a solo instrument, part of an ensemble, or as a rhythm section instrument providing a bassline for other instruments to play over. Double bassists may also perform techniques such as pizzicato (plucking the strings) and arco (playing with a bow) to create a variety of sounds and textures.
