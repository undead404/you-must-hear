## space rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-31T00:56:13.978Z">Fri Oct 31 2025 02:56:13 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Space rock is a subgenre of rock music that blends elements of psychedelic rock, progressive rock, and electronic music, often incorporating themes related to science fiction, space, and exploration. It typically features extended instrumental sections and atmospheric soundscapes, often created with the use of electronic instruments such as synthesizers and theremin. Some notable space rock bands include Pink Floyd, Hawkwind, and Ozric Tentacles.
