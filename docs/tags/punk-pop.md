## punk pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-13T16:12:21.939Z">Thu Nov 13 2025 18:12:21 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Punk pop blends the high-energy rebellion and raw attitude of punk rock with infectious melodies and radio-friendly hooks of pop music. Emerging from the underground in the 1990s and exploding into mainstream culture, it captures youthful angst and defiance while remaining accessible and catchy. The sound features distorted power chords, driving rhythms, and sing-along choruses that balance edge with mass appeal.
