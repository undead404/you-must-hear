## salsa

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:32:45.504Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:32:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Salsa is a vibrant Latin dance music genre that emerged in New York City during the 1960s-70s, blending Cuban son, mambo, and Puerto Rican influences with jazz elements. The music features energetic horn sections, complex percussion patterns including congas and timbales, and call-and-response vocals. Its infectious rhythms and passionate delivery make it popular in nightclubs and social dancing worldwide.
