## jazz guitar

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:37:02.417Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:37:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Jazz guitar is a sophisticated style featuring improvised melodies, complex harmonies, and swing rhythms rooted in African American musical traditions. Players use both acoustic and electric guitars to create expressive solos over jazz standards and original compositions. The genre spans from traditional swing to modern fusion styles.
