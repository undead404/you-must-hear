### Description

Polish music refers to music created or performed in Poland, or by Poles around the world. Poland has a rich musical tradition that dates back centuries, with various styles of folk music, classical music, jazz, rock, and pop. Traditional Polish folk music includes genres such as mazurkas, obereks, and polkas, while classical music in Poland has been heavily influenced by composers such as Chopin, Penderecki, and Lutosławski. Polish jazz has also gained international recognition, with artists such as Krzysztof Komeda and Tomasz Stańko achieving worldwide acclaim. In recent years, Polish pop and rock music have become increasingly popular, with artists such as Dawid Podsiadło, Monika Brodka, and Margaret gaining fans both in Poland and abroad.
