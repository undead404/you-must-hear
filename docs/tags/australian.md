### Description

Australian music refers to the music produced and originated in Australia. It encompasses various genres and styles, including rock, pop, hip hop, electronic, country, and classical music. Australian music has a rich history and has been influenced by various cultural and regional factors, including Indigenous music, European traditions, and Asian and African music. Some of the notable Australian musicians and bands include AC&#x2F;DC, INXS, Midnight Oil, Kylie Minogue, Sia, Tame Impala, and Vance Joy, among many others.
