## polish metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T17:35:38.084Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 19:35:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Polish metal encompasses the diverse heavy metal scene emerging from Poland, blending aggressive riffs and dark atmospheres with Slavic cultural influences and historical themes. The sound ranges from thrash and death metal to gothic and folk-inspired variations, often incorporating Polish language lyrics and regional musical elements. This tag represents Poland&#39;s distinct contribution to European metal, characterized by raw energy and a connection to the nation&#39;s complex heritage.
