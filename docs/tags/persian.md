## persian

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T06:21:39.590Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 09:21:39 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Persian music blends ancient Middle Eastern traditions with distinctive melodic modes called dastgah, featuring ornate vocal techniques and instruments like the tar, santur, and ney. The sound ranges from classical compositions steeped in poetry and mysticism to contemporary fusions incorporating rock, electronic, and jazz elements. It carries the cultural essence of Iran and surrounding regions, characterized by microtonal scales, improvisational passages, and deeply emotional expression.
