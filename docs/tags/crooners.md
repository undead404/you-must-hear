## crooners

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:57:16.832Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:57:16 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Crooners are smooth-voiced male vocalists who rose to prominence from the 1930s-1960s, singing romantic ballads and jazz standards with intimate, conversational delivery. The style emphasized rich tone, precise phrasing, and emotional nuance over vocal power, made possible by microphone technology. Crooners became cultural icons of sophistication and romance, defining an era of American popular music.
