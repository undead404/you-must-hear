## melodic trance

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T04:32:43.901Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 06:32:43 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Melodic trance emphasizes uplifting, emotional melodies and euphoric breakdowns layered over driving 130-140 BPM beats, creating an immersive and transcendent listening experience. Emerging from European club culture in the late 1990s, it balances accessible pop sensibilities with the hypnotic intensity of traditional trance. The sound features soaring synthesizer leads, atmospheric pads, and carefully crafted build-ups that evoke feelings of elation and emotional release on the dancefloor.
