## indian

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T08:50:38.625Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 10:50:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Indian music encompasses diverse classical traditions like Hindustani and Carnatic, built on intricate ragas and talas with instruments like sitar and tabla. It blends ancient spiritual roots with regional folk styles and modern Bollywood pop fusion. The genre emphasizes improvisation, complex rhythms, and melodic ornamentation spanning centuries of cultural evolution.
