## lounge uptempo

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-02T19:01:06.739Z">Sun Nov 02 2025 21:01:06 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Lounge uptempo blends the sophisticated, easy-listening atmosphere of traditional lounge music with energetic, danceable rhythms typically ranging from 110-130 BPM. The genre emerged in the 1990s as part of the lounge revival movement, combining jazz, funk, and electronic elements with a cosmopolitan, retro-futuristic aesthetic. It&#39;s designed for stylish social settings where conversation and movement coexist, offering a more animated alternative to classic lounge&#39;s relaxed pace.
