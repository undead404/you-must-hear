## sunshine pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T20:41:04.454Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 22:41:04 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Sunshine pop is a subgenre of pop music that emerged in the mid-1960s in Southern California. It is characterized by its upbeat melodies, lush vocal harmonies, and optimistic lyrics about love, happiness, and the California lifestyle. The music often features brass and string arrangements, along with catchy hooks and a general feeling of innocence and naivety. Some of the best-known sunshine pop artists include The Beach Boys, The Mamas &amp; The Papas, and The Association.
