## ambient folk noir

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T05:50:31.407Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 07:50:31 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Ambient folk noir music is a genre of music that combines elements of ambient music, folk music, and noir aesthetics. It often features atmospheric and moody soundscapes, haunting melodies, and introspective lyrics that explore dark and mysterious themes. This style of music can be traced back to the 1980s and has been influenced by artists such as David Lynch, Nick Cave, and Leonard Cohen. It is often characterized by its ethereal and dreamlike quality, creating an immersive and introspective listening experience. Some notable artists in this genre include Marissa Nadler, Chelsea Wolfe, and Emma Ruth Rundle.
