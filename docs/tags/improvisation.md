## improvisation

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T19:40:21.196Z">Sat Nov 08 2025 21:40:21 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Improvisation refers to music created spontaneously in the moment rather than from written compositions, spanning jazz, experimental, and avant-garde traditions across cultures. It emphasizes individual expression, risk-taking, and intuitive dialogue between musicians who respond to each other in real time. The approach values spontaneity over perfection, producing unique performances that can never be exactly replicated.
