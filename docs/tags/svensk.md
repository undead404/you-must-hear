### Description

Svensk refers to music sung in Swedish or created by Swedish artists, encompassing the distinctive sound and cultural identity of Sweden&#39;s diverse music scene. The tag spans multiple genres from schlager and dansband to electronic and reggae, united by Swedish language lyrics or Nordic production sensibilities. It captures the melodic pop traditions and electronic innovations that have made Swedish music internationally recognizable.
