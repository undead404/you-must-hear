## phonk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:52:36.568Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:52:36 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Phonk is a hip-hop subgenre that emerged from Memphis rap, characterized by lo-fi production, heavy bass, and samples from 1990s Southern hip-hop. The music often features dark, atmospheric beats with cowbells, vinyl crackle, and chopped vocals. It gained popularity through SoundCloud and has evolved into various styles including drift phonk with aggressive, high-energy tracks.
