## vaporwave

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-24T02:58:38.067Z">Mon Nov 24 2025 04:58:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Vaporwave is an electronic music genre that emerged online in the early 2010s, characterized by slowed-down samples of 1980s and 90s pop, smooth jazz, and muzak. The sound evokes nostalgic commercialism through heavy reverb, looping, and chopped vocals, often critiquing consumer culture. It&#39;s closely tied to retro internet aesthetics featuring Japanese text, old computer graphics, and dated corporate imagery.
