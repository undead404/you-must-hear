## post-bop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-20T03:59:36.246Z">Mon Oct 20 2025 06:59:36 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Post-bop emerged in the 1960s as jazz musicians pushed beyond bebop&#39;s constraints while maintaining its complexity. The style blends hard bop&#39;s soulful intensity with modal jazz&#39;s spacious harmonies and freer improvisation. It represents a sophisticated middle ground between traditional jazz structures and the avant-garde.
