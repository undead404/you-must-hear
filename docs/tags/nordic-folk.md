## nordic folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:32:41.003Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:32:41 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Nordic folk draws from the ancient musical traditions of Scandinavia and Northern Europe, incorporating traditional instruments like the tagelharpa, bone flute, and frame drums alongside haunting vocal techniques. The sound evokes misty forests and Viking-age spirituality through droning textures, ritualistic chanting, and melodic patterns rooted in pre-Christian Norse culture. This tag encompasses both historical reconstructions and modern interpretations that channel the raw, elemental character of the Nordic landscape.
