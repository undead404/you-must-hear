## noise punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T16:34:27.360Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 18:34:27 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Noise punk merges punk rock&#39;s aggressive energy with harsh, abrasive textures drawn from noise music and industrial sounds. The genre emphasizes distortion, feedback, and unconventional sonic assault while maintaining punk&#39;s raw intensity and anti-establishment spirit. It creates deliberately confrontational music that pushes beyond traditional song structures into chaotic, visceral territory.
