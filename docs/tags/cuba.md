## cuba

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-18T10:13:55.977Z">Sat Oct 18 2025 13:13:55 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Cuban music blends African rhythms with Spanish melodies, creating infectious sounds like son, rumba, and salsa that emphasize percussion, syncopation, and call-and-response vocals. Born from the island&#39;s multicultural heritage, it features instruments like congas, bongos, claves, and tres guitar. The genre celebrates dance, storytelling, and the vibrant spirit of Cuban culture.
