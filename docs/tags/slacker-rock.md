## slacker rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:26:51.267Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:26:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Slacker rock is a lo-fi indie genre characterized by deliberately unpolished production, casual vocals, and DIY bedroom recording aesthetics. Emerging from 1990s alternative culture, it embraces intentional amateurism and emotional vulnerability over technical perfection. The sound typically features distorted guitars, hazy melodies, and introspective lyrics that capture feelings of apathy and youthful alienation.
