Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-18T20:27:23.316Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 22:27:23 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dutch pop encompasses the diverse popular music created in the Netherlands, blending Anglo-American influences with distinctly Dutch lyrical sensibility and production styles. The genre ranges from Eurodance and rock to intimate singer-songwriter folk, often featuring both English and Dutch-language vocals. It reflects the Netherlands&#39; cosmopolitan culture while maintaining a characteristic directness and melodic accessibility.
