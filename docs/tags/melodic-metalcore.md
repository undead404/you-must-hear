## melodic metalcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T14:14:37.964Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 16:14:37 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Melodic metalcore is a subgenre of metalcore that blends elements of melodic death metal, metalcore, and traditional heavy metal. The style is characterized by aggressive, heavy guitar riffs, double bass drumming, breakdowns, and melodic choruses. The vocal style often includes both harsh screaming and clean singing, with lyrics that often deal with personal struggles, relationships, and emotional turmoil. Some notable bands in this genre include Killswitch Engage, As I Lay Dying, and Trivium.
