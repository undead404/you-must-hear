## deep house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-12T02:00:13.756Z">Wed Nov 12 2025 04:00:13 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Deep house is a subgenre of house music that originated in the 1980s in the United States, particularly in Chicago. It is characterized by its use of a 4&#x2F;4 beat, intricate melodies, and soulful vocals or instrumentals. The term &quot;deep&quot; refers to the emotional and soulful quality of the music, as well as the use of complex chord progressions and musical arrangements. Deep house is often associated with clubs and nightlife, but it can also be enjoyed as a more introspective and meditative form of electronic music.
