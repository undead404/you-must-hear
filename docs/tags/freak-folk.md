## freak folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-10T23:05:45.451Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 01:05:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Freak folk is an experimental indie genre that emerged in the 2000s, blending traditional folk with avant-garde and psychedelic elements. It features unconventional vocals, eclectic instrumentation, and lo-fi production that challenges folk conventions. The genre embraces whimsy, surrealism, and DIY aesthetics while maintaining organic, acoustic foundations.
