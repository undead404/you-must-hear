## soulful house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-07T09:29:38.976Z">Fri Nov 07 2025 11:29:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Soulful House music is a subgenre of House music that is enriched with elements of soul and jazz. It has a vibrant underground scene, with hotspots in cities like Chicago, New York, London, Brazil, Melbourne, and South Africa. The music often relies heavily on black female vocals or disco samples and has a recognizable tiered structure in which every track has more than one build-up.
