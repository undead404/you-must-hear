## tango

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T00:58:45.964Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 02:58:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Tango is a passionate music and dance genre that originated in late 19th-century Buenos Aires, blending African, European, and indigenous influences. The sound features dramatic bandoneón accordion melodies, violin, and piano creating an intimate yet melancholic atmosphere. Traditional tango emphasizes yearning vocals and syncopated rhythms, while modern interpretations incorporate electronic elements.
