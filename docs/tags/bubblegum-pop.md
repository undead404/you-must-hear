## bubblegum pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-24T01:09:38.096Z">Mon Nov 24 2025 03:09:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Bubblegum pop is a cheerful, catchy style of pop music characterized by simple melodies, upbeat rhythms, and lyrics about young love and fun. Emerging in the late 1960s and evolving through K-pop and teen pop, it targets youth audiences with its infectious, easy-to-remember hooks. The genre prioritizes accessibility and commercial appeal over musical complexity.
