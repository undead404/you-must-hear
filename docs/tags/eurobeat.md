## eurobeat

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T11:57:59.019Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 13:57:59 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Eurobeat is a high-energy electronic dance genre characterized by driving four-on-the-floor beats, soaring synth melodies, and fast tempos typically ranging from 140-160 BPM. Originating in Italy during the late 1980s, it became synonymous with Japanese para para dance culture and gained iconic status through its association with street racing and anime. The style features uplifting vocal hooks, relentless rhythmic intensity, and an unmistakably euphoric, adrenaline-fueled atmosphere.
