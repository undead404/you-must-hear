## garage

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T07:12:49.268Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 09:12:49 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Garage is a raw, energetic genre rooted in lo-fi rock and punk aesthetics, characterized by distorted guitars, driving rhythms, and DIY production values. It emerged from underground music scenes celebrating authenticity over polish, often featuring aggressive vocals and stripped-down instrumentation. The genre embodies a rebellious, unrefined spirit that prioritizes visceral energy and attitude over technical perfection.
