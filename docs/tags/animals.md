### Description

&quot;Animals&quot; encompasses indie and electronic music that draws inspiration from nature, wildlife, and pastoral themes. The genre spans lo-fi bedroom pop, ambient soundscapes, and dance music unified by organic imagery and environmental consciousness. Artists blend electronic production with acoustic elements to create both introspective and energetic sonic landscapes.
