## contemporary jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T10:16:19.871Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 12:16:19 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Contemporary jazz is a modern evolution of jazz that blends traditional improvisation with influences from electronic music, rock, and classical minimalism. Emerging in the late 20th century, it often features atmospheric soundscapes, intricate rhythms, and accessible melodies that appeal beyond traditional jazz audiences. The genre emphasizes group interplay and sonic experimentation while maintaining jazz&#39;s core spirit of spontaneity and musical conversation.
