## east coast rap

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T22:01:13.612Z">Sun Nov 09 2025 00:01:13 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

East Coast rap, also known as &quot;New York hip hop&quot;, is a style of hip hop music that originated in the early 1970s in the Bronx, New York City. It is characterized by its boom bap beats, aggressive delivery, and emphasis on lyrical skill and storytelling. East Coast rap is often associated with social and political commentary, and has been influenced by jazz, funk, and soul music. Notable East Coast rap artists include The Notorious B.I.G., Nas, Jay-Z, Wu-Tang Clan, and Public Enemy.
