## dark electro

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T02:44:22.649Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 04:44:22 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dark electro is an aggressive electronic music genre that emerged from EBM and industrial scenes in the 1990s, characterized by distorted vocals, harsh synthesizers, and pounding beats. The sound evokes themes of darkness, dystopia, and transgression. It&#39;s popular in gothic and industrial club cultures.
