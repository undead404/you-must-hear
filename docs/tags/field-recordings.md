## field recordings

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-24T14:46:11.277Z">Mon Nov 24 2025 16:46:11 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Field recordings capture environmental sounds from the real world—nature, urban spaces, conversations—and incorporate them into musical compositions or present them as standalone sonic documents. This practice bridges documentary art and experimental music, transforming everyday acoustic environments into immersive listening experiences that challenge traditional notions of what constitutes music. The genre emphasizes place, atmosphere, and the poetic qualities found in unscripted sound.
