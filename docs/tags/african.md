## african

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T05:18:45.974Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 07:18:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

African music encompasses the diverse traditional and contemporary sounds from across the African continent, characterized by complex polyrhythms, call-and-response vocals, and percussion-driven arrangements. The genre blends indigenous instruments like the kora and djembe with modern elements, reflecting both ancestral traditions and urban innovation. It serves as a vital expression of cultural identity, storytelling, and social commentary throughout African communities.
