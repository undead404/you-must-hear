## easycore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T07:51:30.372Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 09:51:30 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Easycore blends pop punk&#39;s catchy melodies and upbeat energy with metalcore&#39;s heavy breakdowns and aggressive guitar work. Emerging in the mid-2000s, it appeals to fans seeking accessible yet intense music that balances singalong choruses with mosh-worthy heaviness. The genre thrives in the Warped Tour scene, emphasizing fun and positivity alongside its harder edge.
