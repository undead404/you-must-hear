## contemporary christian

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:50:08.518Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:50:08 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Contemporary Christian is a modern genre blending pop, rock, and worship music with explicitly Christian lyrics and themes. It emerged in the 1960s as a way to express faith through accessible, radio-friendly sounds rather than traditional hymns. The music serves both devotional and entertainment purposes within Christian communities.
