## new rave

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T03:36:07.660Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 05:36:07 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

New rave is a music genre that emerged in the early to mid-2000s in the United Kingdom. It is a fusion of elements of various genres, including indie rock, electronica, post-punk revival, and dance-punk. The term &quot;new rave&quot; was coined by the British music press to describe the music of bands like Klaxons, Shitdisco, and CSS, who combined the danceable beats of electronic music with the energy and guitar-driven sound of indie rock. New rave is characterized by its use of neon colors, glow sticks, and other elements of rave culture, as well as its emphasis on partying and hedonism. The genre enjoyed a brief period of popularity in the mid-2000s, but has since faded from the mainstream.
