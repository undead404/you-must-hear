## native american

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-05T01:50:51.212Z">Wed Nov 05 2025 03:50:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Music incorporating Indigenous peoples&#39; traditional sounds, instruments, and spiritual practices from tribes across North America. Features distinctive elements like flute melodies, ceremonial drumming, chanting, and storytelling that reflect deep connections to land and ancestry. Ranges from authentic ceremonial recordings to contemporary fusion blending native traditions with modern genres.
