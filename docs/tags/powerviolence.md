## powerviolence

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T06:02:32.280Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 08:02:32 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Powerviolence is an extreme hardcore punk subgenre that emerged in the 1990s, characterized by blistering speed, chaotic tempo shifts, and brutally short songs. The music combines grindcore&#39;s intensity with hardcore punk&#39;s raw aggression, often incorporating sudden breakdowns and blast beats. Born from DIY underground scenes, it channels political rage and social frustration through its unrelenting sonic assault.
