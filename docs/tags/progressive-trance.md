## progressive trance

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:41:54.326Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:41:54 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Progressive trance is an electronic dance music style that emerged in the 1990s, blending hypnotic rhythms with gradual buildups and euphoric melodic peaks. It features layered synthesizers, driving basslines, and extended track lengths that create immersive journeys on the dancefloor. The genre balances accessible melodies with sophisticated production, making it popular in clubs and festivals worldwide.
