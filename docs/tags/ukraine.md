### Description

Music tagged &quot;ukraine&quot; represents the diverse sonic landscape of Ukrainian artists, blending Slavic folk traditions with contemporary rock, electronic, and indie pop influences. The sound often features Ukrainian or Russian language vocals with melancholic melodies that reflect the region&#39;s complex cultural identity and emotional depth. From anthemic rock to introspective indie, this tag captures Ukraine&#39;s vibrant modern music scene rooted in Eastern European sensibilities.
