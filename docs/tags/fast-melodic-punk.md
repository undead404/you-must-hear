## fast melodic punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T01:53:11.748Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 03:53:11 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Fast melodic punk, also known as melodic hardcore punk, is a subgenre of punk rock that emphasizes fast tempos, heavy distortion, and melodic and often complex guitar riffs. The genre emerged in the 1980s and is characterized by its blend of punk energy with elements of heavy metal and rock music. Melodic punk bands often incorporate harmonies and intricate guitar work, while retaining the political and socially conscious lyrics of punk rock. Some notable bands in the genre include Bad Religion, NOFX, Descendents, and Pennywise.
