### Description

The &quot;hollywood&quot; tag encompasses music with a glamorous, cinematic quality that evokes the golden age of entertainment and modern pop spectacle. It includes everything from classic crooners and show tunes to contemporary pop anthems that capture the glitz, drama, and larger-than-life appeal of Hollywood culture. The sound often features polished production, theatrical vocals, and an aspirational energy that celebrates stardom and escapism.
