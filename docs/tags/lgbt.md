## lgbt

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T10:51:22.339Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 12:51:22 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

&quot;LGBT music&quot; can refer to music created by and&#x2F;or for individuals who identify as lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, or any other non-heterosexual or non-cisgender identity. It can also refer to music that explores themes and experiences related to the LGBTQ+ community, such as coming out, queer love and relationships, activism, and discrimination. There are many genres of music that incorporate LGBTQ+ themes and identities, including pop, rock, electronic, hip-hop, and more. Some notable LGBTQ+ musicians and artists include Elton John, Freddie Mercury, Lady Gaga, Frank Ocean, and Janelle Monáe, among many others.
