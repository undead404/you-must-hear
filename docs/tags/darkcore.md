## darkcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T15:20:13.721Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 17:20:13 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Darkcore is a subgenre of hardcore techno music that emerged in the early 1990s. It is characterized by dark and aggressive soundscapes, heavy use of distortion and noise, and fast, intense beats. The term &quot;darkcore&quot; was coined to describe the darker and more menacing elements of early hardcore techno, which often featured ominous samples and atmospheric effects that evoked a sense of horror or dread. Darkcore has influenced a number of other electronic music genres, including drum and bass, dubstep, and industrial techno.
