## glasgow

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:56:23.852Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:56:23 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

The Glasgow tag captures the distinctive indie and alternative music scene emerging from Scotland&#39;s largest city, characterized by jangly guitars, melancholic introspection, and raw emotional honesty. Drawing from post-punk and indie pop traditions, Glasgow bands often blend dreamy melodies with gritty realism, reflecting the city&#39;s industrial heritage and working-class roots. The sound ranges from noise-pop experimentation to intimate, confessional songwriting, unified by an unpretentious DIY ethos and darkly poetic sensibility.
