## italian folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T21:07:41.882Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 23:07:41 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Italian folk music is a diverse collection of musical styles that have evolved across the Italian peninsula, reflecting Italy’s geographic position and its many separate cultures. The music embodies the spirit and history of its people, from the vibrant tarantella dances of Southern Italy to the soothing melodies of Northern lullabies. It presents a mix of influences, including Celtic and Slavic in the North, Greek, Arabic, and African in the South, and a combination of these elements in Central Italy.
