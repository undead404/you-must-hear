## chillout

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T01:10:42.328Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 03:10:42 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Chillout music is a subgenre of electronic music that is characterized by its mellow, relaxing, and laid-back vibe. The term &quot;chillout&quot; originally referred to the ambient music played in chillout rooms in nightclubs, where people could take a break from dancing and unwind in a more relaxed atmosphere. Chillout music often features slow, atmospheric beats, smooth melodies, and calming sounds, such as ocean waves, bird songs, or wind chimes. It is often used as background music for relaxation, meditation, or as an ambient soundtrack for various activities such as reading, studying, or yoga.
