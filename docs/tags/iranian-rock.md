### Description

Iranian rock music, also known as Persian rock, is a genre of rock music that emerged in Iran in the 1960s and 1970s. It was heavily influenced by Western rock and pop music, as well as Iranian traditional music. Iranian rock music has gone through many changes and developments over the years, and it continues to be a popular genre in Iran today. Some of the most notable Iranian rock musicians include Kourosh Yaghmaei, Googoosh, Farhad Mehrad, and Shahram Shabpareh.
