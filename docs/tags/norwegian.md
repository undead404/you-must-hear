### Description

Norwegian music refers to the music of Norway or by Norwegian artists. It includes a wide range of genres and styles, from traditional folk music to modern pop, rock, metal, and electronic music. Norwegian music has a long and rich history, dating back to Viking times when music was used in religious and cultural ceremonies. In the modern era, Norway has produced many notable musicians and bands, including Edvard Grieg, A-ha, Kygo, Sigrid, and Aurora. Norwegian music is known for its unique blend of traditional elements and modern sounds, and for its innovative and experimental approach to music-making.
