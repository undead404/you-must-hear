## contemporary classical

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-19T20:36:51.958Z">Wed Nov 19 2025 22:36:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Contemporary classical music is a broad term that refers to music that is composed in the classical tradition after the modernist period, which ended around the mid-20th century. It encompasses a wide range of styles and approaches, from works that are highly experimental and avant-garde to those that are more traditional and accessible. Contemporary classical music may be performed by a variety of instrumental ensembles, from solo piano to large orchestras, and often incorporates elements of electronic music and other genres. It is often associated with composers who seek to push the boundaries of traditional classical music and explore new sounds and forms. Some notable contemporary classical composers include Steve Reich, Philip Glass, John Adams, Arvo Pärt, and Kaija Saariaho, among many others.
