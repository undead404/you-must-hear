## space rock revival

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T07:11:35.735Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 09:11:35 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Space rock revival emerged in the late 1980s and 1990s, reimagining psychedelic rock&#39;s cosmic explorations through shoegazing textures, krautrock repetition, and ambient soundscapes. The genre combines droning guitars, hypnotic rhythms, and ethereal vocals to evoke interstellar vastness and introspective transcendence. Its lo-fi aesthetic and experimental approach influenced both underground indie and mainstream alternative scenes.
