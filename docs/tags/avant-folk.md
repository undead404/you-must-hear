## avant-folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T00:20:00.707Z">Fri Nov 14 2025 02:20:00 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Avant-folk blends traditional folk instrumentation with experimental techniques, creating unsettling yet intimate soundscapes. Emerging from 1960s counterculture and evolving through indie scenes, it challenges folk&#39;s conventions with dissonance, unconventional structures, and raw emotional intensity. The genre attracts artists exploring vulnerability through sonic experimentation.
