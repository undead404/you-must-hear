## noisegrind

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-09T05:34:56.609Z">Tue Dec 09 2025 07:34:56 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Noisegrind is a subgenre of extreme music that combines elements of noise music and grindcore. It typically features aggressive and fast-paced drumming, heavily distorted guitar riffs, and screamed or growled vocals, often with the addition of noise elements such as feedback, samples, and sound effects. Noisegrind bands often focus on creating a chaotic and abrasive sound, with little regard for traditional song structures or melodic content. The genre has its roots in the 1980s noise music scene and has since evolved into a distinct subgenre of extreme music.
