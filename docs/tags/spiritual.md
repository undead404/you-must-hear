## spiritual

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T14:55:17.232Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 16:55:17 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Spiritual music blends sacred themes with diverse sounds from gospel, soul, reggae, and hip-hop, creating reflective atmospheres that explore faith and consciousness. Rooted in African American religious traditions, it has evolved to encompass global spiritual expressions across cultures. The genre emphasizes introspective lyrics, meditative rhythms, and uplifting melodies that connect listeners to higher meaning.
