## sampling

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T10:36:39.106Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 12:36:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Sampling music refers to the practice of taking a portion or &quot;sample&quot; of an existing sound recording and using it as a building block for a new composition. The sample can be taken from any source, such as a piece of music, a sound effect, or a spoken word recording. Once a sample has been isolated, it can be manipulated in various ways, such as altering its pitch or tempo, adding effects, or layering it with other sounds. Sampling has been a popular technique in many genres of music since the 1980s, particularly in hip hop, electronic music, and pop music.
