## slide guitar

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T08:13:20.952Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 10:13:20 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Slide guitar is a distinctive playing technique where a smooth object—typically a glass or metal tube—glides across guitar strings to create seamless, vocal-like tones and expressive pitch bends. Rooted in blues and country traditions, the style produces a weeping, ethereal quality that evokes both raw emotion and melodic fluidity. The technique spans from gritty Delta blues to contemporary rock and Americana, adding soulful texture wherever it appears.
