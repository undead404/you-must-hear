## rumba

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T20:47:21.950Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 22:47:21 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rumba is a vibrant Afro-Cuban rhythm characterized by sensual, syncopated beats and percussion-driven melodies that inspire intimate dance. Rooted in Cuban street culture, it blends African drumming traditions with Spanish guitar influences, creating an infectious, romantic sound. Modern interpretations range from traditional flamenco-rumba fusion to experimental electronic versions that maintain the genre&#39;s hypnotic, swaying groove.
