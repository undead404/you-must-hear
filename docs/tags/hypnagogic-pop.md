## hypnagogic pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T22:29:37.919Z">Sun Nov 09 2025 00:29:37 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hypnagogic pop is a lo-fi genre that evokes hazy nostalgia through warped samples, degraded synths, and murky production reminiscent of forgotten 1980s media. The music captures dreamlike states between sleep and waking, blending pop melodies with unsettling distortion. It emerged in the 2000s as artists explored memory and retro aesthetics through intentionally crude recording techniques.
