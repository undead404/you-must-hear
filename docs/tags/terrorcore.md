## terrorcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T13:53:27.992Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 15:53:27 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Terrorcore is a subgenre of hardcore techno that emerged in the early 1990s, originating in Rotterdam where gabber was created. It is characterized by its aggressive and fast-paced beats, distorted synthesizers, and heavy use of samples from horror films and other sources. The lyrics often deal with themes of violence, terrorism, and the occult.
