### Description

Boston represents the distinctive musical output of Massachusetts&#39; largest city, spanning from arena rock bombast to hardcore punk&#39;s aggressive minimalism and underground indie experimentation. The tag captures a working-class grit and DIY ethos that permeates across genres, reflecting the city&#39;s neighborhoods and youth culture. It encompasses both polished mainstream success and raw underground sounds unified by regional identity.
