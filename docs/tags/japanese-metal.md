## japanese metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T16:34:23.533Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 18:34:23 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Japanese metal, or heavy metal music from Japan, emerged in the mid-to-late 1970s. It is characterized by fast-paced guitar riffs, powerful drumming, and aggressive vocals, often incorporating elements of traditional Japanese music. The lyrics frequently explore themes of Japanese mythology, history, and culture, and the genre has evolved over time, with a significant boom of all-female metal bands in the 2010s.
