## potpourri

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T10:05:25.607Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 12:05:25 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

In music, a potpourri (or pot-pourri) is a collection or medley of different tunes or musical themes, often performed one after another. It can include a mix of songs, melodies, or musical styles from various composers or eras, arranged in a specific order to create a cohesive whole. Potpourris are often used in musical theater, opera, and some forms of popular music, and can provide an entertaining and varied listening experience for the audience.
