### Description

Portuguese music encompasses the melodic traditions and contemporary sounds originating from Portugal, characterized by emotive vocals, poetic lyricism, and influences ranging from fado&#39;s melancholic soul to modern pop-rock sensibilities. The tag captures both traditional Portuguese musical heritage and newer waves of alternative and indie expressions that maintain the language&#39;s rhythmic flow. This music often balances introspective storytelling with atmospheric instrumentation, reflecting Portugal&#39;s cultural identity through diverse sonic landscapes.
