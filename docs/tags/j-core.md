## j-core

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T11:06:56.437Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 13:06:56 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

J-core (Japanese core) is a subgenre of electronic dance music that originated in Japan. It is characterized by fast beats, heavy use of synthesizers, and a fusion of different electronic dance music genres such as hardcore techno, happy hardcore, and trance. J-core often features high-pitched vocals, sound effects from popular anime or video games, and samples from Japanese pop culture. The genre gained popularity in the 2000s and has a dedicated fanbase in Japan and around the world.
