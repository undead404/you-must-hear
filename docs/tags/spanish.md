### Description

Spanish music refers to the musical traditions and styles that have developed in Spain or have been influenced by Spanish culture. There is a wide range of genres within Spanish music, including traditional folk music, classical music, flamenco, pop, rock, electronic music, and more. Spain has a rich musical history and has produced many influential musicians and composers, such as Manuel de Falla, Isaac Albéniz, Joaquín Rodrigo, Paco de Lucía, Camarón de la Isla, and many more.
