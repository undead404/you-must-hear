### Description

Argentina&#39;s music scene blends tango traditions with rock nacional, folk, and electronic influences. The genre reflects diverse regional sounds from Buenos Aires&#39; urban rock to Andean folk rhythms. It captures both melancholic storytelling and energetic experimentation.
