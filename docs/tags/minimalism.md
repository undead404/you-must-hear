## minimalism

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T09:36:58.072Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 11:36:58 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Minimalism is a style or technique of music characterized by a limited number of musical elements, often consisting of simple rhythms, harmonies, and melodies that are repeated and gradually developed over time. The genre emerged in the mid-20th century and is associated with composers such as Philip Glass, Steve Reich, and Terry Riley, among others. Minimalist music often features repetitive patterns, drones, and sustained tones, and is frequently associated with the visual arts and experimental film. The style has influenced a wide range of contemporary genres, including electronic music, ambient music, and post-rock.
