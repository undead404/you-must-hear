## arabic

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T12:28:21.952Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 14:28:21 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Arabic music encompasses the diverse musical traditions of the Arab world, characterized by microtonal scales, intricate melodic ornamentation, and quarter-tone intervals. The genre blends classical forms with contemporary pop, featuring instruments like the oud, qanun, and darbuka alongside modern production. It reflects centuries of cultural exchange across North Africa and the Middle East, emphasizing emotional vocal delivery and complex rhythmic patterns.
