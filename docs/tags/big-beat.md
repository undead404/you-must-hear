## big beat

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-17T05:49:59.461Z">Mon Nov 17 2025 07:49:59 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Big beat is a music genre that emerged in the 1990s and was popularized by artists such as The Chemical Brothers, Fatboy Slim, The Crystal Method, and Propellerheads. It is characterized by its heavy use of sampled breakbeats, electronic and rock sounds, and high-energy tempos, often combined with distorted basslines, synthesizers, and vocal samples. Big beat draws influence from a variety of musical genres, including hip hop, rock, techno, and funk, and is often associated with the electronic dance music scene.
