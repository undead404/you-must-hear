## indie punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:39:29.826Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:39:29 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Indie punk merges the raw energy and DIY ethos of punk rock with the melodic sensibility and introspective songwriting of indie rock. Emerging from basement shows and independent labels, it favors emotional honesty and personal storytelling over polished production. The sound balances driving guitars and urgent tempos with vulnerable lyrics about everyday struggles and growing up.
