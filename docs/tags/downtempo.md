## downtempo

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T14:30:59.906Z">Sat Nov 08 2025 16:30:59 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Downtempo music is a genre of electronic music that is characterized by its relaxed and chilled-out tempo, usually ranging between 60 and 90 beats per minute. It is often used as background music, as it is known for its mellow and atmospheric soundscapes that create a calming and introspective atmosphere. Downtempo music often features elements of trip-hop, ambient, and chillout music, and it can incorporate a variety of instruments, including acoustic and electronic sounds. The genre has its roots in the early 1990s, and it continues to evolve and incorporate new sounds and styles today.
