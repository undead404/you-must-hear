## acid techno

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-18T20:39:53.305Z">Sat Oct 18 2025 23:39:53 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Acid techno is characterized by the squelching, hypnotic sounds of the Roland TB-303 bass synthesizer, creating raw and psychedelic textures over driving four-on-the-floor beats. Emerging from late 1980s rave culture, it emphasizes repetitive grooves and mind-bending modulations that induce trance-like states on the dancefloor. The style ranges from underground warehouse intensity to experimental electronic exploration.
