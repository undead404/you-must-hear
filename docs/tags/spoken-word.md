## spoken word

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:24:45.193Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:24:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Spoken word blends poetry and music, featuring vocal delivery that prioritizes rhythm and narrative over traditional singing. Emerging from beat poetry and experimental rock, it often incorporates atmospheric instrumentation or avant-garde soundscapes. The genre emphasizes raw expression and social commentary through spoken verses.
