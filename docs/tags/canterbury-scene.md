## canterbury scene

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T13:55:14.980Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 15:55:14 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

The Canterbury Scene emerged in late 1960s England as a loose collective of progressive musicians who blended jazz improvisation, psychedelic experimentation, and whimsical British eccentricity. These artists favored complex time signatures and instrumental virtuosity while maintaining a playful, anti-establishment spirit that distinguished them from more pompous prog rock. The sound emphasizes organ-driven melodies, intricate interplay between musicians, and a distinctly pastoral yet avant-garde sensibility.
