## electropunk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T04:59:44.551Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 06:59:44 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Electropunk fuses the raw energy and rebellious attitude of punk rock with electronic instruments like synthesizers, drum machines, and digital effects. Emerging from late 1970s underground scenes, it strips away traditional guitar-driven sounds in favor of aggressive, confrontational electronic textures. The genre emphasizes DIY ethics, chaotic performances, and an abrasive sonic aesthetic that challenges conventional rock music.
