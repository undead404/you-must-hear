## russian emo

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:43:32.782Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:43:32 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Russian Emo music, which emerged in the early 2000s, is a fusion of emo and elements of Russian music. It is characterized by emotional and introspective lyrics, often addressing themes of love, heartbreak, and mental health. The music typically features slow, melodic tunes with heavy use of autotune and distorted vocals.
