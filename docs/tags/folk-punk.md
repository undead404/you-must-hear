## folk punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T07:23:57.145Z">Fri Nov 14 2025 09:23:57 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Folk punk is a subgenre of punk rock that combines the elements of folk music, such as acoustic instruments and storytelling lyrics, with the DIY and anti-authoritarian ethos of punk. Folk punk artists often play acoustic guitars, banjos, mandolins, or other traditional instruments, and incorporate elements of traditional folk music, such as ballads, protest songs, and working-class themes, into their music. The genre emerged in the 1980s and 1990s, and has since developed a diverse and vibrant underground scene, with a range of artists and bands blending punk and folk influences in unique and creative ways.
