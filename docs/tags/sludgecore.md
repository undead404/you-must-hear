## sludgecore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T10:12:41.163Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 12:12:41 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Sludgecore fuses the crushing slowness of sludge metal with hardcore punk&#39;s aggressive intensity, creating an abrasive sound marked by downtuned guitars, guttural vocals, and oppressive atmosphere. The style emerged from Southern US underground scenes, channeling bleakness and nihilism through punishing riffs and feedback-drenched production. It emphasizes raw emotional catharsis over technical precision.
