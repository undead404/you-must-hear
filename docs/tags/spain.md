### Description

Music tagged as &quot;spain&quot; encompasses diverse sounds rooted in Spanish culture, from flamenco&#39;s passionate guitar and palmas to urban Latin trap and indie rock. Expect rhythmic intensity, melodic expressiveness, and a distinct Mediterranean sensibility across multiple genres.
