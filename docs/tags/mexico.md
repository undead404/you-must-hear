### Description

&quot;Mexico&quot; encompasses the diverse popular music traditions of Mexican culture, blending indigenous sounds with Spanish influences and modern styles. The genre spans romantic ballads, rock en español, cumbia, and orchestral pop that dominated Latin American charts. It represents Mexico&#39;s rich musical heritage through passionate vocals, traditional rhythms, and cross-generational appeal.
