## street punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:59:17.862Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:59:17 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Street punk is a raw, aggressive form of punk rock that emerged from working-class urban communities in the late 1970s and early 1980s. The music features fast, stripped-down guitar riffs, shouted vocals, and anthemic choruses focused on social rebellion and street life. It emphasizes authenticity, DIY ethics, and a confrontational attitude that rejects mainstream commercialism.
