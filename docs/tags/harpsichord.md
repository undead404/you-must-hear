## harpsichord

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-12T18:01:00.982Z">Wed Nov 12 2025 20:01:00 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

The harpsichord tag identifies music featuring this distinctive plucked keyboard instrument with its bright, percussive tone that dominated European art music from the Renaissance through the Baroque era. Its characteristic metallic shimmer and inability to vary volume through touch creates an ornate, crystalline sound world associated with courtly elegance and contrapuntal complexity. Though primarily linked to historical classical music, the harpsichord occasionally appears in contemporary experimental and avant-garde contexts.
