## no wave

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T10:16:42.936Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 12:16:42 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

No wave was an abrasive, experimental music movement that emerged in late 1970s New York City as a reaction against punk&#39;s growing commercialization. The genre featured harsh dissonance, atonal guitars, unconventional song structures, and an intentionally confrontational aesthetic that rejected traditional musicianship. It blended punk energy with avant-garde art sensibilities, creating deliberately challenging soundscapes that prioritized raw expression over accessibility.
