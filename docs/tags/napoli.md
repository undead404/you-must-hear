## napoli

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T07:57:42.459Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 09:57:42 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Napoli music, also known as Neapolitan music, refers to the folk music of the city of Naples and the surrounding Campania region in southern Italy. It is characterized by its lively and romantic melodies, sentimental lyrics, and the use of traditional instruments such as the mandolin, guitar, and accordion. Napoli music has a long and rich history, dating back to the 16th century when the Spanish ruled Naples. Over time, it has evolved to include influences from other musical styles, including opera, popular music, and jazz. Famous Napoli musicians include Enrico Caruso, Renato Carosone, and Roberto Murolo.
