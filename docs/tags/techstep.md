## techstep

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T19:29:00.348Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 21:29:00 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Techstep is a subgenre of drum and bass that emerged in the mid-1990s, characterized by a dark, sci-fi mood. It primarily uses synthesized or sampled sound sources, 2-step kicks and snares, and draws influences from industrial and techno music. The genre is known for its “clinical” sound, with a focus on creating high-energy collages from abstract, synthetic noises, including samples, bleeps, and squelches.
