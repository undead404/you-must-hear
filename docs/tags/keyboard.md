## keyboard

### Description

The &quot;keyboard&quot; tag encompasses music where piano, synthesizer, organ, or electronic keyboards take a prominent melodic or textural role, spanning classical harpsichord works to modern synth-driven compositions. This broad category unites diverse styles through the common thread of keyboard virtuosity and timbre, from jazz fusion&#39;s Rhodes electric piano to progressive rock&#39;s layered synthesizers. The tag celebrates the keyboard&#39;s versatility as both a solo instrument and ensemble centerpiece across centuries and genres.
