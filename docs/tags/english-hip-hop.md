## english hip hop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:59:29.087Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:59:29 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

English hip hop is a UK-based genre blending American rap influences with British urban culture, grime, and garage music. It features distinct London accents, slang, and references to British life over diverse beats. The sound ranges from hard-hitting grime to melodic trap-influenced styles.
