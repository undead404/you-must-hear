## disco

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T19:49:08.593Z">Sat Nov 08 2025 21:49:08 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Disco is a genre of dance music that originated in the United States in the 1970s, characterized by a four-on-the-floor beat and a strong emphasis on rhythm and groove. It typically features lush orchestral arrangements, catchy melodies, and lyrics about love, dancing, and partying. The disco era was popularized by artists such as Donna Summer, Bee Gees, Chic, Gloria Gaynor, and Earth, Wind &amp; Fire. The genre&#39;s popularity peaked in the late 1970s and early 1980s, but it has continued to influence dance music to this day.
