## schlager

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-16T14:32:28.271Z">Sun Nov 16 2025 16:32:28 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Schlager is a popular European music genre, primarily from Germany and Austria, featuring catchy melodies, simple lyrics about love and life, and an upbeat, easy-listening sound. It emerged in the post-war era and remains beloved at festivals and celebrations. The style emphasizes sing-along choruses and accessible, feel-good entertainment.
