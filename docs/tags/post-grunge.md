## post-grunge

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-31T21:24:51.222Z">Fri Oct 31 2025 23:24:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Post-grunge is a subgenre of alternative rock music that emerged in the mid-1990s as a reaction to the popularity of grunge music. Post-grunge incorporates elements of grunge, such as distorted guitars and heavy, emotional lyrics, but often has a more polished sound and less overtly angst-filled lyrics. Post-grunge bands often focus on radio-friendly melodies and catchy hooks, and are characterized by a more mainstream sound than their grunge predecessors. Some of the most well-known post-grunge bands include Creed, Nickelback, and Three Days Grace.
