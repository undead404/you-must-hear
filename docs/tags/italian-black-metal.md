## italian black metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:54:10.376Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:54:10 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Italian black metal is a subgenre of black metal music that originated in Italy in the 1990s. It shares many of the same characteristics as traditional black metal, such as fast tempos, shrieked vocals, and often abrasive and dissonant guitar riffs. However, Italian black metal often incorporates elements of traditional Italian music, as well as more melodic and atmospheric elements than some other subgenres of black metal. Italian black metal bands include groups such as Mortuary Drape, Opera IX, and Abhor.
