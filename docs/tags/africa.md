## africa

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:37:37.148Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:37:37 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

African music encompasses the diverse musical traditions of the continent, blending indigenous rhythms, call-and-response vocals, and instruments like the kora and djembe. It reflects rich cultural heritage through polyrhythmic percussion, melodic storytelling, and communal expression. The genre ranges from traditional folk to contemporary fusions with Western styles.
