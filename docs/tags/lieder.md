## lieder

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-27T18:40:05.654Z">Mon Oct 27 2025 20:40:05 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Lieder refers to German art songs from the Romantic era, typically featuring a solo voice accompanied by piano, setting poetry to intimate, expressive music. These compositions blend classical sophistication with emotional depth, exploring themes of love, nature, and longing through the marriage of text and melody. The tradition emphasizes nuanced interpretation and the delicate balance between singer and pianist as equal artistic partners.
