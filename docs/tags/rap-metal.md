## rap metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T15:58:25.765Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 17:58:25 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rap metal fuses aggressive hip-hop vocals and rapping with heavy metal guitar riffs and rock instrumentation. Emerging in the late 1980s and peaking in the 1990s, it bridged urban and rock cultures during an era of genre experimentation. The style emphasizes rebellion, intensity, and raw energy through distorted guitars paired with rhythmic vocal delivery.
