## spanish rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T14:04:46.140Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 16:04:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Spanish rock encompasses guitar-driven rock music sung primarily in Spanish, blending Anglo-American rock traditions with distinctly Iberian lyrical sensibilities and Mediterranean passion. The sound ranges from raw, blues-tinged hard rock to poetic alternative styles, often featuring gritty vocals and socially conscious lyrics that reflect Spanish cultural identity. It represents decades of Spanish-language artists carving their own path within rock music, creating a sound that honors global rock traditions while remaining unmistakably Spanish in spirit.
