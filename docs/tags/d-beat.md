## d-beat

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-21T03:34:48.643Z">Tue Oct 21 2025 06:34:48 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

D-beat is a raw, aggressive punk drumming style characterized by a relentless galloping rhythm that originated in early 1980s UK hardcore. Named after pioneering band Discharge, it features a distinctive drum pattern with rapid-fire snare and bass drum hits that create an intense, militaristic pulse. The sound became synonymous with anarcho-punk and crust punk movements, emphasizing anti-war themes and stripped-down sonic brutality.
