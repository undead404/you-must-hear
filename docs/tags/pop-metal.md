## pop metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-22T23:06:05.083Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 01:06:05 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Pop metal fuses the accessibility and melodic hooks of pop music with the heavy guitars, aggressive drums, and intensity of metal. The style emerged from hair metal&#39;s commercial success in the 1980s and has evolved to include modern electronic elements, creating an accessible entry point to heavier music. It prioritizes catchy choruses and radio-friendly structures while maintaining metal&#39;s powerful sonic energy.
