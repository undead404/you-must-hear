## polish rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-25T20:46:57.170Z">Tue Nov 25 2025 22:46:57 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Polish rock is a diverse genre that emerged in communist-era Poland, blending Western rock influences with Polish folk traditions and poetic, often politically charged lyrics. The sound ranges from post-punk and new wave to alternative rock and indie, unified by its use of the Polish language and cultural themes. It served as both artistic expression and social commentary, becoming a voice for Polish youth and national identity from the 1980s onward.
