## indian classical

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T14:50:48.852Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 16:50:48 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Indian classical music is an ancient artistic tradition from the Indian subcontinent built on intricate melodic frameworks called ragas and rhythmic cycles known as talas. Performances feature highly sophisticated improvisation within these structures, often using instruments like the sitar, tabla, sarod, and bansuri flute. The music emphasizes spiritual expression and emotional resonance, with each raga traditionally associated with specific times of day, seasons, and moods.
