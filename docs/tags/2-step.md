## 2-step

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:31:45.905Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:31:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

2-step is a rhythmic approach originating from UK garage that features syncopated, shuffling drum patterns with distinctive skip-like beats instead of straight four-on-the-floor kicks. The sound creates an infectious groove through off-kilter percussion and typically pairs with soulful vocals, warm basslines, and garage-influenced production. Originally emerging in late &#39;90s London club culture, it has recently experienced a global revival through bedroom pop and modern R&amp;B reinterpretations.
