## industrial rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T02:13:22.892Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 04:13:22 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Industrial rock is a subgenre of rock music that blends elements of industrial music and rock. It originated in the late 1980s and early 1990s, with bands such as Nine Inch Nails, Ministry, and KMFDM. Industrial rock typically features heavy, distorted guitar riffs, electronic instrumentation, and vocals that range from spoken word to aggressive screaming. The genre often incorporates samples, loops, and other audio manipulations to create a soundscape that is both abrasive and futuristic. Some industrial rock bands also incorporate elements of other genres, such as metal, punk, and techno.
