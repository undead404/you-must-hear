Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-12T22:39:58.591Z">Thu Nov 13 2025 00:39:58 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rapcore, also known as rap rock, is a music genre that combines elements of hip hop and rock music. It emerged in the mid-1980s and gained popularity in the 1990s with bands such as Rage Against the Machine, Beastie Boys, and Limp Bizkit. The music typically features rapped vocals, heavy guitar riffs, and a strong rhythm section. Rapcore often addresses social and political issues in its lyrics, and can be seen as a form of protest music. The genre continues to have a dedicated following, with bands such as Hollywood Undead and Linkin Park carrying on the tradition.
