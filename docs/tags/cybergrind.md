## cybergrind

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T04:23:06.137Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 06:23:06 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Cybergrind is an extreme fusion of grindcore&#39;s aggressive intensity with electronic elements, drum machines, and digital production techniques. Emerging from underground internet communities in the early 2000s, it combines blast beats, harsh vocals, and chaotic song structures with synthesizers, glitchy electronics, and industrial textures. The genre embraces a DIY ethos and often features abrupt tempo changes, making it intentionally disorienting and confrontational.
