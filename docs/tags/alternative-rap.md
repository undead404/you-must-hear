## alternative rap

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-24T23:59:37.954Z">Tue Nov 25 2025 01:59:37 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Alternative rap, also known as underground hip hop or indie hip hop, is a subgenre of hip hop music that emerged in the 1980s and 1990s as a response to the mainstream commercialization of hip hop. Alternative rap artists often focus on social and political issues and use more experimental and diverse musical elements, such as jazz, funk, and rock. They tend to have a more poetic and introspective lyrical style and often challenge traditional hip hop stereotypes and conventions. Some notable alternative rap artists include Aesop Rock, MF Doom, Atmosphere, and Common.
