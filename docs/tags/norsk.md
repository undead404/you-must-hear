## norsk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T12:09:47.631Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 14:09:47 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

&quot;Norsk music&quot; refers to music from Norway. Norway has a rich musical history that includes traditional folk music, classical music, and more contemporary genres like black metal, electronic music, and pop music. Some famous Norwegian musicians include composer Edvard Grieg, black metal band Mayhem, and pop artist Sigrid.
