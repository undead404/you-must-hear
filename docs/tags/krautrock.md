## krautrock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-07T18:59:01.713Z">Fri Nov 07 2025 20:59:01 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Krautrock is an experimental rock genre that emerged in West Germany during the late 1960s and 1970s, blending electronic music, psychedelia, and avant-garde influences. The style emphasizes repetitive rhythms, synthesizer textures, and improvisation while rejecting traditional rock structures. It represented a cultural break from Germany&#39;s past, creating a distinctly forward-looking sonic identity.
