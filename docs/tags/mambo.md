## mambo

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:48:02.443Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:48:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Mambo is an energetic Cuban dance music genre that emerged in the 1940s, blending Afro-Cuban rhythms with big band jazz influences. Characterized by syncopated brass sections, layered percussion, and a distinctive forward-driving beat, it became a ballroom sensation across Latin America and the United States. The genre&#39;s infectious, improvisational style established it as a foundation for modern salsa and Latin jazz.
