### Description

The &quot;international&quot; tag encompasses music rooted in specific cultural and regional traditions from around the world, often sung in non-English languages. It celebrates diverse musical heritage, from African rhythms and Latin grooves to Celtic folk and beyond, representing authentic expressions of local identity. This category highlights global sounds that retain their cultural distinctiveness while crossing borders to reach wider audiences.
