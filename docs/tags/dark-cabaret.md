## dark cabaret

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-13T21:50:22.764Z">Thu Nov 13 2025 23:50:22 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dark cabaret is a theatrical music genre that blends the dramatic flair of Weimar-era cabaret with gothic and punk sensibilities. The sound combines accordion, piano, and unconventional instrumentation with darkly whimsical lyrics exploring macabre themes. It emerged from underground alternative scenes, embracing vaudeville aesthetics and Victorian-inspired performance art.
