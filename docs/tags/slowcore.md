## slowcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T08:54:43.806Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 10:54:43 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Slowcore, also known as sadcore, is a subgenre of indie rock that emerged in the 1990s. The genre is characterized by slow tempos, minimalist arrangements, and introspective lyrics. Slowcore songs typically feature simple, repetitive guitar riffs, slow and deliberate drumming, and quiet vocals that often emphasize the melancholic nature of the lyrics. The lyrical themes of slowcore often explore feelings of loneliness, depression, and alienation, and the music is often associated with a sense of introspection and reflection. Notable slowcore bands include Red House Painters, Codeine, Low, and Galaxie 500.
