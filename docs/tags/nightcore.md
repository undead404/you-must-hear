## nightcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-03T00:44:51.625Z">Mon Nov 03 2025 02:44:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Nightcore is a sped-up remix style that increases the tempo and pitch of existing songs, creating a higher, more energetic sound. Originating from early 2000s internet culture, it became popular on platforms like YouTube among anime and gaming communities. The genre transforms tracks from various styles into fast-paced, chipmunk-voiced versions that emphasize intensity and nostalgia.
