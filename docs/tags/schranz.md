## schranz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T00:31:42.424Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 02:31:42 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Schranz is a style of techno music that originated in Germany in the mid-1990s. It is characterized by its hard and pounding beats, often featuring aggressive and distorted sounds. The name &quot;schranz&quot; comes from a German word meaning &quot;to scrape,&quot; which refers to the abrasive and industrial sound of the genre. Schranz often incorporates elements of hardcore techno and gabber, as well as influences from other electronic music genres like trance and acid. The tempo of schranz music is typically around 150-160 beats per minute, making it one of the faster and more intense forms of techno.
