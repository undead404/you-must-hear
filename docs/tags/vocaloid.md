## vocaloid

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-29T00:58:40.042Z">Wed Oct 29 2025 02:58:40 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Vocaloid is a genre built around singing synthesizer software, primarily from Japan, featuring digitally generated vocals with distinctive robotic-yet-expressive qualities. The music spans diverse styles from hyper-pop to rock, unified by its use of virtual singers like Hatsune Miku as the voice. Born from internet culture, it thrives through collaborative online communities where producers create songs for these digital personas.
