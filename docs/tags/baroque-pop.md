## baroque pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T01:25:15.603Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 03:25:15 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Baroque pop is a subgenre of pop music that emerged in the mid-1960s, characterized by a fusion of classical music elements and rock music. It typically features ornate arrangements, intricate melodies, and the use of instruments commonly found in classical music, such as harpsichords, cellos, and violins. The lyrics often have a poetic or literary quality, drawing on themes from literature, mythology, or history. Some of the most notable artists associated with baroque pop include The Beatles, The Beach Boys, The Left Banke, and Scott Walker.
