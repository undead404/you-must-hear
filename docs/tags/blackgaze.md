## blackgaze

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-17T23:07:16.742Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 01:07:16 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Blackgaze merges black metal&#39;s aggressive intensity with shoegaze&#39;s dreamy, atmospheric textures, creating a contrast between harsh vocals and lush soundscapes. The genre emerged in the 2000s from artists seeking to blend metal&#39;s raw emotion with ethereal, reverb-drenched guitars. It often explores themes of nature, melancholy, and transcendence through its distinctive wall-of-sound approach.
