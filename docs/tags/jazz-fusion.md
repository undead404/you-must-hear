## jazz fusion

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T04:52:32.937Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 06:52:32 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Jazz fusion is a genre of music that combines elements of jazz with other styles such as rock, funk, R&amp;B, and world music. It emerged in the late 1960s and early 1970s and is characterized by complex rhythms, virtuosic instrumental solos, and often experimental approaches to composition and improvisation. Some of the key musicians associated with jazz fusion include Miles Davis, Weather Report, Mahavishnu Orchestra, and Return to Forever. Jazz fusion has influenced a wide range of other musical genres and continues to be a vibrant and evolving form of music.
