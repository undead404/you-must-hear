### Description

The &quot;guitarist&quot; tag highlights music where the guitar takes center stage as the primary voice and creative force, spanning rock, jazz, folk, and blues traditions. These works showcase technical mastery, distinctive tone, and personal expression through the instrument, whether via virtuosic solos, innovative techniques, or signature playing styles. The tag represents a musician-focused approach where the guitarist&#39;s individual artistry defines the sound.
