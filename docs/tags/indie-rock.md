## indie rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T17:07:45.086Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 19:07:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Indie rock emerged in the 1980s as guitar-driven music produced outside major record labels, emphasizing creative independence over commercial appeal. The genre blends raw, melodic rock with diverse influences from punk to folk, often featuring introspective lyrics and unconventional song structures. Its DIY ethos and artistic authenticity helped shape alternative music culture from underground scenes to mainstream recognition.
