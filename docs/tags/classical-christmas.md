## classical christmas

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T03:43:34.226Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 05:43:34 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Classical Christmas music typically refers to classical music compositions that are associated with Christmas and holiday season celebrations. These compositions may be religious or secular, and often include traditional carols, hymns, and seasonal songs that have been arranged for orchestras, choirs, and various instrumental ensembles. Examples of classical Christmas music include Handel&#39;s &quot;Messiah,&quot; Bach&#39;s &quot;Christmas Oratorio,&quot; Tchaikovsky&#39;s &quot;The Nutcracker,&quot; and many more. Classical Christmas music is often performed in churches, concert halls, and other venues during the Christmas season.
