## contemporary gospel

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T18:49:50.578Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 20:49:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Contemporary Gospel music is an updated, polished version of traditional gospel, often sounding similar to urban music. It incorporates the rhythms and instrumentation of secular genres, while maintaining its spiritual focus. The material in contemporary gospel is mostly newly written, and the gospel choir has absorbed many innovations in soul music, both vocally and musically.
