## progressive house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T05:33:38.087Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 07:33:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Progressive house is a subgenre of house music that emerged in the early 1990s. It is characterized by its gradual progression of melodic and rhythmic elements throughout the track, often building up to a climax and featuring long, extended mixes. Progressive house often includes elements of trance, techno, and other electronic dance music genres, and is known for its atmospheric and emotional qualities. Key artists in the genre include Sasha, John Digweed, Paul Oakenfold, and Deadmau5, among many others.
