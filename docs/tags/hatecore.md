## hatecore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T04:31:46.255Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 06:31:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hatecore represents music that channels aggressive, confrontational, or divisive themes through various sonic approaches, from heavy metal and hardcore punk to hip-hop and pop. The tag captures artists who court controversy through provocative messaging, polarizing personas, or deliberately abrasive content that elicits strong reactions. These acts often blur entertainment with antagonism, creating work that simultaneously attracts devoted followers and generates significant backlash.
