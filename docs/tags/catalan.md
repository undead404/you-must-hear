## catalan

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T07:11:05.841Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 09:11:05 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Catalan refers to music sung in the Catalan language, originating from Catalonia, Valencia, and the Balearic Islands in Spain. The tag encompasses diverse styles from rock and ska to rumba and folk, unified by their linguistic and cultural identity rather than a specific sound. This music often carries themes of regional pride and social consciousness while ranging from festive party anthems to introspective singer-songwriter works.
