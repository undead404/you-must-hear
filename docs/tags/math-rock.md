## math rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T04:48:17.005Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 06:48:17 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Math rock is a subgenre of rock music that originated in the late 1980s and early 1990s. It is characterized by complex and intricate rhythms, unconventional time signatures, and the use of dissonant and angular guitar riffs. The genre often features complex instrumental arrangements, with a focus on musicianship and technical skill. Math rock bands often incorporate elements of post-rock, punk rock, and avant-garde music, and the genre is known for its experimental and unconventional approach to songwriting. Some notable math rock bands include Don Caballero, Hella, Battles, and Tera Melos.
