## pagan metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T14:13:33.584Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 16:13:33 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Pagan metal blends extreme metal styles like black or folk metal with themes from pre-Christian European traditions and mythology. The music often incorporates traditional folk instruments and melodies alongside heavy guitar riffs and harsh vocals. It emphasizes cultural heritage and nature worship.
