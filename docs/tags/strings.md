## strings

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T13:45:05.557Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 15:45:05 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

&quot;Strings&quot; encompasses music centered on bowed and plucked string instruments, from intimate solo cello to sweeping orchestral arrangements. The genre bridges classical tradition with contemporary indie, folk, and cinematic soundscapes, often emphasizing emotional depth and textural beauty. These compositions range from minimalist chamber pieces to lush, atmospheric scores that blur the lines between classical and modern genres.
