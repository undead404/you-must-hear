## turkish

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:50:19.625Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:50:19 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Turkish pop blends traditional Middle Eastern melodies and instrumentation with contemporary Western pop production. The genre features distinctive vocal ornamentation, emotional delivery, and rhythmic patterns rooted in Turkish folk music. It dominates mainstream music across Turkey and Turkish-speaking communities worldwide.
