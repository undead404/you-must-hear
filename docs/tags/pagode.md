## pagode

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T14:05:45.890Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 16:05:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Pagode is a Brazilian samba subgenre that emerged in Rio de Janeiro in the 1980s, characterized by informal acoustic gatherings featuring instruments like the cavaquinho and banjo. The style emphasizes romantic lyrics, syncopated rhythms, and a relaxed party atmosphere that reflects working-class Afro-Brazilian culture. Modern pagode has evolved to incorporate pop and R&amp;B influences while maintaining its infectious groove and celebration-focused spirit.
