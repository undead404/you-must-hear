## trap

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T04:07:06.107Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 06:07:06 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Trap is a hip-hop subgenre that emerged from Southern rap, characterized by heavy 808 bass, rapid hi-hats, and dark atmospheric synths. The lyrics often reflect street life and hustling culture. Its hypnotic, hard-hitting sound has made it a dominant force in modern rap.
