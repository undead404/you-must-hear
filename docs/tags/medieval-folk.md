## medieval folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T02:10:14.736Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 05:10:14 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Medieval folk blends traditional European folk music with historical instruments and melodies inspired by the Middle Ages, creating an atmospheric sound that evokes ancient times. The music often features period instruments like hurdy-gurdies, lutes, bagpipes, and recorders alongside driving percussion and choral vocals. This tag encompasses both faithful historical recreations and modern interpretations that capture the spirit of pre-Renaissance European culture through ritualistic, earthy soundscapes.
