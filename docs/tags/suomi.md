### Description

&quot;Suomi&quot; refers to Finnish-language music or music originating from Finland, encompassing diverse genres from hip-hop and pop to rock and folk. The tag represents Finland&#39;s distinct musical identity, shaped by the Finnish language&#39;s unique rhythmic qualities and the nation&#39;s cultural heritage. Artists under this umbrella blend international influences with distinctly Nordic sensibilities and introspective storytelling traditions.
