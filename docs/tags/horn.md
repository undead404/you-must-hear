## horn

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T13:09:38.166Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 15:09:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Horn music typically refers to music that prominently features horn instruments, such as the French horn, the trumpet, the trombone, and the tuba. Horns have been a part of musical ensembles for centuries and have been used in a variety of genres, from classical to jazz to rock. In classical music, horns are often used in orchestral and chamber music, while in jazz, they are a key component of big band arrangements. Horns are also commonly used in marching bands and in military music. The specific type of horn music can vary depending on the context and the cultural tradition from which it arises.
