### Description

Portuguese music is diverse, encompassing styles from classical to popular music, with a rich history influenced by various cultures. Traditional Portuguese music includes Fado, a genre known for its deep expression of emotion, often associated with feelings of sadness, pain, and love. In addition to Fado, Portugal has a vibrant popular music scene, with genres ranging from rock and hip-hop to dance and reggae.
