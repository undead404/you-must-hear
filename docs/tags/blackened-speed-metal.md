## blackened speed metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T15:34:08.873Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 17:34:08 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Blackened speed metal fuses the raw velocity and riff-driven aggression of speed metal with black metal&#39;s harsh vocals, lo-fi production, and occult atmosphere. This sound emerged from the underground&#39;s desire to recapture early extreme metal&#39;s primitive energy while maintaining a relentless, headbanging intensity. The result is a filthy, alcohol-soaked style that prioritizes visceral thrills over technical precision.
