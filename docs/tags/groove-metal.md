## groove metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-22T17:12:06.286Z">Sat Nov 22 2025 19:12:06 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Groove metal is a subgenre of heavy metal that emerged in the late 1980s and early 1990s. It combines the intensity of thrash metal with the rhythmic and groovy elements of funk and groove-oriented rock music. The genre is characterized by heavy, down-tuned guitars, syncopated rhythms, and a strong emphasis on the groove. It is often associated with bands such as Pantera, Machine Head, and Sepultura.
