## humour

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T07:16:02.562Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 09:16:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Humour is a diverse music genre centered on comedic, satirical, or absurdist themes rather than a specific sound. Artists use wit, parody, or unconventional performances to entertain and provoke, spanning styles from children&#39;s songs to punk provocations. The genre prioritizes clever lyrics and theatrical delivery over musical conventions.
