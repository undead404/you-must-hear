## microsound

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T03:13:52.629Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 05:13:52 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Microsound refers to electronic music focused on manipulating extremely small sonic particles, often at the threshold of perception—clicks, glitches, grain-like textures, and ultra-brief sounds. This approach emerged from experimental digital audio practices in the 1990s, treating sound itself as raw material to be examined and reconstructed at microscopic time scales. The aesthetic emphasizes precision, minimalism, and the exploration of what happens in the tiny spaces between conventional musical notes.
