## mantra

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T15:09:31.752Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 17:09:31 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Mantra music features repetitive sacred chants and devotional phrases rooted in Hindu, Buddhist, and yogic traditions, designed to focus the mind and elevate consciousness. The sound typically layers vocal repetitions over drone instruments, harmonium, and gentle percussion to create meditative, trance-inducing atmospheres. This spiritual practice bridges ancient Eastern traditions with contemporary wellness culture, offering listeners a tool for meditation and inner transformation.
