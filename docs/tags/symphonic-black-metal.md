## symphonic black metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T01:57:11.678Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 03:57:11 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Symphonic black metal is a subgenre of black metal that incorporates symphonic elements, such as orchestral instruments or synthesizers, to create a more grandiose and epic sound. The genre emerged in the mid-1990s and often features atmospheric, dark, and intense music with high-pitched vocals, blast beats, and fast guitar riffs. The symphonic elements can range from subtle background textures to full orchestral arrangements, adding a layer of complexity and drama to the music. Some notable bands in the symphonic black metal genre include Emperor, Dimmu Borgir, and Cradle of Filth.
