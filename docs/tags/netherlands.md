### Description

The &quot;netherlands&quot; tag encompasses the diverse musical output from the Dutch music scene, reflecting the country&#39;s multicultural urban centers and experimental artistic traditions. It spans from Dutch hip-hop and pop to avant-garde classical compositions and extreme metal, united by a characteristically direct, unpretentious approach to creative expression. The tag captures both mainstream Dutch-language releases and internationally-oriented productions that emerge from this small but influential European music hub.
