## hard bop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:34:03.848Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:34:03 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hard bop emerged in the 1950s as a rawer, more soulful evolution of bebop that incorporated blues, gospel, and R&amp;B influences. The genre featured aggressive rhythms, extended solos, and a grittier sound that reconnected jazz with its African-American roots. It balanced technical virtuosity with emotional intensity and accessibility.
