## djent

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-09T05:13:10.640Z">Tue Dec 09 2025 07:13:10 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Djent is a progressive metal subgenre characterized by palm-muted, syncopated guitar riffs with a distinctive &quot;djent&quot; sound produced through extended-range guitars and heavy distortion. Emerging in the 2000s online metal community, it blends technical precision with polyrhythmic complexity and ambient textures. The style emphasizes rhythm guitar as a percussive element alongside intricate drumming patterns.
