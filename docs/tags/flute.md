### Description

The flute tag encompasses music prominently featuring the flute&#39;s airy, melodic voice across classical, jazz, folk, and new age contexts. Its sound ranges from baroque orchestral pieces and pastoral melodies to improvisational jazz explorations and meditative world music. The instrument&#39;s versatility allows it to convey both technical virtuosity and intimate, breath-driven expressiveness across centuries and cultures.
