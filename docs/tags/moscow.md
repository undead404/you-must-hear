## moscow

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T22:13:17.533Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 00:13:17 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

The &quot;moscow&quot; tag captures the diverse and experimental electronic music scene emerging from Russia&#39;s capital, blending cold, atmospheric soundscapes with elements of darkwave, ambient, and avant-garde production. This sound often features stark minimalism, haunting vocals, and a distinctly post-Soviet aesthetic that reflects the city&#39;s complex cultural identity. The music ranges from ethereal and meditative to harsh and industrial, unified by an underlying sense of urban alienation and artistic defiance.
