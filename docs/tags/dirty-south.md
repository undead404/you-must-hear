## dirty south

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T11:21:24.290Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 13:21:24 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dirty South is a subgenre of hip-hop that originated in the southern United States, particularly in the cities of Atlanta, Houston, and Memphis, during the 1990s. It is characterized by heavy beats, slow and gritty rhythms, and synthesized melodies, often featuring heavy bass lines, 808 drum machines, and chopped and screwed vocal samples. The lyrics often center around themes of street life, drug dealing, violence, and southern pride, and are delivered in a slow, drawling style that is typical of the region. Some of the most well-known Dirty South artists include Outkast, UGK, Lil Wayne, and T.I.
