## nu metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T16:51:24.531Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 18:51:24 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Nu metal emerged in the late 1990s as a fusion of heavy metal with hip-hop, grunge, and industrial elements, featuring downtuned guitars, aggressive rapping, and melodic singing. The genre became the soundtrack for youth angst and rebellion, addressing themes of alienation and inner turmoil. Its raw, accessible sound brought metal to mainstream audiences.
