## roots

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T05:58:02.660Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 07:58:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Roots music encompasses authentic reggae and dub sounds that emerged from Jamaica in the 1960s-70s, characterized by heavy bass lines, steady one-drop rhythms, and socially conscious lyrics. The genre emphasizes spiritual themes, often drawing from Rastafarian culture, while addressing social justice, unity, and liberation. Its organic, stripped-down production style celebrates traditional instrumentation and raw vocal delivery that captures the essence of Caribbean musical heritage.
