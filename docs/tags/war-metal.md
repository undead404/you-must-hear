## war metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T04:27:47.976Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 06:27:47 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

War metal is an extreme subgenre of black and death metal characterized by chaotic, hyperaggressive sound with relentless blast beats, distorted guitars, and raw production that evokes sonic warfare. Emerging from the underground in the 1980s-90s, it embraces militant imagery and apocalyptic themes while rejecting conventional song structures for pure auditory assault. The style prioritizes overwhelming intensity and primitive brutality over melody or accessibility.
