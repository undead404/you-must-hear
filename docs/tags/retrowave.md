## retrowave

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-16T20:12:23.864Z">Sun Nov 16 2025 22:12:23 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Retrowave is a synthesizer-driven electronic music genre that emerged in the mid-2000s, drawing heavy inspiration from 1980s film soundtracks, video games, and pop culture. The style features lush analog synths, nostalgic melodies, and often evokes neon-lit cityscapes and futuristic imagery. It blends vintage production aesthetics with modern electronic music techniques.
