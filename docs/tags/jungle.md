## jungle

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:43:59.693Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:43:59 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Jungle is a fast-paced electronic music genre that emerged from UK rave culture in the early 1990s, characterized by rapid breakbeats (typically 160-180 BPM) and heavy basslines. The genre blends elements of reggae, dancehall, and hip-hop with chopped-up drum samples creating a frenetic, energetic sound. Jungle became a cornerstone of British underground club culture and influenced countless electronic music styles that followed.
