## rawstyle

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T00:22:53.199Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 02:22:53 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rawstyle is an aggressive, unpolished subgenre of hardstyle characterized by distorted, screeching kicks, raw industrial textures, and tempos around 150-160 BPM. Emerging in the early 2010s as a darker countermovement to mainstream hardstyle&#39;s melodic direction, it emphasizes brutal sound design and relentless energy over accessibility. The style dominates harder stages at major festivals and appeals to fans seeking intense, uncompromising electronic music.
