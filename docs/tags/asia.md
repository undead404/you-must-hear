### Description

The &quot;asia&quot; tag encompasses the vast musical diversity of the Asian continent, spanning from Middle Eastern classical traditions to East Asian pop, South Asian film music, and Southeast Asian folk styles. This broad category reflects both traditional instruments and melodies rooted in centuries-old cultural practices, as well as contemporary fusion sounds that blend regional characteristics with global influences. The tag captures music sung in dozens of languages and dialects, united by geographic origin rather than a single sonic identity.
