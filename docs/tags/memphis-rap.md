## memphis rap

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T23:23:50.142Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 01:23:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Memphis rap, also known as Memphis hip hop or Memphis horrorcore, is a subgenre of hip hop that originated in Memphis, Tennessee, in the late 1980s and early 1990s. It is characterized by its dark, gritty sound, heavy use of bass and synthesizers, and lyrics that often deal with violence, crime, and the occult. Memphis rap has had a significant impact on the hip hop industry, with notable artists including Three 6 Mafia, Project Pat, and DJ Paul. It continues to influence modern hip hop, particularly in the southern United States.
