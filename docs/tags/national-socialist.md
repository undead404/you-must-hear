## national socialist

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T09:34:26.961Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 11:34:26 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

National Socialist music refers to music that promotes or expresses the ideals, beliefs, and principles of National Socialism, a far-right political ideology associated with Nazi Germany. This type of music is often characterized by its explicit racism, antisemitism, and glorification of fascist or Nazi ideology. It has been associated with various sub-genres of music, including neo-Nazi punk, neo-Nazi metal, and neo-folk. It is widely condemned and considered to be hate music, and many countries have taken measures to ban or restrict its dissemination.
