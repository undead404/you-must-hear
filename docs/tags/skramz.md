## skramz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T06:48:36.262Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 08:48:36 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Skramz is an internet-era term for the most raw and intense strain of emo, characterized by explosive screamed vocals, chaotic guitar work, and deeply personal, often anguished lyrics. Born from 90s screamo and hardcore punk, it emphasizes emotional catharsis through abrasive, DIY aesthetics and dynamic shifts between whispered introspection and visceral outbursts. The tag captures a specific underground sensibility that values authenticity and unfiltered emotional expression over polish.
