## ccm

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T05:55:18.556Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 07:55:18 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

CCM (Contemporary Christian Music) is a broad genre that blends modern pop, rock, and worship styles with Christian lyrical themes and faith-based messages. It emerged in the late 1960s as a way to reach younger audiences through accessible, radio-friendly sounds rather than traditional hymns. The genre spans diverse subgenres from soft rock to hip-hop, unified by its spiritual content and appeal to Christian communities.
