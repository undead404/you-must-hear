## dubstep

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T22:04:41.646Z">Sun Nov 09 2025 00:04:41 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dubstep is an electronic music genre that originated in the UK in the late 1990s. It is characterized by heavy bass lines, syncopated rhythms, and a range of sonic effects such as drops, wobbles, and glitches. Dubstep typically features a tempo range of around 138-142 beats per minute, and often incorporates elements of other genres such as reggae, garage, and grime. Dubstep has gained a significant following worldwide, with notable artists including Skrillex, Rusko, and Zeds Dead.
