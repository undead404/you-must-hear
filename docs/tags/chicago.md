## chicago

### Description

The Chicago music genre represents the diverse sounds emerging from the city&#39;s independent music scene, blending experimental rock, indie pop, and alternative hip-hop. It&#39;s characterized by lo-fi production, jazz-influenced instrumentation, and introspective lyricism that reflects urban Midwestern life. The style emphasizes creative freedom and cross-genre collaboration over commercial appeal.
