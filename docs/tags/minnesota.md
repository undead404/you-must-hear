### Description

The &quot;minnesota&quot; tag represents music associated with the state&#39;s diverse independent music scene, spanning underground hip-hop, indie rock, and experimental sounds rooted in Minneapolis-St. Paul&#39;s creative communities. The sound often reflects the region&#39;s cold-weather introspection and DIY ethos, blending raw authenticity with innovative production. This tag captures Minnesota&#39;s legacy as a breeding ground for genre-defying artists who operate outside mainstream conventions.
