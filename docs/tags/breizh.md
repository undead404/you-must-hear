## breizh

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T02:43:53.415Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 04:43:53 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Breizh music is a type of traditional music from Brittany, a region in northwestern France. It is characterized by its use of the diatonic accordion, bombarde (a type of oboe), and biniou (a type of bagpipe). Breizh music often incorporates traditional Breton dance rhythms such as the hanter dro and the gavotte, and the lyrics are typically sung in the Breton language. Breizh music has experienced a revival in recent years, with a new generation of musicians combining traditional elements with contemporary influences such as rock and jazz.
