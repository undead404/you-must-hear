## avant-garde metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T07:47:25.732Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 09:47:25 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Avant-garde metal pushes beyond conventional metal boundaries by incorporating experimental structures, unconventional instruments, and influences from jazz, classical, and noise music. It emerged from the extreme metal scene as artists sought to challenge genre conventions and create more abstract, artistic expressions. The music often features dissonant harmonies, complex rhythms, and unpredictable song structures that prioritize innovation over accessibility.
