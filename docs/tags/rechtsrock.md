## rechtsrock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T19:24:38.553Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 21:24:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rechtsrock is a controversial music tag associated with far-right and nationalist rock music, primarily originating from Germany and Eastern Europe. The sound typically features aggressive guitar-driven rock, punk, or metal styles paired with politically extreme lyrics promoting nationalist or white supremacist ideologies. This tag represents music that exists outside mainstream channels due to its extremist political content rather than defining a specific musical style.
