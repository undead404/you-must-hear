## synthesizer

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T12:01:54.877Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 14:01:54 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Synthesizer music centers on electronic instruments that generate and shape sound through oscillators, filters, and modulators, creating tones impossible with traditional instruments. Emerging prominently in the late 1970s and 1980s, it spans from experimental avant-garde compositions to catchy synth-pop anthems, defined by its futuristic, often crystalline textures. The tag captures any music where synthesizers play a defining role in the sonic character, whether providing lush atmospheric pads, punchy basslines, or melodic leads.
