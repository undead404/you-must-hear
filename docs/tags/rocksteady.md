## rocksteady

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T06:42:27.254Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 08:42:27 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rocksteady is a Jamaican music genre that emerged in the mid-1960s as a slower, more soulful evolution of ska, featuring prominent bass lines and romantic vocal harmonies. The genre flourished briefly between 1966 and 1968, bridging ska and reggae while reflecting Kingston&#39;s urban culture and dance scene. Its relaxed tempo and emphasis on melody made it ideal for intimate dancing and laid the foundation for reggae&#39;s worldwide influence.
