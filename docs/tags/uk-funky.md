## uk funky

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:41:38.160Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:41:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

UK funky is a fast-paced electronic dance style that emerged from London&#39;s underground clubs in the late 2000s, blending house, garage, and African rhythms with syncopated percussion and bouncy basslines. The sound features skippy, off-kilter drum patterns typically around 130 BPM, creating an infectious energy that bridges the gap between UK garage&#39;s shuffle and house music&#39;s directness. It emphasizes groove and dancefloor momentum while incorporating soulful vocals and warping synthesizers.
