## thrashcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T09:44:51.686Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 11:44:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Thrashcore fuses the breakneck speed of hardcore punk with the aggressive riffing of thrash metal, creating an intense, chaotic sound built on extremely short songs and relentless energy. Emerging from the early 1980s underground, it strips both genres down to their rawest elements, favoring visceral impact over technical complexity. The style embodies pure sonic aggression and DIY ethos, often clocking in at under a minute per track.
