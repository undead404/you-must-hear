## country

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-16T17:57:37.922Z">Sun Nov 16 2025 19:57:37 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Country music is a genre rooted in American folk traditions, featuring storytelling lyrics about everyday life, love, and hardship. The sound typically combines acoustic guitars, fiddles, and distinctive vocal twangs. Originating in the rural South, it blends blues, gospel, and Appalachian influences.
