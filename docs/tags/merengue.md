## merengue

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:54:13.766Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:54:13 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Merengue is a lively, fast-paced dance music genre originating from the Dominican Republic, characterized by a distinctive two-step rhythm and the prominent use of accordion, tambora drum, and güira. The music features an infectious, syncopated beat that makes it instantly recognizable and irresistibly danceable at social gatherings throughout Latin America and beyond. Merengue blends African and European musical influences, creating an energetic sound that has become a cornerstone of Caribbean culture and Latin popular music.
