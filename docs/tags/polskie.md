### Description

&quot;Polskie&quot; refers to music sung in the Polish language, encompassing diverse styles from rock and punk to pop and poetry-driven ballads rooted in Polish cultural traditions. The tag captures Poland&#39;s rich musical heritage, blending folk influences with contemporary genres and often featuring introspective, poetic lyrics. It represents the sound of Polish identity across generations, from emotional singer-songwriters to rebellious rock bands.
