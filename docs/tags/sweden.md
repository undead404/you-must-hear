## sweden

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-02T18:30:51.118Z">Sun Nov 02 2025 20:30:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Swedish pop music blends infectious melodies with electronic production, creating a distinctively crisp and polished sound that has dominated global charts since the 1970s. The genre ranges from bright, danceable tracks to darker, experimental synthpop. Sweden&#39;s strong music education system and collaborative songwriting culture have made it a pop music powerhouse despite its small population.
