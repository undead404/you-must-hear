## math metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-09T05:52:18.751Z">Tue Dec 09 2025 07:52:18 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Math metal is a technically complex subgenre of heavy metal characterized by odd time signatures, polyrhythms, and intricate guitar work that creates a mechanically precise yet chaotic sound. Emerging in the 1990s from progressive and djent scenes, it appeals to musicians and fans who appreciate mathematical precision in extreme music. The genre blends aggressive distortion with calculated compositional structures that challenge conventional rhythm and melody.
