## sasscore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T05:55:17.536Z">Fri Nov 14 2025 07:55:17 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Sasscore blends pop, rock, and alternative elements with bold, unapologetic lyrics that express attitude, defiance, and emotional intensity. The genre celebrates strong feminine voices pushing back against expectations through catchy melodies and sharp wordplay.
