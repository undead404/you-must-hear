## tech house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T00:51:49.582Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 02:51:49 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Tech house is a electronic dance music genre that blends the rhythmic groove of house music with the mechanical, minimalist elements of techno. Emerging in the 1990s club scene, it features stripped-down beats, subtle basslines, and crisp percussion designed for late-night dancefloors. The sound prioritizes functional, hypnotic repetition over melody, creating an understated yet driving energy.
