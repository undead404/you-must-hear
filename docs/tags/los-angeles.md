### Description

Los Angeles music refers to the diverse range of music genres that have emerged from or been associated with the city of Los Angeles, California. The city has played a pivotal role in the development of various styles of music, including rock and roll, punk rock, and hip-hop. Some notable musicians and bands from Los Angeles include the Beach Boys, the Doors, Guns N&#39; Roses, N.W.A, Dr. Dre, Tupac Shakur, and Kendrick Lamar, among many others. The city&#39;s thriving music scene and its numerous recording studios, music venues, and music festivals have made it a hub for aspiring musicians and music enthusiasts from all over the world.
