### Description

Poetry as a music genre blends spoken or sung verse with musical accompaniment, prioritizing lyrical depth and literary craftsmanship over conventional song structures. It spans from jazz-influenced spoken word to hip-hop and singer-songwriter traditions, emphasizing social commentary and personal reflection. The genre values wordplay, rhythm, and storytelling as central elements rather than melodic hooks.
