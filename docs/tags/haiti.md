## haiti

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T10:22:37.640Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 13:22:37 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

The &quot;haiti&quot; tag encompasses music rooted in Haitian culture, blending African rhythms, Caribbean influences, and French colonial elements into distinctive styles like compas, rara, and vodou drumming. The sound features syncopated guitar patterns, horn sections, and call-and-response vocals that reflect Haiti&#39;s resilient spirit and rich folkloric traditions. This music often carries themes of social commentary, celebration, and cultural pride expressed through both traditional acoustic and modern electric arrangements.
