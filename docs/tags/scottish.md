## scottish

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-31T02:35:14.102Z">Fri Oct 31 2025 04:35:14 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Scottish music refers to the traditional and contemporary music of Scotland, which includes various genres such as Scottish folk music, Celtic music, traditional Scottish pipe music, Gaelic music, and modern Scottish music. Scottish music has a rich history and includes various musical instruments, including bagpipes, fiddles, accordions, guitars, and drums. Traditional Scottish music is often characterized by its lively and upbeat tempo, while contemporary Scottish music is known for its fusion of traditional Scottish sounds with modern elements. Scottish music has also influenced other genres, such as country and rock music.
