## nintendocore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:38:34.444Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:38:34 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Nintendocore fuses aggressive metalcore and post-hardcore with 8-bit video game sounds and chiptune aesthetics, creating a chaotic yet nostalgic sonic experience. The style emerged in the mid-2000s as musicians incorporated Game Boy synthesizers and retro gaming samples into heavy breakdowns and screamed vocals. It celebrates both the intensity of extreme music and the playful energy of classic Nintendo-era gaming culture.
