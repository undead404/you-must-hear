## teen pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-10T22:46:53.245Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 00:46:53 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Teen pop music is a subgenre of pop music that is aimed at a teenage audience. It is characterized by its catchy, upbeat melodies, simple lyrics, and often features a heavily produced sound. Teen pop became popular in the late 1990s and early 2000s, with acts such as Britney Spears, Backstreet Boys, NSYNC, and Christina Aguilera dominating the charts. The genre continues to have a strong presence in popular music, with newer acts such as Justin Bieber and Ariana Grande being labeled as teen pop artists.
