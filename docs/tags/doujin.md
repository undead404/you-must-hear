## doujin

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T06:40:50.364Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 09:40:50 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Doujin refers to self-published or independent music originating from Japan&#39;s creative circles, often associated with fan-made arrangements of video game soundtracks and original compositions. The sound spans diverse genres from orchestral rock to electronic, frequently drawing inspiration from Japanese gaming culture and anime. These works are typically distributed at conventions like Comiket, representing a vibrant grassroots music scene outside mainstream commercial channels.
