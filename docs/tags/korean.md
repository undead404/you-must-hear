### Description

Korean pop, or K-pop, blends electronic dance music, hip-hop, and R&amp;B with meticulously produced vocals and synchronized choreography. Originating from South Korea&#39;s entertainment industry, it emphasizes visual spectacle, catchy hooks, and highly trained idol groups. The genre has become a global phenomenon through its polished production and dedicated fan engagement.
