## bedroom pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-17T13:04:51.145Z">Mon Nov 17 2025 15:04:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Bedroom pop is a subgenre of indie pop that originated in the early 2010s, characterized by its lo-fi aesthetic and DIY approach to music production. As the name suggests, bedroom pop is often made by artists who create music in their bedrooms or home studios, using simple recording equipment and software. The genre is known for its dreamy, intimate sound, featuring hushed vocals, jangly guitars, and electronic elements. Bedroom pop has gained popularity in recent years thanks to platforms like Bandcamp and SoundCloud, which have made it easier for independent artists to share and promote their music.
