## microhouse

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T05:04:02.072Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 07:04:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Microhouse is a minimal electronic dance music genre that emerged in the late 1990s, stripping techno and house down to subtle grooves, glitchy textures, and intricate sonic details. It emphasizes space and restraint over bombast, featuring crisp percussion, warm basslines, and digital artifacts as melodic elements. The genre thrives in intimate club settings and headphone listening, bridging underground dance culture with experimental electronic music.
