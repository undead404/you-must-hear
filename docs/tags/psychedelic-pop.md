## psychedelic pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-18T16:02:27.607Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 18:02:27 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Psychedelic pop is a genre of music that emerged in the mid-1960s as an offshoot of the psychedelic rock movement. It combines elements of psychedelic rock, folk, and pop music, featuring trippy melodies, harmonies, and lyrics, as well as the use of unconventional instrumentation, such as sitars, mellotrons, and other electronic effects. The style is often characterized by a dreamy, otherworldly sound, and the use of studio production techniques to create a dense and layered sonic landscape. Some notable examples of psychedelic pop bands include The Beatles, The Beach Boys, The Zombies, and Pink Floyd.
