## acoustic rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-18T08:03:20.721Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 10:03:20 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Acoustic rock strips away electric instrumentation to emphasize organic sounds like acoustic guitars, piano, and minimal percussion while maintaining rock&#39;s melodic sensibility. It emerged as artists sought intimate, unplugged alternatives to heavily produced rock music. The genre blends folk&#39;s authenticity with rock&#39;s energy and pop accessibility.
