## korn

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T02:30:00.950Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 04:30:00 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

A tag representing the signature sound pioneered by its namesake band, characterized by downtuned seven-string guitars, hip-hop influenced grooves, and anguished vocal delivery that defined nu-metal. The style blends heavy metal aggression with raw emotional vulnerability, often exploring themes of childhood trauma and inner turmoil. Its cultural impact shaped late-90s alternative metal with a distinctly dark, cathartic energy.
