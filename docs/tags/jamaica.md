## jamaica

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:30:37.333Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:30:37 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Jamaican music encompasses the island&#39;s rich musical traditions, including reggae, ska, dancehall, and dub, characterized by offbeat rhythms, heavy bass, and socially conscious lyrics. Rooted in African and Caribbean influences, these genres emerged from Kingston&#39;s sound system culture and Rastafarian spirituality. The music&#39;s syncopated grooves and laid-back yet powerful energy have profoundly influenced global popular music.
