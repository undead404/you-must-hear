### Description

Music tagged as &quot;india&quot; encompasses traditional and contemporary sounds rooted in the Indian subcontinent, featuring instruments like sitar, tabla, and tanpura alongside classical ragas and devotional forms. The tag bridges ancient musical traditions with modern fusion, blending Indian classical elements with electronic, rock, and world music influences. It reflects India&#39;s diverse cultural heritage through both authentic classical performances and cross-cultural collaborations that bring Indian sonic textures to global audiences.
