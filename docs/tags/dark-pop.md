## dark pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T22:07:30.671Z">Sun Nov 09 2025 00:07:30 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dark pop is a subgenre of pop music that is characterized by moody, atmospheric, and sometimes gloomy lyrics and musical elements. It is often associated with themes of heartbreak, loneliness, and introspection. The music typically features electronic or synthetic instruments and production techniques, and may incorporate elements of alternative or indie rock, as well as gothic, trip-hop, or other genres. Some notable artists associated with dark pop include Lorde, Billie Eilish, Lana Del Rey, The Weeknd, and Halsey.
