### Description

Hebrew music encompasses songs performed in the Hebrew language, spanning traditional Jewish liturgical chants, modern Israeli pop, and contemporary fusion styles. The tag reflects diverse cultural expressions from religious devotional music to secular innovations that blend Middle Eastern instrumentation with electronic, rock, and folk elements. This category bridges ancient spiritual traditions with modern musical experimentation across the global Jewish diaspora and Israeli culture.
