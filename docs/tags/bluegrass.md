## bluegrass

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-18T11:53:52.645Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 13:53:52 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Bluegrass is an acoustic American roots music style characterized by fast tempos, intricate string instrumentals, and high lonesome harmonies. Originating in Appalachia during the 1940s, it features mandolin, banjo, fiddle, guitar, and upright bass in energetic ensemble interplay. The genre blends Scottish, Irish, and African American musical traditions into a distinctively American sound.
