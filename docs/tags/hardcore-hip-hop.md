## hardcore hip hop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-10T13:05:23.078Z">Mon Nov 10 2025 15:05:23 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hardcore hip hop is a subgenre of hip hop music that is characterized by aggressive and confrontational lyrics, often dealing with themes of violence, social and political issues, and street life. The music often features hard-hitting beats, fast rhymes, and a strong emphasis on lyricism and wordplay. Hardcore hip hop emerged in the mid-to-late 1980s and was popularized by artists such as Public Enemy, N.W.A., and Ice-T. The genre has since evolved and continues to be a significant part of hip hop culture.
