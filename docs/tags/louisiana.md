## louisiana

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T18:39:37.960Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 20:39:37 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Louisiana’s music is rich and diverse, with the state being the birthplace of multiple musical genres including jazz, Cajun, zydeco, and swamp pop. The music of rural south Louisiana is particularly unique, featuring Creole music (zydeco), swamp pop, and swamp blues. In contrast, the Greater New Orleans area is renowned for its musical heritage tied to Dixieland jazz, blues, and Afro-Caribbean rhythms.
