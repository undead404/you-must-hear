## ebm

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T04:44:27.057Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 06:44:27 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

EBM stands for &quot;Electronic Body Music,&quot; which is a subgenre of electronic music that originated in Belgium in the early 1980s. EBM combines elements of post-punk, industrial, and electronic dance music, with a focus on repetitive, aggressive beats and heavy use of synthesizers and drum machines. The lyrics often deal with themes of dystopian societies, political oppression, and social commentary. EBM has influenced a wide range of other electronic and industrial music genres and has remained popular in underground music scenes around the world.
