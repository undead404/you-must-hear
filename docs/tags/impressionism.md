## impressionism

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T06:34:58.059Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 08:34:58 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Impressionism in music emphasizes atmosphere, color, and mood over traditional structure, using unconventional scales, unresolved harmonies, and delicate textures to evoke images and emotions. Originating in late 19th-century France as a parallel to the visual arts movement, it broke from Romantic-era conventions to capture fleeting moments and sensory experiences. The style creates dreamlike, shimmering soundscapes that suggest rather than directly state, prioritizing tone and ambiance.
