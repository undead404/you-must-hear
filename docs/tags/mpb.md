## mpb

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-12T01:54:56.020Z">Wed Nov 12 2025 03:54:56 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

MPB stands for Música Popular Brasileira, which translates to &quot;Brazilian Popular Music.&quot; It is a broad term that encompasses a wide range of musical styles and genres that have developed in Brazil since the 1960s. MPB is a fusion of traditional Brazilian music, such as samba and bossa nova, with rock, jazz, and other genres from around the world. It is characterized by its diverse rhythms, rich harmonies, and complex melodies, as well as its often-political lyrics. Many famous Brazilian musicians, such as Caetano Veloso, Chico Buarque, and Gilberto Gil, are associated with the MPB genre.
