## manchester

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-12T06:56:35.993Z">Wed Nov 12 2025 08:56:35 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Manchester is a British indie rock sound that emerged from the city&#39;s post-punk scene in the 1980s, blending jangly guitars with dance rhythms and melancholic lyrics. The genre captured working-class youth culture through its fusion of alternative rock, psychedelia, and electronic influences. Its distinctive character combined introspective songwriting with an energetic, rebellious spirit that defined an era.
