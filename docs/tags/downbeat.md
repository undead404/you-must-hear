## downbeat

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-16T20:13:22.649Z">Sun Nov 16 2025 22:13:22 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Downbeat describes atmospheric electronic music characterized by slower tempos, heavy bass, and a melancholic or introspective mood. Emerging from trip-hop and ambient traditions, it blends elements of dub, jazz, and experimental electronica into hypnotic, often nocturnal soundscapes. The music emphasizes texture and space over energy, creating immersive listening experiences that feel contemplative rather than dance-oriented.
