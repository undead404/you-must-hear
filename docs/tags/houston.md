## houston

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-08T19:09:07.089Z">Sat Nov 08 2025 21:09:07 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Houston represents the distinctive sonic identity of Texas&#39;s largest city, characterized by chopped and screwed production techniques, heavy bass, and slowed-down tempos that create a syrupy, hypnotic atmosphere. The sound emerged from the city&#39;s hip-hop scene, blending Southern rap traditions with innovative studio experimentation and reflecting Houston&#39;s car culture and late-night cruising lifestyle. This tag encompasses both the city&#39;s influential rap legacy and its broader musical heritage, from soulful vocals to trap-influenced modern productions.
