## opera

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:34:00.800Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:34:00 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Opera is a dramatic theatrical art form combining classical singing, orchestral music, and stagecraft to tell stories through powerful vocal performances. Originating in Renaissance Italy, it emphasizes trained voices performing in multiple languages without amplification. The genre ranges from tragic historical epics to comedic tales, often featuring elaborate costumes and sets.
