## delta blues

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T15:09:45.894Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 17:09:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Delta blues is a raw, emotionally intense style of blues that originated in the Mississippi Delta region during the early 20th century, characterized by slide guitar, bottleneck techniques, and deeply personal vocals. The music often features sparse instrumentation, with performers using acoustic guitars to create haunting, hypnotic rhythms that reflect the hardships of rural Southern life. Its influence shaped virtually all modern blues and rock music.
