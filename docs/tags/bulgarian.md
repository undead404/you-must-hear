## bulgarian

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T13:55:11.972Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 15:55:11 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Bulgarian music is a vibrant blend of classical, folk, and popular styles, with a rich tradition of vocal and instrumental performances. It is internationally recognized for its unique rhythms, often featuring complex time signatures known as &quot;Bulgarian rhythms&quot;. The genre is also known for its wide range of instruments, from traditional ones like the gadulka and gaida, to modern electric instruments.
