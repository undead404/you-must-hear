## musical

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T03:33:45.900Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 05:33:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

A musical is a form of theatrical performance that combines songs, spoken dialogue, acting, and dance. The plot and characters are often developed through the music and lyrics, which are usually written specifically for the show. Musicals can range from lighthearted and comedic to dramatic and serious, and may incorporate various styles of music, including pop, rock, jazz, and classical. Musicals have been a popular form of entertainment since the 19th century and have been adapted into movies and television shows as well. Famous examples of musicals include &quot;Les Miserables,&quot; &quot;The Phantom of the Opera,&quot; and &quot;Hamilton.&quot;
