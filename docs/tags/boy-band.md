## boy band

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T16:49:33.607Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 18:49:33 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Boy band music is a genre of pop music that features a group of young male singers who perform songs that are typically written and produced by other musicians and songwriters. The term &quot;boy band&quot; typically refers to a group of three or more young male performers who sing and dance together, often with synchronized choreography. Boy bands became popular in the 1990s, and some of the most successful groups of all time include the Backstreet Boys, NSYNC, and One Direction. Boy band music often features catchy melodies, harmonies, and upbeat lyrics that are designed to appeal to a primarily young female audience.
