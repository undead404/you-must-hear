## doomergaze

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T10:57:09.207Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 12:57:09 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Doomergaze blends melancholic pop with shoegaze-inspired atmospheric textures, expressing millennial and Gen Z anxieties through dreamy, introspective soundscapes. The genre captures feelings of digital-age alienation and romantic despair with lush production and emotionally vulnerable vocals. It emerged from internet culture as a soundtrack to existential malaise and nostalgic yearning.
