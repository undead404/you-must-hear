## pipe organ

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T17:40:33.886Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 19:40:33 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Pipe organ music refers to music that is specifically written for the pipe organ, an instrument that produces sound by air flowing through pipes of various sizes and shapes. Pipe organs are often found in churches, concert halls, and other large venues due to their size and the grandeur of their sound. The music written for the pipe organ can range from sacred music, such as hymns and religious pieces, to secular music, such as organ transcriptions of orchestral works or original compositions written specifically for the instrument. The pipe organ has a long and rich history in Western music, dating back to the Middle Ages, and has been used in a wide range of genres, from baroque and classical music to contemporary and experimental music.
