## live set

### Description

A live set in music typically refers to a performance by a musician or group of musicians in front of a live audience, as opposed to a recorded or studio performance. A live set can include a variety of musical genres, from rock and pop to electronic and classical. The setlist may include original songs, covers, and improvisations, and the performance may also incorporate visual elements such as lighting, stage design, and projections. Live sets can be recorded and released as live albums, concert films, or online streams, allowing fans to experience the energy and excitement of a live performance from the comfort of their own homes.
