## western

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:27:00.297Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:27:00 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Western music evokes the American frontier through narrative ballads about cowboys, outliers, and desert landscapes, often featuring acoustic guitars, harmonicas, and orchestral arrangements. The genre gained prominence in mid-20th century film soundtracks, blending folk traditions with cinematic drama. Its influence extends from classic country to spaghetti western scores and modern interpretations.
