## metallic hardcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T01:39:53.339Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 03:39:53 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Metallic hardcore fuses the aggressive intensity of hardcore punk with the crushing heaviness and precision of metal, creating a sound defined by razor-sharp riffs, punishing breakdowns, and raw vocal fury. Emerging from the crossover between DIY hardcore scenes and extreme metal, it emphasizes both technical musicianship and uncompromising energy. The style channels visceral anger and catharsis through dense, distorted guitar work and relentless rhythmic assault.
