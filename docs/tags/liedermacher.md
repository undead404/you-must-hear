## liedermacher

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T09:48:50.287Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 11:48:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Liedermacher is a German singer-songwriter tradition emphasizing poetic, socially conscious lyrics delivered with acoustic instrumentation and intimate vocal performance. Rooted in 1960s folk movements, it blends political commentary, personal storytelling, and literary craftsmanship with influences from chanson and cabaret. The style values authenticity and lyrical depth over production polish, creating direct connections between artist and audience.
