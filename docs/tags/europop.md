## europop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-24T04:01:38.016Z">Mon Nov 24 2025 06:01:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Europop is a dance-oriented pop music style that emerged from Europe, characterized by catchy melodies, electronic production, and upbeat rhythms designed for mainstream radio and club play. The genre blends synthesizers, drum machines, and polished vocals with an emphasis on hooks and accessibility. It became globally influential in shaping modern pop music&#39;s sound from the 1980s onward.
