## pacific

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:27:09.836Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:27:09 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Pacific music refers to the music of the Pacific Islands, which include Melanesia, Micronesia, and Polynesia. This type of music is diverse and reflects the unique cultures of each island nation. Traditional Pacific music is often based on vocal harmonies and accompanied by instruments such as the ukulele, guitar, and percussion. Some well-known examples of Pacific music include Hawaiian hula music, Maori music from New Zealand, and Tahitian music. More contemporary Pacific music styles include island reggae, which is popular in Fiji and Samoa, and Vanuatu string band music, which incorporates elements of traditional and modern music.
