## norway

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:57:45.916Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:57:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

&quot;Norway&quot; encompasses diverse Nordic music ranging from folk traditions featuring Hardanger fiddles and traditional instruments to contemporary indie pop and electronic sounds. The genre reflects Norwegian cultural identity through both preservation of heritage music and modern experimentation. Artists blend melancholic Scandinavian sensibilities with innovative production across folk, pop, and alternative styles.
