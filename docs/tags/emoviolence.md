## emoviolence

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T11:04:53.057Z">Fri Nov 14 2025 13:04:53 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Emoviolence fuses the raw emotional intensity of screamo with the chaotic brutality of powerviolence, creating an aggressive and cathartic sound marked by dissonant guitar work, frantic tempo shifts, and anguished vocals. Emerging from the late 1990s DIY hardcore scene, it channels personal pain and social frustration through brief, explosive songs that prioritize visceral impact over traditional structure. The style emphasizes unfiltered emotion and sonic extremity, often incorporating moments of fragile melody within its overwhelming heaviness.
