## new beat

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:37:51.040Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:37:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

New Beat is a genre of electronic dance music that originated in Belgium around 1987 and gained popularity through 1989. It is characterized by a relatively slow beat, incorporating samples, synths, and hypnotic bass lines. The genre is a fusion of new wave, hi-NRG, EBM, hip hop, and other popular dance music of its era, and it had a significant influence on the evolution of European electronic dance music styles.
