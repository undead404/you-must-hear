## doomcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T07:56:44.354Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 09:56:44 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Doomcore is a subgenre of electronic music that combines elements of doom metal and hardcore techno. It is characterized by a slow and heavy beat, distorted and aggressive soundscapes, and intense, often menacing vocals. The music often features dark and foreboding themes, drawing inspiration from horror movies and dystopian literature. Doomcore emerged in the early 1990s and has since developed a small but dedicated following among fans of experimental and underground electronic music.
