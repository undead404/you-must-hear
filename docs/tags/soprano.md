## soprano

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-20T23:27:58.208Z">Tue Oct 21 2025 02:27:58 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Soprano refers to music prominently featuring the highest female vocal register, characterized by bright, soaring tones that convey power and emotional intensity. This vocal range spans classical opera, contemporary classical crossover, symphonic metal, and theatrical performance traditions. The soprano voice often serves as the primary melodic focus, capable of expressing both delicate vulnerability and dramatic grandeur.
