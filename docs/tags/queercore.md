## queercore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:59:57.679Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:59:57 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Queercore is a punk rock subgenre that emerged in the 1980s as an explicitly LGBTQ+ countercultural movement. The music combines aggressive punk energy with lyrics addressing queer identity, gender expression, and resistance to heteronormative society. It emphasizes DIY ethics and radical politics.
