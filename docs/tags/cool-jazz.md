## cool jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:37:58.519Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:37:58 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Cool jazz emerged in the late 1940s as a softer, more relaxed alternative to bebop&#39;s intensity, favoring understated melodies and lighter tones. The style emphasized sophisticated arrangements, smooth phrasing, and a laid-back temperament that contrasted with jazz&#39;s typically energetic approach. Its restrained elegance appealed to both jazz aficionados and mainstream listeners seeking accessible yet artistically sophisticated music.
