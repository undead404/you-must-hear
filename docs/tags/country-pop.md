## country pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T01:03:37.922Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 03:03:37 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Country pop is a subgenre of country music that emerged in the 1960s and combines the traditional sounds and themes of country music with elements of pop music. It often features more polished production, greater use of electric instruments, and a more commercial sound compared to traditional country music. Country pop artists often crossover to the pop charts and enjoy mainstream success, such as Taylor Swift, Shania Twain, and Faith Hill.
