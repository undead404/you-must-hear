### Description

German metal music is a genre characterized by its heavy and aggressive sound. It has a rich history, with Germany being one of Europe’s major contributors to the metal scene. The genre is known for bands like Scorpions and Accept, and it continues to thrive with newer bands like Rammstein, all of whom have made significant global impact.
