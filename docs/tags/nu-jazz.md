## nu jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T04:25:34.955Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 06:25:34 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Nu jazz, also known as jazztronica or electro-jazz, is a subgenre of jazz that incorporates electronic music styles, such as electronica, funk, hip hop, and house music. Nu jazz artists typically use live instruments, such as saxophones, trumpets, and keyboards, as well as electronic instruments and programming to create a fusion of traditional jazz and contemporary electronic music. The genre emerged in the 1990s and has since gained popularity around the world, particularly in Europe and Japan. Some well-known nu jazz artists include St. Germain, The Cinematic Orchestra, and Jazzanova.
