## heavy metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:40:51.728Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:40:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Heavy metal is a loud, aggressive rock genre characterized by distorted electric guitars, powerful drums, and intense vocals. Emerging in the late 1960s and 1970s, it often explores dark themes through dramatic musicianship and theatrical performance. The genre emphasizes technical skill, amplified sound, and rebellious energy.
