## nederlandstalig

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T11:56:10.234Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 13:56:10 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Nederlandstalig refers to music performed in the Dutch language, encompassing a wide range of genres from folk and cabaret to pop and rock. This tag represents a rich tradition of lyric-focused songwriting where poetic storytelling and wordplay take center stage, often reflecting Dutch and Flemish cultural experiences. The music ranges from intimate singer-songwriter material to stadium-filling rock, unified by its use of Dutch as the primary language of expression.
