## swedish death metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-01-07T21:24:00.631Z">Tue Jan 07 2025 23:24:00 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Swedish death metal is a subgenre that emerged in the late 1980s, characterized by its distinctively raw, buzzsaw guitar tone and d-beat influenced drumming. The sound blends melodic sensibility with crushing heaviness, creating an atmosphere that&#39;s both brutal and atmospheric. It represents a particularly influential strain of extreme metal that helped define the genre&#39;s evolution in Scandinavia.
