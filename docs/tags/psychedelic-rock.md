## psychedelic rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-03T06:35:12.924Z">Mon Nov 03 2025 08:35:12 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Psychedelic rock emerged in the 1960s counterculture, using experimental sounds, effects, and extended instrumental passages to evoke altered states of consciousness. The genre features distorted guitars, reverb, and unconventional song structures that challenge traditional rock formats. Its dreamy, surreal quality aims to replicate or enhance psychedelic experiences through music.
