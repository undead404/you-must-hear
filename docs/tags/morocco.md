## morocco

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T02:06:45.907Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 04:06:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Moroccan music refers to the musical traditions and styles that originate from the North African country of Morocco. Morocco&#39;s music has been influenced by a variety of cultures and civilizations, including Berber, Arabic, Andalusian, and Sub-Saharan African. Traditional Moroccan music often features the use of stringed instruments such as the oud and the guembri, percussion instruments such as the bendir and the darbuka, and wind instruments such as the nay and the ghaita. Moroccan music has also incorporated Western musical elements and has produced a popular music scene that incorporates styles such as rock, rap, and electronic music.
