## eurodance

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T08:57:43.401Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 10:57:43 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Eurodance is a genre of electronic dance music that originated in Europe in the late 1980s and early 1990s. It is characterized by upbeat tempos, catchy melodies, and heavily synthesized sounds. The genre combines elements of house, techno, and pop music, and often features female vocals and rap verses. Some of the most popular Eurodance artists include Haddaway, 2 Unlimited, Real McCoy, and La Bouche. Eurodance enjoyed widespread commercial success in the early to mid-1990s, particularly in Europe, but also gained popularity in other parts of the world.
