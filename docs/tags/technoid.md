## technoid

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-25T18:37:48.556Z">Tue Nov 25 2025 20:37:48 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Technoid music is a genre that emerged in the 1990s, primarily in Germany. It is characterized by its fast, hard-hitting beats and its use of technology in the music production process. Technoid music draws on elements of techno, industrial, and electronic dance music, and is often associated with the hard techno and schranz subgenres. The music often features repetitive and minimalist rhythms, with heavy use of samples, loops, and electronic effects. Technoid is often played in clubs and at raves, and is popular among fans of electronic dance music.
