## modern rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-01T02:51:51.387Z">Sat Nov 01 2025 04:51:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Modern rock emerged in the 1980s as an alternative to mainstream rock, blending post-punk energy with melodic sensibilities and electronic elements. The genre emphasizes atmospheric production, introspective lyrics, and diverse influences ranging from indie to pop. It appeals to audiences seeking guitar-driven music with contemporary polish and emotional depth.
