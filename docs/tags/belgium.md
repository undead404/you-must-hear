### Description

&quot;Belgium&quot; represents the diverse musical output from Belgium, reflecting the country&#39;s multilingual culture through French chanson, Dutch-language pop, and English-language indie rock. The genre spans from theatrical, poetic songwriting to electronic pop and dance music, often blending European sophistication with contemporary production. Belgian artists frequently achieve international success while maintaining distinct national characteristics of artistic experimentation and lyrical depth.
