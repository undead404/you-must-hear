## smooth jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:25:01.417Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:25:01 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Smooth jazz emerged in the 1970s as a radio-friendly fusion of jazz, R&amp;B, and pop, characterized by polished production and mellow grooves. The genre emphasizes accessible melodies over complex improvisation, creating an elegant backdrop for casual listening. Its relaxed sophistication made it popular in upscale settings and adult contemporary formats.
