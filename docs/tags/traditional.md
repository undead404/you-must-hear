### Description

Traditional music encompasses culturally rooted sounds passed down through generations, reflecting the heritage and customs of specific regions or communities. The tag spans diverse styles from folk ballads to ceremonial songs, characterized by acoustic instrumentation, oral transmission, and connection to cultural identity. These recordings preserve historical musical practices while often bridging past and present through contemporary interpretations.
