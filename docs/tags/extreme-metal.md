## extreme metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T16:06:55.959Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 19:06:55 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Extreme metal encompasses the most intense and uncompromising subgenres of heavy metal, characterized by aggressive vocals, distorted guitars, blast beats, and dark thematic content. Born from the underground in the 1980s, it pushes musical and lyrical boundaries through speed, technical complexity, and confrontational imagery. The sound ranges from crushing brutality to chaotic dissonance, united by an ethos of sonic extremity.
