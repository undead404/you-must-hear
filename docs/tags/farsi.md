## farsi

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T06:08:58.902Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 08:08:58 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Farsi music refers to music that is performed or composed in the Farsi language. Farsi is the official language of Iran and is also spoken in parts of Afghanistan, Tajikistan, and other Central Asian countries. Farsi music encompasses a wide range of styles and genres, including traditional folk music, classical music, pop music, and rock music, among others. Many Farsi songs are characterized by intricate melodies, complex rhythms, and poetic lyrics that often touch on themes of love, nature, and spirituality. Some notable Farsi musicians and singers include Mohammad Reza Shajarian, Shahram Nazeri, Googoosh, and Ebi, among others.
