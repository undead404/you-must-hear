## big band

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T03:53:03.528Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 05:53:03 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Big band is a jazz-based genre featuring large orchestras of 10-25 musicians arranged in brass, woodwind, and rhythm sections. It dominated American popular music from the 1930s-1940s, providing danceable swing rhythms that defined the era&#39;s social scene. The sound is characterized by powerful brass fanfares, complex arrangements, and a driving beat.
