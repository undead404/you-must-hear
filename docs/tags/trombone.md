## trombone

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T10:58:56.823Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 12:58:56 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Trombone music refers to any music that features the trombone as a prominent instrument. The trombone is a brass instrument that produces sound through the vibration of the player&#39;s lips as they blow air into the instrument&#39;s mouthpiece. Trombone music can be found in a variety of genres including jazz, classical, pop, and rock, among others. In jazz, the trombone is often used to play improvisational solos and as part of a larger horn section, while in classical music it is often used in orchestral and chamber music settings. In pop and rock music, the trombone can be found in various horn sections and is often used to add depth and texture to a song.
