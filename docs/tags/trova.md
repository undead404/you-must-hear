## trova

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T11:56:15.261Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 13:56:15 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Trova is a Latin American singer-songwriter tradition rooted in poetic storytelling and social consciousness, blending folk melodies with literary lyrics that explore love, politics, and everyday life. Originating in Cuba and spreading across Spanish-speaking countries, it emphasizes acoustic guitar accompaniment and intimate vocal delivery. The style values craftsmanship in both music and verse, creating songs that function as cultural commentary and personal reflection.
