## gypsy jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-10T22:44:49.236Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 00:44:49 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Gypsy jazz is a vibrant acoustic style born in 1930s Paris, blending Romani musical traditions with American jazz improvisation. The sound features rapid-fire guitar strumming, intricate melodic runs, and a distinctive swing rhythm driven by acoustic instruments like violin and double bass. Its passionate, virtuosic playing evokes both the romance of European cafés and the nomadic spirit of Romani culture.
