## punjabi bhangra

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T11:06:39.930Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 13:06:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Punjabi bhangra music is a genre of popular music that originated in the Punjab region of India and Pakistan. Bhangra music is known for its high-energy beats and rhythmic percussion, with instruments such as the dhol, tumbi, and dholak being commonly used. The music often features Punjabi lyrics and vocals, as well as elements of traditional Indian and Pakistani folk music. Bhangra has become increasingly popular around the world, with artists blending the genre with hip hop, reggae, and other styles to create unique fusions.
