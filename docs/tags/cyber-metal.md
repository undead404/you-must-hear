## cyber metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-29T09:11:39.652Z">Wed Oct 29 2025 11:11:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Cyber metal fuses aggressive metal instrumentation with electronic, industrial, and synthwave elements, creating a dystopian soundscape that evokes futuristic technology and digital warfare. The style emphasizes mechanical precision, pulsing synths, and heavily processed vocals alongside distorted guitars and punishing rhythms. It embodies the aesthetic of cyberpunk culture, blending human intensity with machine-like coldness.
