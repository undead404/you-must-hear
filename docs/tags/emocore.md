## emocore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-29T12:02:39.507Z">Wed Oct 29 2025 14:02:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Emocore, also known as emo, is a subgenre of punk rock music that emerged in the mid-1980s. It is characterized by its emotional, often confessional lyrics, which deal with themes such as heartbreak, social anxiety, and self-doubt. Emo music is often associated with a distinctive fashion style, which includes tight-fitting clothes, studded belts, and dyed hair. Emo music experienced a surge in popularity during the early 2000s, with bands like My Chemical Romance, Fall Out Boy, and Panic! at the Disco achieving mainstream success.
