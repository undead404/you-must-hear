## showtune

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T01:07:46.488Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 03:07:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Showtune music, also known as musical theatre music, refers to music written for stage productions, including Broadway musicals, West End productions, and other theatrical productions. Showtunes often feature a combination of music, lyrics, and choreography to tell a story or convey a specific message. The music in showtunes can range from ballads and romantic songs to upbeat dance numbers and comedic tunes. Some popular composers of showtune music include Andrew Lloyd Webber, Stephen Sondheim, and Rodgers and Hammerstein.
