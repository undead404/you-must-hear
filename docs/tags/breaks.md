## breaks

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T10:20:24.138Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 12:20:24 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Breaks is an electronic dance music genre built around syncopated breakbeats—chopped and looped drum patterns that create a distinctive stop-start rhythm. Emerging from 1990s UK rave culture, it blends hip-hop breaks with bass-heavy electronic production and funk influences. The sound is energetic and groove-oriented, emphasizing punchy drums and creative sampling over steady four-on-the-floor beats.
