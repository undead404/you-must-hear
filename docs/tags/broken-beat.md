## broken beat

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:36:01.631Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:36:01 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Broken beat is an electronic music genre that emerged from London&#39;s West End club scene in the late 1990s, blending jazz, funk, and soul with intricate, syncopated rhythms. The style features deliberately fragmented drum patterns that deviate from standard four-on-the-floor beats, creating a sophisticated, off-kilter groove. It represents a fusion of UK electronic innovation with global influences, appealing to dancers and listeners seeking complex, musicianship-focused productions.
