## liquid funk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-18T20:39:50.167Z">Sat Oct 18 2025 23:39:50 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Liquid funk is a melodic subgenre of drum and bass that emerged in the early 2000s, characterized by smooth, soulful atmospheres and jazzy influences over fast breakbeats. It emphasizes musicality with warm basslines, uplifting pads, and often incorporates vocal samples or live instrumentation. The style became popular in underground club scenes for its more accessible, emotionally resonant approach to the typically hard-edged drum and bass sound.
