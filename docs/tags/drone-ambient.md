## drone ambient

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-09T05:51:29.171Z">Tue Dec 09 2025 07:51:29 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Drone ambient is a subgenre of ambient music that emphasizes the use of sustained or repeated sounds, often generated through electronic or acoustic means. The sounds may be produced using synthesizers, guitars, or other instruments, and are often manipulated or processed to create a sense of immersion or hypnotic effect. The music tends to be slow-paced and meditative, with an emphasis on texture and atmosphere rather than melody or rhythm. Drone ambient is often associated with themes of nature, mysticism, and spirituality.
