## woman

### Description

The &quot;woman&quot; tag encompasses vocal music featuring female artists across diverse genres, united by the presence and perspective of women performers. This broad category spans from intimate singer-songwriter works to powerful pop performances, celebrating the distinctive timbres, emotional expressions, and artistic visions that female voices bring to music. It reflects both the universal human experience through a feminine lens and the rich variety of women&#39;s contributions to musical culture worldwide.
