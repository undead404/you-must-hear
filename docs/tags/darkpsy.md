## darkpsy

### Description

Darkpsy (also known as Dark Psychedelic Trance) is a subgenre of psytrance music that originated in the early 2000s. It is characterized by fast and repetitive psychedelic sounds, dark and eerie atmospheres, and heavy basslines. The tempo is usually between 150 and 180 beats per minute, and the music often includes samples of horror films or other dark themes. Darkpsy is often associated with the underground rave culture and is known for its intense and hypnotic qualities that can induce a trance-like state in the listener. It is a popular genre among psychedelic enthusiasts and has a strong following in countries like Russia and Brazil.
