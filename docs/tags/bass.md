## bass

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T13:51:36.736Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 15:51:36 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Bass music is a diverse electronic genre characterized by heavy, deep low-frequency sounds and powerful sub-bass that creates visceral physical impact. Emerging from UK club culture, it encompasses styles from dubstep to trap, emphasizing rhythm and bass over melody. The genre prioritizes immersive sonic experiences designed for sound system culture and festival environments.
