## tribal industrial

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-25T18:44:24.836Z">Tue Nov 25 2025 20:44:24 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Tribal industrial is a genre of music that combines elements of industrial music with tribal rhythms and instrumentation. The sound is often characterized by the use of percussion instruments such as djembes and congas, along with heavy use of electronic production techniques such as sampling, loops, and distorted sound effects. The music is often associated with primal or tribal themes, and can be highly energetic and intense, with a strong emphasis on rhythm and repetition. The genre emerged in the 1990s as a subgenre of industrial music, and has since developed its own distinct sound and style.
