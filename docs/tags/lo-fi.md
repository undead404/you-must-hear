## lo-fi

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-16T20:11:33.466Z">Sun Nov 16 2025 22:11:33 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Lo-fi music is a genre of music that is characterized by its intentionally low-quality, unpolished, or &quot;lo-fi&quot; sound. The term &quot;lo-fi&quot; is short for &quot;low fidelity,&quot; which refers to the quality of the sound recording or production. Lo-fi music often features a relaxed, mellow, and melancholic sound, with elements of jazz, hip-hop, and indie rock. The use of analog equipment, such as tape machines and vintage instruments, is also a common feature of lo-fi music. The genre gained popularity in the 1990s and has continued to influence contemporary music, with many artists and producers incorporating lo-fi elements into their work.
