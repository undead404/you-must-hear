## power electronics

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-01-08T10:59:03.938Z">Wed Jan 08 2025 12:59:03 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Power electronics is an extreme form of noise music characterized by harsh, distorted sounds, aggressive vocals, and confrontational themes that often explore violence, transgression, and psychological intensity. Emerging from industrial music in the 1980s, it strips away conventional song structure in favor of abrasive textures created through feedback, distortion, and electronic manipulation. The genre prioritizes raw emotional impact and provocation over accessibility, creating an intentionally unsettling listening experience.
