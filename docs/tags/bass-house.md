## bass house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T06:40:22.985Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 08:40:22 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Bass house is an electronic dance music genre that emerged in the mid-2010s, combining the four-on-the-floor beat of house music with aggressive, wobbling basslines inspired by UK bass and dubstep. The sound is characterized by heavy low-end frequencies, punchy drops, and energetic rhythms designed for festival main stages and club dancefloors. It bridges the gap between accessible house grooves and the intensity of bass music culture.
