## hardcore techno

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T11:16:35.703Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 13:16:35 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hardcore techno is a subgenre of electronic dance music (EDM) that originated in the Netherlands and Germany in the early 1990s. It is characterized by its fast and aggressive beats, usually between 160 and 200 beats per minute, and its use of distorted sounds and heavy bass. The music often features repetitive and heavily processed vocals, and may also incorporate elements of gabber, industrial, and breakbeat music. Hardcore techno is known for its high-energy and intense sound, and is popular in underground rave scenes and clubs around the world.
