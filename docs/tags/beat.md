### Description

&quot;Beat&quot; refers to the fundamental rhythmic pulse that drives a piece of music forward, serving as its temporal foundation and groove. This tag encompasses music with prominent, often danceable rhythms that emphasize percussion and bass elements, ranging from 1960s beat music to contemporary hip-hop and electronic productions. The beat creates physical momentum and emotional energy, making it essential to how listeners experience and move to music across all genres and cultures.
