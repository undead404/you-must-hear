## hyperpop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T03:54:31.783Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 05:54:31 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hyperpop is a music genre that emerged in the late 2010s and early 2020s. It is characterized by its heavy use of digital production techniques, including autotune, glitchy sound effects, and chopped and manipulated vocal samples. Hyperpop also draws on a wide range of influences from different genres, including pop, electronic dance music, hip hop, and punk. The genre is often associated with a DIY (do-it-yourself) aesthetic and a rebellious, boundary-pushing attitude. Some of the most notable artists associated with hyperpop include 100 gecs, SOPHIE, Charli XCX, and Dorian Electra.
