## tech trance

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:53:22.716Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:53:22 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Tech Trance is a subgenre of trance music that emerged in the mid-1990s, heavily influenced by techno. It is characterized by complex electronic rhythms, heavy use of synthesizers, rhythms, percussions, and basslines, rather than melodies. The music creates a futuristic, upbeat sound, often with an aggressive feel, emphasizing technical precision and rhythmic intricacy.
