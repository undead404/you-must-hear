## new jack swing

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T22:39:12.847Z">Wed Oct 29 2025 00:39:12 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

New jack swing is a fusion of R&amp;B, hip-hop, and dance-pop that emerged in the late 1980s and dominated urban radio through the mid-1990s. The genre features swingbeat rhythms, synthesizers, and rap verses combined with smooth vocal harmonies. It bridged street culture and mainstream pop, defining the sound of Black American youth music in that era.
