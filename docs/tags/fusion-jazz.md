## fusion jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T09:38:04.247Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 11:38:04 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Fusion jazz, also known as jazz fusion, is a genre that emerged in the late 1960s, combining elements of jazz with rock, funk, and rhythm and blues. It often features a mix of traditional jazz instruments like saxophones and trumpets, along with electric guitars, synthesizers, and other electronic instruments. The music is characterized by jazz harmony and improvisation, complex arrangements, and often incorporates groove-based vamps or unconventional time signatures.
