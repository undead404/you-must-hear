## duo

### Description

The &quot;duo&quot; tag identifies music created by two-person collaborations, whether lifelong partnerships or project-based pairings. These acts span every genre from pop and R&amp;B to folk and electronic, united by an intimate creative chemistry that shapes their distinctive sound. The format allows for tight musical interplay and balanced vocal harmonies that feel more personal than larger ensembles.
