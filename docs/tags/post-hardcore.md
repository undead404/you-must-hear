## post-hardcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-18T16:01:36.706Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 18:01:36 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Post-hardcore is a subgenre of punk rock and hardcore punk that emerged in the 1980s. It is characterized by its emphasis on complex song structures, dissonant guitar riffs, unconventional rhythms, and often emotional and introspective lyrics. Post-hardcore often incorporates elements of other genres such as post-punk, emo, and experimental rock. Some notable post-hardcore bands include Fugazi, At the Drive-In, Refused, Glassjaw, and Thrice.
