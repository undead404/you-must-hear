## maghreb

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T00:53:45.927Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 02:53:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Maghreb music refers to the traditional and popular music of the Maghreb region of North Africa, which includes Algeria, Morocco, and Tunisia. The music of this region is characterized by its strong rhythmic and melodic components, as well as its use of various instruments such as the oud, qanun, ney, and darbuka. Maghreb music has been influenced by a variety of cultures and styles, including Arab, Berber, Andalusian, and African, and it continues to evolve and innovate in the modern era. Some famous Maghreb musicians include Cheb Khaled, Nass El Ghiwane, and Warda Al-Jazairia.
