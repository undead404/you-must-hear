## death-thrash metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T05:04:47.724Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 07:04:47 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Death-thrash metal fuses the aggressive speed and rhythmic precision of thrash metal with death metal&#39;s guttural vocals and darker themes. Emerging in the late 1980s, it appeals to fans seeking extreme intensity while maintaining thrash&#39;s technical edge. The style emphasizes relentless energy and brutality.
