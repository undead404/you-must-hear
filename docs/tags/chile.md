## chile

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-19T09:18:26.070Z">Wed Nov 19 2025 11:18:26 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Chilean music blends folk traditions with rock, hip-hop, and indie influences, reflecting the country&#39;s complex political and social history. The genre ranges from protest songs addressing inequality to dreamy psychedelic rock and Latin alternative pop. It&#39;s characterized by poetic Spanish lyrics, diverse rhythms, and a spirit of creative experimentation.
