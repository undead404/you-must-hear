## g-funk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-10T07:16:13.665Z">Mon Nov 10 2025 09:16:13 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

G-funk is a subgenre of hip hop music that originated on the West Coast of the United States in the early 1990s. The &quot;G&quot; in G-funk stands for &quot;gangsta,&quot; and the music is characterized by its heavy basslines, slow tempos, and funk-inspired melodies. G-funk was popularized by artists such as Dr. Dre, Snoop Dogg, and Warren G, and is known for its use of synthesizers, talkbox, and samples from classic funk and soul songs. The style has had a lasting impact on hip hop and popular music, and is often associated with the West Coast hip hop scene.
