## instrumental rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T03:18:43.698Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 05:18:43 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Instrumental rock is a guitar-driven genre that emphasizes technical musicianship and melody without vocals. Emerging in the 1950s and flourishing through the 1980s-90s, it showcases virtuosic playing and compositional creativity. The genre blends rock energy with jazz and classical influences.
