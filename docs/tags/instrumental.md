## instrumental

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T04:27:46.292Z">Fri Nov 14 2025 06:27:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Instrumental music is music that is solely or primarily composed of musical instruments, without any vocals or lyrics. In other words, it&#39;s music that is performed using instruments rather than using the human voice as the primary instrument. It can encompass a wide variety of genres, including classical, jazz, electronic, rock, and more. Instrumental music can be used for various purposes, such as background music, meditation, relaxation, study, and performance. Some notable examples of instrumental music include classical symphonies, jazz improvisations, electronic ambient music, and rock guitar solos.
