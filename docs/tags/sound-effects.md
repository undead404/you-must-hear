## sound effects

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T07:07:27.749Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 09:07:27 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Sound effects encompass non-musical audio recordings that replicate real-world sounds, ambient noise, or synthesized audio elements used in media production, gaming, and artistic contexts. This tag includes everything from nature recordings and everyday sounds to comedic or explicit audio clips designed for entertainment or practical application. Rather than traditional music, these recordings serve functional purposes in storytelling, atmosphere creation, or comedic enhancement.
