## piano trio

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-22T17:05:27.391Z">Sat Nov 22 2025 19:05:27 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Piano trio music refers to a genre of music typically played by a piano, a violin, and a cello. The piano is the featured instrument, with the violin and cello providing melody and harmony. The piano trio is a popular classical music ensemble that has been used in a wide variety of musical styles, including classical, jazz, and pop music. The repertoire for piano trio music includes a broad range of works from the Baroque, Classical, Romantic, and contemporary eras. Some of the most famous composers of piano trio music include Beethoven, Mozart, Mendelssohn, Schubert, Brahms, and Shostakovich.
