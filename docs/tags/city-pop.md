## city pop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-31T23:29:51.401Z">Sat Nov 01 2025 01:29:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

City pop is a Japanese music genre from the late 1970s-80s that blends funk, disco, R&amp;B, and soft rock with polished production and urban themes. It captures the optimistic spirit of Japan&#39;s economic bubble era through sophisticated arrangements and breezy melodies. The genre has seen a global revival, influencing modern Asian pop music.
