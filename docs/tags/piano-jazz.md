## piano jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T02:40:46.153Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 04:40:46 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Piano jazz is a style centered on the piano as the lead instrument, emerging from early 20th-century American jazz traditions. It features sophisticated harmonies, syncopated rhythms, and improvisation that ranges from delicate and introspective to virtuosic and swing-driven. The genre showcases both solo performances and intimate trio settings with bass and drums.
