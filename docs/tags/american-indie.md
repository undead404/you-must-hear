## american indie

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:38:13.842Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:38:13 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

American indie encompasses the diverse spectrum of independently-minded rock and pop music emerging from the U.S. alternative scene, characterized by DIY ethics and creative freedom outside major label constraints. The sound ranges from jangly guitar pop to dreamy atmospherics and lo-fi experimentation, unified by an emphasis on authenticity and artistic vision over commercial appeal. It represents a cultural movement valuing grassroots communities and unconventional songwriting approaches.
