### Description

Reggae is a music genre that originated in Jamaica in the late 1960s. It is characterized by its syncopated rhythms, bass-heavy sound, and use of offbeat accents. Reggae music often features socially conscious lyrics that address issues such as poverty, oppression, and inequality. It has been influenced by various genres, including African and Caribbean music, American rhythm and blues, and jazz. Some of the most well-known reggae musicians include Bob Marley, Peter Tosh, and Jimmy Cliff.
