### Description

Swedish music refers to music originating from or produced in Sweden. Sweden has a long and rich history of music, ranging from traditional Nordic folk music to contemporary pop and electronic music. Swedish music has had a significant impact on popular music worldwide, with a number of Swedish artists and songwriters achieving international success. Some of the most popular genres of Swedish music include pop, rock, electronic, folk, and metal, among others. Sweden is also home to a number of well-known music festivals, including the Stockholm Jazz Festival, Way Out West, and the Sweden Rock Festival.
