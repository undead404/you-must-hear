## southern rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T03:13:45.921Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 05:13:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Southern rock is a subgenre of rock music that originated in the Southern United States in the late 1960s and early 1970s. It is known for its combination of elements from rock, blues, country, and soul music, as well as its use of electric guitars, drums, and keyboards. Southern rock bands often feature distinctive vocal harmonies, and lyrics that focus on the culture, history, and geography of the Southern United States. Some of the most famous Southern rock bands include Lynyrd Skynyrd, The Allman Brothers Band, and ZZ Top.
