## quebec

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:32:50.975Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:32:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Quebec music represents the vibrant French-language music scene from Canada&#39;s predominantly francophone province, blending traditional folk influences with modern indie rock, chanson, and alternative pop sensibilities. The tag encompasses artists who sing primarily in French while incorporating distinctly Québécois cultural identity, storytelling traditions, and a spirit of linguistic pride. The sound ranges from accordion-driven folk-rock to experimental indie, united by lyrical wit and a celebration of Quebec&#39;s unique cultural heritage.
