## zeuhl

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T21:33:41.663Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 23:33:41 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Zeuhl is an avant-garde fusion of progressive rock, jazz, and classical music characterized by militaristic rhythms, operatic vocals, and dystopian themes. Pioneered in 1970s France, it often features invented languages and creates an intense, ritualistic atmosphere through repetitive bass patterns and dramatic choral arrangements. The style evokes cosmic mythology and science fiction through its bombastic, otherworldly sound.
