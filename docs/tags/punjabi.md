## punjabi

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T17:09:53.048Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 19:09:53 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Punjabi music encompasses the vibrant sounds originating from Punjab, characterized by energetic dhol drums, tumbi strings, and passionate vocals sung in the Punjabi language. The style blends traditional South Asian folk elements with modern genres like bhangra, hip-hop, and electronic dance music, creating infectious rhythms perfect for celebration. It carries the spirited cultural identity of Punjab while evolving globally through diaspora communities.
