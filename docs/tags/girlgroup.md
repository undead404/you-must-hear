## girlgroup

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-18T10:15:12.460Z">Sat Oct 18 2025 13:15:12 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Girlgroup is a pop genre centered on all-female vocal ensembles performing synchronized choreography with polished production. Originating in both Western pop and K-pop scenes, it emphasizes catchy hooks, tight harmonies, and visually striking performances that blend singing with dance. The sound ranges from bubblegum pop to edgier electronic styles, unified by group dynamics and commercial appeal.
