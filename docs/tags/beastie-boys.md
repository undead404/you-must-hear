## beastie boys

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T22:19:53.907Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 00:19:53 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

This tag captures the irreverent, high-energy fusion of hip-hop, punk rock, and playful experimentation pioneered by its namesake group. The sound combines aggressive beats, distorted guitars, rapid-fire sampling, and humorous vocal delivery that balances party atmosphere with social commentary. It represents a rebellious, tongue-in-cheek approach to music that refuses to take itself too seriously while maintaining sharp technical skill.
