## neurofunk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:47:45.900Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:47:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Neurofunk is a dark, technically complex subgenre of drum and bass that emerged in the late 1990s, characterized by intricate bass modulations, dystopian atmospheres, and precise, aggressive sound design. The genre emphasizes futuristic, mechanical textures and heavily processed basslines that create an intense, cerebral listening experience. Born from the UK&#39;s electronic music scene, it represents drum and bass at its most experimental and sonically sophisticated.
