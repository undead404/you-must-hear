## deathgrind

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:40:10.380Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:40:10 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Deathgrind is an extreme metal fusion combining death metal&#39;s heavy brutality with grindcore&#39;s chaotic speed and intensity. Characterized by blast beats, guttural vocals, and brief explosive songs, it emerged in the late 1980s underground scene. The genre pushes sonic extremity to its limits through relentless aggression and technical precision.
