## deathrock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-01T11:48:51.462Z">Sat Nov 01 2025 13:48:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Deathrock emerged from late 1970s punk rock, blending horror aesthetics with dark, theatrical performances and macabre imagery. The sound features driving punk energy combined with eerie guitar effects, prominent bass lines, and dramatic vocals. It became a cornerstone of gothic subculture.
