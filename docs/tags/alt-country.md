## alt-country

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-12T18:27:52.038Z">Wed Nov 12 2025 20:27:52 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Alt-country, also known as alternative country or insurgent country, is a subgenre of country music that emerged in the 1990s as a reaction to the commercialization of mainstream country music. Alt-country artists combine elements of traditional country music with rock, folk, punk, and other genres, often with a darker, more introspective or subversive edge. The term &quot;alt-country&quot; has been applied to a wide range of artists and styles, from the raw, punk-influenced sound of Uncle Tupelo to the more polished and diverse sounds of Wilco and Ryan Adams. Other notable alt-country artists include Neko Case, Lucinda Williams, and Steve Earle.
