## 2ne1

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T06:19:02.123Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 08:19:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

This tag represents the bold, experimental K-pop sound pioneered by the groundbreaking girl group 2NE1, characterized by fierce electronic beats, hip-hop influences, and powerful vocals that challenged conventional feminine pop aesthetics. The music blends aggressive synths, bass-heavy production, and confident swagger with unexpectedly vulnerable moments. It embodies the &quot;girl crush&quot; concept that revolutionized K-pop in the late 2000s and early 2010s.
