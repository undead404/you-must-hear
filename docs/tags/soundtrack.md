### Description

Soundtrack music, also known as film score or background music, refers to the musical composition written specifically for movies, TV shows, video games, and other visual media. It is designed to accompany and enhance the visuals and mood of the story being told, and can range from orchestral pieces to electronic beats and everything in between. Soundtracks often incorporate a mix of original music and pre-existing songs to create a cohesive audio experience that complements the visual story being told.
