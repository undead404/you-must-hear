## irish folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:51:31.458Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:51:31 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Irish folk is a traditional music genre rooted in Ireland&#39;s rural heritage, featuring instruments like fiddles, tin whistles, and bodhráns. The songs often tell stories of historical events, emigration, love, and daily life with distinctive Celtic melodies. It ranges from lively pub sing-alongs to haunting ballads.
