## celtic

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-12T01:58:39.035Z">Wed Nov 12 2025 03:58:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Celtic music refers to a style of music originating from the traditional music of the Celtic nations, including Ireland, Scotland, Wales, Brittany (in France), Galicia (in Spain), and the Isle of Man. The style is characterized by the use of traditional instruments such as the fiddle, harp, bodhrán (Irish drum), pipes, and whistle, as well as the incorporation of Celtic languages and lyrical themes. Celtic music can range from lively dance tunes, such as jigs and reels, to mournful ballads, and has been influential in the development of various other genres, such as folk, bluegrass, and even rock music.
