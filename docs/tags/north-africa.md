## north africa

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T08:29:26.823Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 10:29:26 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

North Africa is a region in Africa that comprises a group of countries including Algeria, Egypt, Libya, Morocco, Sudan, Tunisia, and Western Sahara. The music of North Africa is diverse and reflects the cultural, ethnic, and linguistic diversity of the region. Traditional music of North Africa is characterized by the use of instruments such as the oud, ney, darbuka, and qanun, and often includes vocals, call-and-response singing, and complex rhythms. In modern times, North African music has also incorporated elements of Western music, including rock, pop, and jazz, resulting in a fusion of styles that are popular in the region and beyond.
