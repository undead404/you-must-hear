## brazilian

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T20:42:09.167Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 22:42:09 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Brazilian music is a diverse and vibrant musical genre that incorporates a wide range of musical styles and traditions from Brazil&#39;s different regions, including samba, bossa nova, choro, forró, frevo, maracatu, and many others. It has been influenced by African, European, and indigenous music and rhythms, and has had a significant impact on global music, particularly in jazz and popular music. Brazilian music has also played a vital role in shaping Brazilian identity and culture, with many of its styles and rhythms representing specific regions or social and cultural groups within Brazil. Some of the most well-known Brazilian musicians and composers include Tom Jobim, João Gilberto, Caetano Veloso, Gilberto Gil, and Elis Regina.
