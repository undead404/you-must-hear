## czech

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T07:07:32.724Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 09:07:32 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Czech music encompasses the rich classical tradition of Bohemia and Moravia, characterized by folk-inspired melodies, nationalistic themes, and distinctive Slavic harmonies. The genre spans from romantic orchestral works to contemporary indie and electronic music, all reflecting Czech cultural identity. It often features lyrical storytelling and draws from traditional dance forms like the polka.
