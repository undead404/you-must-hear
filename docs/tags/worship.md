## worship

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:59:12.253Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:59:12 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Worship is a genre of contemporary Christian music designed for congregational singing in religious services. It typically features accessible melodies, repetitive lyrical phrases focused on praise and devotion, and instrumentation ranging from acoustic to full rock band arrangements. The genre prioritizes emotional connection and spiritual engagement over musical complexity.
