## ween

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T02:52:08.545Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 04:52:08 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Ween represents an irreverent, genre-bending approach to rock music characterized by absurdist humor, deliberate lo-fi aesthetics, and eclectic pastiche of various styles from prog to country. The sound embraces intentional amateurism and satirical takes on mainstream music conventions while maintaining genuine musicianship beneath the quirky surface. This tag captures music that refuses to take itself seriously, blending experimental impulses with accessible melodies and tongue-in-cheek lyrics.
