## dark electronic

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-09T05:21:16.249Z">Tue Dec 09 2025 07:21:16 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dark electronic music is a genre of electronic music that typically features dark or ominous tones and themes. It often incorporates elements of industrial, techno, and other electronic genres, as well as elements of darker styles such as gothic or horror. This type of music often makes use of electronic instruments such as synthesizers, drum machines, and samplers to create complex soundscapes and driving rhythms. Some examples of dark electronic music artists include Nine Inch Nails, Skinny Puppy, and Front Line Assembly.
