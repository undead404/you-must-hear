## cantautor

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-06T06:34:30.175Z">Sat Dec 06 2025 08:34:30 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Cantautor refers to Spanish and Latin American singer-songwriters who craft poetic, introspective songs with literary depth and social consciousness. Rooted in folk traditions but often incorporating broader influences, these artists prioritize meaningful lyrics that explore love, politics, and philosophical themes. The term emphasizes the unity of composer and performer, celebrating authenticity and intimate storytelling over commercial production.
