## jazz hop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-03T20:31:18.791Z">Mon Nov 03 2025 22:31:18 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Jazz hop is a fusion genre blending hip-hop beats with live jazz instrumentation, including horns, keyboards, and upright bass. Emerging in the 1990s, it bridges the gap between jazz&#39;s improvisational heritage and hip-hop&#39;s rhythmic foundation. The style emphasizes musical sophistication while maintaining the groove and energy of contemporary urban music.
