## accordion

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-28T09:49:17.381Z">Tue Oct 28 2025 11:49:17 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Music featuring the accordion as a prominent or defining instrument, instantly recognizable by its bellows-driven sound that can range from melancholic and nostalgic to upbeat and festive. The accordion appears across diverse cultural contexts, from French café music and Latin American styles to folk traditions and experimental compositions. Its distinctive reedy timbre and ability to sustain notes while providing both melody and harmony make it versatile across genres from intimate solo pieces to energetic ensemble performances.
