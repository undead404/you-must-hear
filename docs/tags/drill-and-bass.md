## drill and bass

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-11T13:28:51.351Z">Tue Nov 11 2025 15:28:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Drill and bass is characterized by hyperkinetic, fragmented breakbeats pushed to extreme tempos, often exceeding 160 BPM, combined with aggressive bass lines and chaotic rhythmic structures. Emerging from 1990s experimental electronic music, it takes jungle and drum and bass to their most abrasive, avant-garde extremes. The sound is deliberately disorienting and intense, favoring precision-programmed complexity over dancefloor accessibility.
