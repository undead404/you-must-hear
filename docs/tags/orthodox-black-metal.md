## orthodox black metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T00:23:22.800Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 03:23:22 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Orthodox black metal refers to a dark, atmospheric style that adheres strictly to the raw, anti-religious aesthetics and lo-fi production values established by the genre&#39;s second wave in the early 1990s. The music emphasizes tremolo-picked guitars, blast beats, shrieked vocals, and an uncompromising devotion to occult or explicitly anti-Christian themes. It rejects commercialization and mainstream influences, maintaining black metal&#39;s original transgressive spirit.
