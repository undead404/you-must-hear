## garage house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T11:16:29.618Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 13:16:29 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Garage house is a subgenre of house music that originated in the early 1990s in New York City, particularly in the neighborhood of Paradise Garage. It is characterized by its soulful and gospel-infused sound, and is often associated with the use of samples from classic disco and R&amp;B tracks. The name &quot;garage&quot; comes from the legendary Paradise Garage club, where DJs such as Larry Levan and Frankie Knuckles played a key role in shaping the genre. Garage house music has had a significant influence on the development of other subgenres of house music, as well as on the broader electronic dance music scene.
