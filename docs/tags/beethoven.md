## beethoven

### Description

The &quot;beethoven&quot; tag encompasses works by and inspired by the revolutionary German composer who bridged Classical and Romantic eras with unprecedented emotional depth and structural innovation. His compositions range from delicate piano sonatas to thunderous symphonies, characterized by dramatic contrasts, heroic themes, and an unwavering pursuit of musical perfection. This tag represents not just a composer but a transformative force in Western classical music whose influence resonates across centuries.
