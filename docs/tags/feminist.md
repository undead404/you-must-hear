## feminist

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-09T05:51:38.506Z">Tue Dec 09 2025 07:51:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Feminist music encompasses works that champion gender equality, challenge patriarchal norms, and amplify women&#39;s voices across diverse genres from riot grrrl punk to hip-hop to experimental pop. These artists address themes of empowerment, bodily autonomy, and social justice through bold, unapologetic lyrics and often confrontational sonic approaches. The tag represents an ideological stance rather than a specific sound, united by its commitment to challenging gender-based oppression through music.
