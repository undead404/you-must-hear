### Description

Français is a broad category encompassing popular music sung in the French language, spanning from traditional chanson to modern pop, rock, and indie styles. Rooted in France&#39;s rich storytelling tradition, it emphasizes lyrical poetry, melodic sophistication, and emotional expressiveness. The genre reflects French cultural identity while embracing diverse contemporary influences from folk to electronic music.
