### Description

Colombian pop blends infectious reggaeton and Latin rhythms with urban and tropical influences, creating vibrant, danceable music that has achieved global mainstream success. The genre reflects Colombia&#39;s rich musical heritage while embracing modern production and bilingual lyrics. It&#39;s characterized by catchy melodies, romantic themes, and a distinctive blend of Caribbean and urban sounds.
