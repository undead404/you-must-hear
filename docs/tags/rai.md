## rai

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T03:19:02.243Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 05:19:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rai music is a popular genre of music that originated in the western region of Algeria in the 1920s. The word &quot;rai&quot; means &quot;opinion&quot; or &quot;viewpoint&quot; in Arabic, and the music often deals with social issues and political commentary. Rai music combines traditional Algerian music with Western popular music styles such as jazz, rock, and reggae, and often features electronic instruments and a prominent beat. The style has evolved over the years, and today&#39;s rai music includes a mix of traditional and modern sounds, often with lyrics sung in Arabic, French, or Berber. Rai music has gained popularity throughout North Africa and the Middle East, and has also had some success in Europe and other parts of the world.
