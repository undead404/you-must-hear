### Description

Australian music blends folk, indie rock, and pop sensibilities with laid-back coastal vibes reflecting the country&#39;s beach culture and expansive landscapes. The sound often features acoustic guitars, warm vocals, and introspective lyrics that balance earnestness with casual storytelling. This genre captures Australia&#39;s easygoing spirit while maintaining contemporary production and melodic accessibility.
