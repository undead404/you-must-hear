## minimal wave

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T06:52:14.888Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 08:52:14 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Minimal wave is a stark, lo-fi electronic genre that emerged from the late 1970s DIY punk scene, characterized by stripped-down synthesizers, drum machines, and detached vocals. The sound emphasizes cold, minimalist atmospheres with primitive production quality that creates an intimate yet alienated aesthetic. It bridges post-punk&#39;s raw energy with early synth-pop&#39;s electronic experimentation.
