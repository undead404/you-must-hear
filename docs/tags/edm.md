## edm

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-30T09:52:39.326Z">Thu Oct 30 2025 11:52:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

EDM stands for Electronic Dance Music, a genre of electronic music that is primarily created for use in nightclubs, raves, and festivals. EDM originated in the late 1980s and early 1990s and has since developed into a wide variety of sub-genres, including house, techno, trance, dubstep, drum and bass, and more. EDM is characterized by its use of electronic instruments and digital production techniques, and is typically created using software such as synthesizers, drum machines, and digital audio workstations. The music is often energetic, with strong beats and pulsating rhythms designed to keep listeners dancing for extended periods of time.
