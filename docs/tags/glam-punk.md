## glam punk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-26T02:46:25.608Z">Wed Nov 26 2025 04:46:25 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Glam punk fuses the raw energy and rebellious spirit of punk rock with the theatrical flamboyance and androgynous aesthetics of glam rock. Born from the same gritty urban scenes that spawned both movements, it emphasizes outrageous fashion, sexual provocation, and unapologetic excess alongside aggressive guitars and sneering vocals. The style celebrates trashy glamour and deliberate bad taste as forms of anti-establishment expression.
