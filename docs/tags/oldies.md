## oldies

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-19T11:00:20.497Z">Wed Nov 19 2025 13:00:20 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Oldies music typically refers to popular music from the 1950s, 1960s, and 1970s, particularly in the genres of rock and roll, doo-wop, soul, and pop. The term &quot;oldies&quot; can vary depending on the context and the person using it, but it generally refers to music that was popular during a particular era, and has a nostalgic or classic feel to it. Some examples of oldies artists include Elvis Presley, The Beatles, The Supremes, The Beach Boys, and many others.
