## ragtime

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T02:10:03.497Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 05:10:03 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Ragtime is a syncopated, piano-driven style that emerged from African American communities in the late 1890s, characterized by its &quot;ragged&quot; rhythmic patterns that place melodic accents against a steady march-like bass. The music creates a jaunty, optimistic feeling through its distinctive bounce and mathematical precision, evoking turn-of-the-century dance halls and parlors. Though rooted in a specific historical era, ragtime&#39;s playful syncopation has influenced countless later genres and continues to be revived by contemporary artists.
