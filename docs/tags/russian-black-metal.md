## russian black metal

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-08T08:16:10.640Z">Mon Dec 08 2025 10:16:10 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Russian black metal is a subgenre of black metal music that originated in Russia. It is characterized by its aggressive and raw sound, which often incorporates elements of traditional Russian music and folklore. Lyrically, Russian black metal bands often focus on themes of Slavic mythology, paganism, and anti-Christianity. Some notable Russian black metal bands include Pagan Reign, Forest, and Branikald.
