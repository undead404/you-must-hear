## dark folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:30:39.068Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:30:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dark folk is a somber acoustic genre blending traditional folk instrumentation with haunting atmospheres and melancholic themes. It often draws from pagan, medieval, or nature-inspired imagery, creating an ethereal yet grounded sound. The music emphasizes raw emotion and introspection through minimal arrangements.
