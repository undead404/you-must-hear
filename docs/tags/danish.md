### Description

Danish pop blends melodic Scandinavian sensibilities with international influences, ranging from bubblegum dance to alternative rock. The genre reflects Denmark&#39;s compact music scene where artists often cross between pop accessibility and experimental edge. Danish acts typically sing in English to reach global audiences while maintaining Nordic production aesthetics.
