## grime

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-12T02:00:42.238Z">Wed Nov 12 2025 04:00:42 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Grime is a genre of electronic music that originated in London in the early 2000s. It combines elements of UK garage, dancehall, and hip-hop, and is characterized by its fast-paced, aggressive beats and lyrical content that often addresses social and political issues. The music typically features sparse, minimalist production with heavy use of bass and percussion, and is often accompanied by rapid-fire vocal delivery known as &quot;MCing&quot; or &quot;spitting&quot;. Grime has gained a large following in the UK and has been influential in the development of other genres such as UK drill and trap.
