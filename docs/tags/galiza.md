## galiza

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T13:52:02.859Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 15:52:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Galiza (also known as Galicia) is a region located in the northwest corner of Spain. Its music is diverse and encompasses various traditional forms, including bagpipes, harps, and fiddles, among others. Galician music is heavily influenced by Celtic music, with many musicians using traditional instruments like the gaita (Galician bagpipe) to play jigs, reels, and other dance tunes. Modern Galician music has also incorporated elements of rock, pop, and other contemporary styles.
