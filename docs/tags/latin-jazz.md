## latin jazz

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-03T01:11:51.104Z">Mon Nov 03 2025 03:11:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Latin jazz blends jazz improvisation with rhythms from Cuba, Brazil, and other Latin American countries. The genre features complex percussion patterns like clave and samba alongside traditional jazz instruments. It emerged in the 1940s when Caribbean and South American musical traditions merged with American jazz.
