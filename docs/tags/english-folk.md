## english folk

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T21:33:09.815Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 23:33:09 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

English folk music is a traditional form of music that originated in England and has been passed down through generations. It includes songs, ballads, dance tunes, and instrumental music that reflect the cultural heritage of the people of England. English folk music is characterized by its storytelling lyrics, use of acoustic instruments such as the fiddle, guitar, and accordion, and its strong connection to community and social events. The style has influenced many genres of music including rock, pop, and country, and has a rich history dating back to the Middle Ages. Popular English folk musicians include Martin Carthy, Shirley Collins, and Fairport Convention.
