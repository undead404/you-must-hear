## desi

### Description

Desi music refers to music genres that originate from the Indian subcontinent, which includes India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, and Sri Lanka. It is a fusion of traditional music with contemporary elements such as electronic beats, pop, rock, hip-hop, and R&amp;B. Desi music encompasses a wide range of styles, including bhangra, qawwali, ghazal, filmi, sufi, and fusion. Bhangra, a lively and upbeat Punjabi music genre, is one of the most popular forms of desi music and is known for its infectious rhythms and catchy beats. Qawwali is a form of Islamic devotional music that has its roots in the subcontinent, and is characterized by powerful vocals and percussion. Ghazal is a poetic form of music that is typically sung in Urdu, and is known for its romantic and melancholic lyrics. Film music, or filmi, is the popular music that is often used in Indian movies, and has a wide range of styles, from classical to pop. Sufi music, with its roots in Islamic mysticism, is characterized by its spiritual themes and repetitive rhythms. Desi fusion music combines traditional elements with contemporary styles to create a unique sound that appeals to a modern audience.
