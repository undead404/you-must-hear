## blues rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T03:57:35.106Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 05:57:35 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Blues rock is a genre of rock music that emerged in the 1960s and is characterized by a heavy focus on the blues style of music, which originated in African American communities in the southern United States in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. Blues rock typically features heavy guitar riffs, powerful vocals, and a strong rhythm section. It also often includes elements of other genres such as rock, soul, and jazz. Some of the most notable blues rock musicians include Eric Clapton, Jimi Hendrix, Stevie Ray Vaughan, and the band Led Zeppelin.
