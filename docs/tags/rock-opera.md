## rock opera

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-23T11:30:45.784Z">Sun Nov 23 2025 13:30:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Rock opera music is a subgenre of rock music that combines elements of classical opera with rock music. It typically tells a story through music and lyrics, often with a theatrical and grandiose style. Rock operas are usually performed by rock bands with vocalists trained in classical singing techniques, and often include a large ensemble cast of singers and musicians. Some of the most well-known examples of rock opera include The Who&#39;s &quot;Tommy,&quot; Andrew Lloyd Webber&#39;s &quot;Jesus Christ Superstar,&quot; and Pink Floyd&#39;s &quot;The Wall.&quot;
