## jam

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T03:39:12.332Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 05:39:12 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Jam is a rock-oriented genre emphasizing extended improvisational passages and live performance over studio recordings. Born from 1960s psychedelic rock and the Grateful Dead&#39;s legacy, it thrives on spontaneous musical exploration and audience interaction. The sound blends elements of rock, jazz, funk, and blues into lengthy, evolving compositions.
