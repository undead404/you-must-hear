## oud

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T06:20:28.599Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 09:20:28 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

The oud tag features the distinctive sound of the Middle Eastern lute, a pear-shaped stringed instrument central to Arabic, Turkish, and Persian musical traditions. Its warm, resonant tones and fretless neck allow for microtonal melodies and expressive ornamentation that evoke ancient cultural heritage. Music tagged with oud ranges from traditional solo performances to contemporary fusion blending jazz, classical, and world music elements.
