## latin

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T06:24:57.336Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 08:24:57 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Latin music is a genre that encompasses a wide range of styles and rhythms that have originated from various Latin American and Caribbean countries, including but not limited to, salsa, cumbia, merengue, bachata, reggaeton, bossa nova, tango, and flamenco. Latin music is characterized by its lively and upbeat rhythms, strong percussion, and complex melodies and harmonies. It has gained immense popularity globally and has influenced many other genres of music, including pop, rock, and hip hop.
