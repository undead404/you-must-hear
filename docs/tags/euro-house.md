## euro house

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T23:37:35.483Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 01:37:35 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Euro house emerged in the late 1980s as a polished, commercially accessible style of electronic dance music that blended infectious four-on-the-floor beats with catchy vocal hooks and bright synthesizer melodies. Originating primarily in countries like Italy, the Netherlands, and Belgium, it emphasized radio-friendly production and pop sensibilities over underground rave culture. The sound is characterized by uplifting piano riffs, anthemic choruses, and an energetic tempo designed for both clubs and mainstream charts.
