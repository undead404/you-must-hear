## chillout downtempo

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-18T12:07:48.381Z">Tue Nov 18 2025 14:07:48 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Chillout downtempo is a relaxed electronic music style characterized by slow tempos, smooth beats, and ambient atmospheres. It emerged from 1990s club culture as a softer alternative to dance music, blending jazz, soul, and electronic elements. The genre creates a mellow, sophisticated mood ideal for lounging and unwinding.
