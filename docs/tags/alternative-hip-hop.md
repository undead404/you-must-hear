## alternative hip hop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T05:53:23.161Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 07:53:23 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Alternative hip hop is a subgenre of hip hop music that emerged in the 1980s and 1990s and is characterized by its experimental and eclectic approach to the genre. It often incorporates elements from other genres, such as jazz, rock, and electronic music, and focuses on lyrics that are more socially conscious and introspective than mainstream hip hop. Alternative hip hop artists often use unconventional production techniques and instruments, and their music is usually less focused on commercial success and more on artistic expression. Some notable alternative hip hop artists include A Tribe Called Quest, De La Soul, The Roots, Outkast, and Kendrick Lamar.
