## brostep

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-14T22:51:43.368Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 00:51:43 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Brostep is an aggressive subgenre of dubstep that emerged in the late 2000s, characterized by harsh, metallic bass drops and high-energy synths. It gained mainstream popularity in North America, attracting a younger audience beyond electronic music&#39;s traditional fanbase. The style emphasizes raw power and intensity over dubstep&#39;s original minimalist groove.
