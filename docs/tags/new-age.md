## new age

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-15T10:40:39.403Z">Sat Nov 15 2025 12:40:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

New age music is a genre of music that emerged in the 1970s and is characterized by its relaxing and meditative qualities. It often features instrumental pieces that combine acoustic and electronic elements, with an emphasis on creating a calming and peaceful atmosphere. New age music has been used for a variety of purposes, including meditation, relaxation, yoga, and as background music for spa treatments and other wellness activities. Some of the most popular new age artists include Enya, Yanni, and Kitaro.
