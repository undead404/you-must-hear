### Description

The Philadelphia tag represents the city&#39;s rich musical legacy spanning soul, R&amp;B, hip-hop, and indie rock. It encompasses the lush orchestral arrangements of the &quot;Philly Sound&quot; from the 1970s alongside gritty, socially conscious East Coast hip-hop that emerged decades later. This tag captures Philadelphia&#39;s distinct cultural identity where smooth sophistication meets raw urban authenticity.
