## mincecore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T08:11:45.659Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 10:11:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Mincecore is an extreme subgenre of grindcore characterized by ultra-short songs, intentionally raw production, and politically charged lyrics focused on anarchism and social justice. The music features blasting drums, distorted bass-heavy guitars, and harsh vocals delivered at breakneck speed, often with a deliberately lo-fi aesthetic. Emerging from the DIY punk underground in the 1990s, it emphasizes accessibility and anti-commercial ethos over technical precision.
