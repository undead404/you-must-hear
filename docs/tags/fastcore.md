## fastcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-04T03:16:51.008Z">Tue Nov 04 2025 05:16:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Fastcore, also known as thrashcore, is a high-speed subgenre of hardcore punk that emerged in the late 1980s and early 1990s. It is characterized by its relentless speed, often featuring bursts of extreme tempo changes that can reach up to 1,000 beats per minute (BPM), short song lengths, typically under a minute, and chaotic arrangements that showcase the technical prowess of the musicians. The vocal style in Fastcore ranges from high-pitched shrieks to guttural growls, often accompanied by politically charged lyrics that address social issues and personal experiences.
