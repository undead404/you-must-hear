## rhythmic noise

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-10-22T03:52:59.913Z">Wed Oct 22 2025 06:52:59 GMT+0300 (Eastern European Summer Time)</time>

### Description

Rhythmic noise fuses harsh industrial textures with driving, often aggressive beats, creating an intense sonic assault that maintains dancefloor functionality. Emerging from 1990s power electronics and industrial scenes, it channels raw mechanical energy through distorted percussion and abrasive sound design. The style appeals to those seeking confrontational electronic music that balances chaos with hypnotic, club-ready rhythms.
