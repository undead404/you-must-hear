## hard dance

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T15:45:19.059Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 17:45:19 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Hard dance music is a genre of electronic dance music (EDM) that emerged in the late 1990s and early 2000s. It is characterized by fast, hard-hitting beats, with an emphasis on hardstyle, hard trance, and hardcore techno. Hard dance music has a high tempo and uses distorted, heavy basslines and synthesizer sounds. It often features a combination of repetitive and melodic elements, with the aim of creating a highly energetic and euphoric sound. Hard dance music is popular in dance clubs, music festivals, and raves, and has a dedicated following among fans of EDM.
