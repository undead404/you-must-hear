### Description

Hindi music encompasses songs performed in the Hindi language, predominantly originating from India&#39;s vibrant Bollywood film industry and independent music scene. The sound blends traditional Indian classical and folk elements with contemporary pop, rock, and electronic production, featuring characteristic melodic vocals and rhythmic tabla alongside modern instrumentation. This tag represents one of the world&#39;s most prolific music traditions, merging ancient ragas with global influences to create emotionally expressive soundscapes.
