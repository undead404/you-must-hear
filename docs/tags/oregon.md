## oregon

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T07:20:30.017Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 09:20:30 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Oregon’s music scene is diverse, reflecting the array of styles present in American music, from Native American to contemporary genres like rock, country, rhythm and blues, jazz, pop, electronic music, and hip hop. The state’s contributions to American popular music began in the 1960s, with bands like The Kingsmen and Paul Revere &amp; the Raiders, and has evolved into a strong indie music scene, particularly in Portland. Notable indie musicians from Oregon include The Decemberists, Gossip, The Dandy Warhols, and M. Ward.
