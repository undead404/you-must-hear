### Description

&quot;European&quot; is a broad classification for music originating from or heavily influenced by diverse European cultural traditions and contemporary scenes. The genre encompasses everything from electronic experimentation and ambient soundscapes to indie pop with distinctly regional aesthetics and production styles. It reflects the continent&#39;s artistic diversity while often sharing common threads of innovation and cross-cultural exchange.
