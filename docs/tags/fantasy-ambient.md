## fantasy ambient

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T04:36:02.153Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 06:36:02 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Fantasy ambient music is a genre of music that combines elements of fantasy or science fiction with ambient music. It often features lush soundscapes, atmospheric textures, and otherworldly sounds that transport the listener to imaginary realms or far-off worlds. Fantasy ambient music can be used as background music for tabletop gaming, reading, or other activities that require a sense of immersion and escapism. Some examples of fantasy ambient music artists include Brian Eno, Tangerine Dream, Steve Roach, and Klaus Schulze.
