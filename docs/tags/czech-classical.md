## czech classical

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T10:48:38.013Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 12:48:38 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Czech classical music refers to classical music composed by Czech composers or performed by Czech musicians. The Czech Republic has a rich classical music tradition, with many famous composers such as Antonín Dvořák, Bedřich Smetana, and Leoš Janáček, among others. Czech classical music is known for its use of folk melodies and rhythms, as well as its emotional and expressive character. The Czech Philharmonic Orchestra, based in Prague, is one of the most famous classical music ensembles in the world.
