## justice

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T12:47:01.665Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 14:47:01 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

The &quot;justice&quot; tag captures music with themes of retribution, righteousness, or moral reckoning, often featuring aggressive energy and confrontational lyrics that address societal wrongs or personal vindication. Sonically, it spans from hard-hitting electronic and metal to hip-hop with defiant attitudes, unified by an underlying sense of standing up against injustice or demanding accountability. The music conveys empowerment through its raw intensity and unapologetic delivery.
