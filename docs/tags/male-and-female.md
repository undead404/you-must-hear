## male and female

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T07:16:29.762Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 09:16:29 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

This tag identifies tracks featuring both male and female vocalists performing together, often trading verses or harmonizing. It emerged prominently in 1990s Eurodance and electronic pop, where the interplay between deeper male rap sections and soaring female melodic hooks became a defining formula. The dynamic contrast between masculine and feminine vocal timbres creates an energetic, crowd-pleasing sound that remains popular in dance music worldwide.
