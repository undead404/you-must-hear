## industrial hardcore

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T10:32:39.648Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 12:32:39 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Industrial hardcore is a subgenre of electronic dance music that combines elements of industrial and hardcore techno. It is characterized by aggressive, distorted beats, dark and dystopian themes, and heavy use of sampling, often taken from industrial and noise music. The genre originated in the Netherlands in the late 1990s and has since spread to other parts of Europe. Some notable industrial hardcore artists include Promo, D-Passion, Ophidian, and Nosferatu.
