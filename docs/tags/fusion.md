## fusion

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T21:56:51.995Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 23:56:51 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Fusion blends jazz improvisation with elements from rock, funk, world music, and electronic styles, creating adventurous cross-cultural sounds. Emerging in the late 1960s, it breaks down barriers between musical traditions through complex rhythms and virtuosic playing. The genre celebrates experimentation, often combining Western harmonies with non-Western instruments and scales.
