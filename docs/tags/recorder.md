## recorder

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-04T13:09:21.142Z">Thu Dec 04 2025 15:09:21 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Recorder music refers to music played on the recorder, a woodwind instrument that is part of the flute family. The recorder has been used as a solo instrument and as part of chamber music ensembles since the Middle Ages. It has also been used in various genres of music, including classical, folk, and popular music. The instrument&#39;s distinctive sound has made it a popular choice for early music ensembles and for teaching music to children. Some well-known composers who have written music for the recorder include Johann Sebastian Bach, Georg Philipp Telemann, and Antonio Vivaldi.
