## dixieland

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-07T01:44:36.816Z">Sun Dec 07 2025 03:44:36 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Dixieland music is a style of jazz that originated in New Orleans, Louisiana in the early 20th century. It is characterized by the use of brass instruments, particularly the trumpet, trombone, and clarinet, as well as a rhythm section that typically includes a banjo, drums, and double bass. Dixieland is known for its lively and energetic sound, with musicians often improvising in a collective manner over a simple, swinging beat. The genre has had a lasting influence on jazz and other forms of popular music, and remains popular with jazz enthusiasts around the world.
