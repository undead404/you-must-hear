## chillwave

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-03T07:07:49.430Z">Wed Dec 03 2025 09:07:49 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Chillwave is a music genre that emerged in the late 2000s and is characterized by its dreamy, nostalgic, and atmospheric sound. It typically features lo-fi production, a slow to mid-tempo beat, heavily reverberated vocals, and heavy use of electronic instruments such as synthesizers and samplers. Chillwave is often associated with a relaxed, beachy vibe and has been described as a fusion of various genres including dream pop, shoegaze, and electronic music. Some notable artists associated with the genre include Washed Out, Toro y Moi, and Neon Indian.
