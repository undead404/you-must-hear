## goth rock

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-11-01T03:06:50.959Z">Sat Nov 01 2025 05:06:50 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

Goth rock emerged from post-punk in the late 1970s, blending dark, atmospheric guitars with introspective lyrics exploring themes of melancholy, romance, and existential darkness. The genre features reverb-heavy production, driving basslines, and dramatic vocals that range from baritone crooning to ethereal wails. Its aesthetic embraces Victorian and romantic imagery, creating a moody sonic landscape that influenced alternative music for decades.
