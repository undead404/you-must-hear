## uk bhangra

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T01:05:45.964Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 03:05:45 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

UK bhangra music is a genre of music that originated in the Punjabi community in the United Kingdom. It is a fusion of traditional Punjabi music and modern western musical styles such as hip-hop, R&amp;B, and electronic dance music. The genre developed in the 1980s and 1990s and gained popularity in the UK among the South Asian diaspora. UK bhangra music often features upbeat rhythms, heavy percussion, and traditional Punjabi instruments such as the dhol, tumbi, and tabla, as well as modern synthesizers and drum machines. The lyrics are usually sung in Punjabi and often celebrate Punjabi culture, history, and values.
