## german hip hop

Last updated: <time datetime="2025-12-05T09:49:05.544Z">Fri Dec 05 2025 11:49:05 GMT+0200 (Eastern European Standard Time)</time>

### Description

German hip hop is rap music performed in the German language, emerging in the 1990s as artists adapted American hip hop culture to express local urban experiences. The genre often features clever wordplay and humor, blending German linguistic traditions with boom-bap beats and electronic elements. It ranges from playful, party-oriented tracks to socially conscious commentary on German society.
